---

You are lost in the haze
of memories that linger
like the scent of old books
in a forgotten library
your footsteps echo
with the ghostly whispers
of strangers who once knew
this place as home

Every step I take
feels like a betrayal
a surrender to the past
where memories are the only
thing that's left
of the present's sharp edge

The city's roar
is a distant hum
a reminder I'm a stranger
in a world I once knew
the buildings loom
like sentinels
 guarding secrets I've long
forgotten to uncover

In this place, time
is a puzzle
with missing pieces
only memories
hold the key
to unlocking the past

Your name is a thread
that weaves through
my memories like a
torn seam
unraveling the fabric
of my present
in search of a way
to break free

But what if
I'm the one who's lost
returning to a place
that's no longer mine
a stranger's home
in a world I've left
behind?

---

This poem uses free-verse style to explore the feeling of returning to a place that no longer feels familiar. It captures the nostalgia and longing that can come with leaving a place behind and trying to find one's way back. The poem delves into the memories and emotions associated with the place, and how they can be both a source of comfort and a source of conflict.

The poem also touches on the theme of identity and self-discovery, as the speaker grapples with their sense of self and how it has changed over time. The line "your name is a thread / that weaves through / my memories like a / torn seam / unraveling the fabric / of my present" suggests that the speaker's sense of self is being constantly unraveled, as memories and past experiences intersect and collide.

Overall, the poem invites the reader to reflect on their own experiences and emotions, and to consider how their sense of self and identity are shaped by their memories and past experiences.
