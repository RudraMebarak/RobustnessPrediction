MIKE: (smirking) Oh yeah? That's true, but sometimes you can make something amazing at home that you wouldn't be able to order at a restaurant.

JEN: (smiling) Maybe I will try it sometime. (pauses) Hey, do you want to come with me to Indian food tonight?
