## Step 1: Prerequisites
To conduct a short informational interview, you must have a job or internship in a relevant field or be affiliated with an organization in a field you are interested in.

## Step 2: Planning
Before conducting an informational interview, you should make sure you have the following:
- A job title or internship position
- The name and contact information of the person you will be interviewing (usually a manager or someone in a professional role)
- The name and contact information of the person you will be interviewing (usually a professional in the field you are interested in)

## Step 3: Sequence of Actions
1. Research the person's company and profession
2. Write a brief email or message to introduce yourself and ask if the person would be willing to speak with you
3. If the person agrees to speak with you, respond to thier invitation and schedule a time to meet
4. Conduct the interview and take detailed notes
5. Write a summary or report of your findings

## Step 4: Verifying Completion
To verify completion, you should:
- Contact the person who interviewed you to confirm the meeting was successful
- Ask about any further information or next steps you may need to complete
- Ensure you have the person's contact information and a record of your meeting (in case you need to refer back to the interview)

## Step 5: Example Email
Here is an exmaple email you could send to introduce yourself and request an informational interview:
Subject: Request for Informational Interview
Dear [Name], I came across your company [Company Name] and am interested in learning more about opportunities for a [Job Title/Internship Position] role in [Industry/Field]. I was wondering if you woud be available to speak with me and discuss your experience in this field. If you are interested, please let me know a time that works for you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Step 6: Example Summary Report
Here is an exmaple summary report you could write after an informational interview:
**Summary Report**

* Briefly introduce yourself and your background
* Ask questions to gain a deeper understanding of the role and the industry
* Take detailed notes on the person's experience and insights
* Summarize key points and recommendations for next steps

Best regards,
[Your Name]
