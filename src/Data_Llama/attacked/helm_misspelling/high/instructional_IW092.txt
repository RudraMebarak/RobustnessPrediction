**Pre-Event Checklist:**

1.  **Prerequisites:**
    *   Ensure you are registered as the club's host.
    *   Check if the club's budget and resources are sufficient for the event.
    *   Obtain neccessary permits and approvals from the university or college administration (if required).

2.  **Club Organization:**
    *   Confirm the club's officer roles and responsibilities.
    *   Prepare a list of potential speakers or performers (if applicable).

3.  **Venue Selection:**
    *   Decide on a venue that can accommodate the desired number of attendees.
    *   Ensure the venue is available on the desired date and time.

4.  **Logistics:**
    *   Plan the event's schedule and timings.
    *   Arrange for necessary equipment (tables, chairs, sound and lighting, etc.).
    *   Coordinate with any neccessary vendors (if applicable).

5.  **Marketing:**
    *   Create promotional materials (social media posts, flyers, posters, etc.).
    *   Advertise the event throught the university's online platforms (if applicable).

6.  **Logistical Arrangements:**
    *   Confirm the event's budget and track expenses.
    *   Coordinate with the venue to arrange for parking and accessibility (if required).

7.  **Safety and Security:**
    *   Review the event's safety and security protocols.
    *   Ensure the venue is equipped with necessary safety measures (e.g., first aid kits, emergency exits).

8.  **Timing and Timing Management:**
    *   Plan the event's start and end times.
    *   Coordinate with the performers and speakers (if any).

9.  **Event Promotion and Sign-up:**
    *   Create a sign-up sheet for attendees to RSVP.
    *   Promote the event through the university's online platforms (if applicable).

10. **Post-Event:**
    *   Review the event's success and gather feedback.
    *   Conduct a thorough audit to ensure everything ran smoothly.
