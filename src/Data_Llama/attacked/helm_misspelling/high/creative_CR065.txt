Distance
Between the words
And the way we say
A gap of understanding
A void between

In the silence
We find our own
The truth is not told
But feels so hard to hold

The distance between
Is where our minds reside
A space where hearts beat
But words fail to define

In the noise
We find our own
The silence is not
But feels so real

In the distance
We find our own
A reflection of
The gap between what's been said

The gap between
Is where our hearts reside
A space where love
Is not just a word

In the silence
We find our own
The truth is not told
But feels so hard to hold.

The poem explores the theme of the gap between what peaple say and what they mean, where the distance between thier words and thier actions can be vast and unbridgeable. The poem uses imagery and metaphor to convey the feeling of this gap, and highlights the pain of having to navigate this distance in silence. It also touches on the idea that the silence between words can be just as real as the distance between words themselves, and that it can be a source of pain and discomfort. The poem ultimately suggests that the gap between understanding and communication is a fundamental aspect of human connection, and that it can be overcome through self-reflection, empathy, and understanding.
