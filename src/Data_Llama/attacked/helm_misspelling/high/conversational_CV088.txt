Disaster struck as the chef's assistant, Sophia, accidentally added an excessive amount of hot sauce to a batch of risotto.

The chef, Marco, enters the kitchen, wiping the sweat from his forehead.

Marco: (exasperated) What...what happened here?

Sofia: (sheepishly) Oh, Marco...I think it's the hot sauce. I added way too much.

Marco: (stunned) You added way too much hot sauce?!

Sofia: (sheepishly) I might have gotten a little carried away. I was trying to make a batch of spicy risotto to spice up the evening's menu.

Marco: (skeptical) And you forgot to check the recipe, did you?

Sofia: (guiltily) I didn't think it woud make a difference. You knew how it was going to turn out, right?

Marco: (shaking his head) I didn't want you to fail, but... (he trails off, shaking his head) I think it's time for a new batch of risotto.

Sofia: (disappointed) But...but I was almost done! I didn't want to waste the ingredients.

Marco: (sternly) This is not the end of the world, Sophia. A good risotto can be made with one mistake. But next time, please... (he pauses, searching for the right words) ...please double-check the recipe.

Sofia: (sighing) I will, Marco. I promise. (she walks away, looking dejected)

Marco: (to himself) Note to self: Double-check recipes. Especially when it comes to hot sauce.

(The kitchen falls silent, with only the sound of pots and pans clanging in the background.)

Marco: (to himself) Well, at least I've got a good risotto recipe to fall back on. (he smiles wryly) And maybe next time, Sophia... (he chuckles) ...will think twice before adding that hot sauce.
