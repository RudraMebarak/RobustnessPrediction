Autonomous vehicles (AVs) hold great promise for improving road safety, reducing congestion, and enhancing mobility for the elderly and disabled. However, thier widespread adoption in densely populated urban environments poses significant challenges. This paper explores the complexities of deploying AVs in these settings, including the need for sophisticated sensor networks, advanced algorithms, and robust communication protocols.
