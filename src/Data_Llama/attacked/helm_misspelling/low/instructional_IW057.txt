**Pre-Journey Checklist (Prerequisites: None)**

**Prerequisites:**

* You should be at least 18 years old.
* You should have a reliable vehicle or transportation method (e.g., public transportation, taxi, or rental car).

**Sequence of Actions:**

1. **Prepare Your Vehicle (10-15 minutes)**:
	* Check the oil level, tire pressure, and brake pads (if using a rental car).
	* Make sure the vehicle's registration and insurance are up to date.
	* Check the fuel level and refill as needed.
* **Gather Essential Documents (5-10 minutes)**:
	* Make sure you have a valid driver's license, vehicle registration, and any necessary insurance documents (e.g., health insurance card).
* **Pack Essential Items (30-60 minutes)**:
	* Pack clothing and personal items (e.g., phone charger, snacks, first-aid kit).
	* Check the weather forecast for your destination and pack accordingly (e.g., umbrella, raincoat).
* **Verify Completion (5-10 minutes)**:
	* Check the list to ensure you have everything.

**Verification Method:**

* Take a photo of the checklist and attach it to your driver's license or a printed copy.
* Ask someone to review the checklist with you.

By following these steps, you'll be well-prepared for a successful journey. Safe travels!
