## Step 1: Understand the Group Activity
Begin by understanding what the group activity is, the materials needed, and any specific rules or guidelines provided by the group leader or instructor.

## Step 2: Prerequisites
Determine if there are any prerequisites or restrictions for participation. This could include prerequisites such as age, height, or any special skills or equipment needed.

## Step 3: Write Clear Instructions
Write clear and concise instructions that outline what each participant needs to do. Use simple language and avoid jargon or technical terms if possible. Include visual aids and diagrams as necessary to help participants understand the process.

## Step 4: Sequence of Actions
Outline the exact sequence of actions that participants must follow. This will help ensure that the group activity runs smoothly and that no one is left behind or confused.

## Step 5: Verification of Completion
Provide a way for participants to verify that they have completed the activity. This could involve a checklist, a checklist checklist, or even a simple verification prompt.

## Step 6: Example
Here is an exmaple of a beginner-friendly guide:

**Activity:** Build a Birdhouse

**Prerequisites:** Age 8 and up, no prior building experience neccessary

**Materials:**

* Lumber (2" x 4" x 6' long)
* Hammer
* Drill
* Drill bits
* Screws
* Nails
* Waterproof glue
* Paint or varnish (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. Cut the lumber into the following pieces: 2 pieces for the sides (12" long), 1 piece for the back (12" long), and 1 piece for the front (12" long).
2. Assemble the sides, back, and front by screwing them together with 2" screws.
3. Attach the roof to the top of the birdhouse using nails.
4. Use waterproof glue to seal any gaps between the pieces.
5. Paint or varnish the birdhouse (optional).

**Verification:**
To verify that you have completed the activity, answer the following:

* Have you assembled the birdhouse?
* Is it securely attached to the sides?
* Is it free of gaps and uneven edges?

If you answered yes to all the questions, you have completed the activity.

The final answer is: This is not a numerical anwser, but rather a written guide for writing clear instructions for a group activity.
