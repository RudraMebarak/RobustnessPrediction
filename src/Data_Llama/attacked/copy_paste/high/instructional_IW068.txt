**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with a billing platform or CRM (Customer Relationship Management) software
* Understanding of recurring payments and subscriptions
* Basic accounting or bookkeeping knowledge

**Guide:**

### Step 1: Gather Necessary Information

1. Identify the recurring subscriptions you want to track:
	* Make a list of all recurring subscriptions you want to monitor.
2. Gather necessary information:
	* Subscription name
	* Start and end dates
	* Payment frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly)
	* Payment amount
	* Payment method (e.g., credit card, bank account)

### Step 2: Set Up Your Tracking System

1. Set up a new tracking spreadsheet or CRM:
	* Create a table or list to store recurring subscription information.
	* Include columns for:
		+ Subscription name
		+ Start date
		+ End date
		+ Payment frequency
		+ Payment amount
		+ Payment method
2. If you're using a CRM or billing platform, integrate it with your tracking spreadsheet or CRM.

### Step 3: Track and Update Subscription Information

1. Update the tracking spreadsheet or CRM:
	* Enter the information for a List**
	* Create a list of recurring subscriptions you want to track
	* Include the subscription name, description, and payment method
	* Note the subscription start date and end date (if applicable)
4. **Analyze Subscription History**
	* Review the subscription history for each recurring subscription
	* Look for any recurring charges or payments
	* Identify any recurring subscriptions that are no longer active
5. **Determine Which Subscriptions to Cancel**
	* Review the subscription list and identify any recurring subscriptions that:
		+ Are no longer active
		+ Have been inactive for over 90 days
		+ Have a payment method that is no longer active
	* Consider canceling any subscriptions that are not being used or are no longer needed
6. **Verify Completion**
	* Check the "Active" status for each subscription to confirm it has been canceled
	* Verify that the subscription has been removed from the list
