## Step 1: Understanding Centralized Database Architecture
A centralized database architecture is a type of database where a single entity controls access to the data. All data is stored on a single server, and any request to access or modify data must be sent to the central server. This architecture is often used in small to medium-sized organizations or in applications requirements.
- Scalability: Decentralized databases can handle low traffic and small amounts of data.
- Cost-effectiveness: Decentralized databases can be less expensive to implement and maintain.

Limitations:
- Scalability: Decentralized databases can be more challenging to scale, as each server must be manually updated.
- Performance: Decentralized databases can experience slower access times due to network latency.
