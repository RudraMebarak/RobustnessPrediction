## Step 1: Understanding the concept of sustainable development
Sustainable development is a concept that aims to balance economic, social, and environmental needs by using natural resources and generating waste in a way that does not threaten the long-term viability of the planet.

## Step 2: Identifying the three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development
The three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development are economic, social, and environmental.

## Step 3: Describing the economic dimension
The economic dimension of sustainable development involves the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services. It considers the economic costs and benefits of development activities, sustainable development refers to the production and consumption of goods and services, as well as the allocation of resources. It involves the assessment of economic costs and benefits, and the consideration of factors such as economic growth, employment, and poverty reduction.

## Step 4: Explain the social dimension of sustainable development
The social dimension of sustainable development refers to the well-being and quality of life of individuals and communities. It involves the assessment of social costs and benefits, and the consideration of factors such as education, healthcare, and social justice.
