**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with Excel and basic accounting concepts
2. Understanding of the importance of creating a budget
3. Basic knowledge of financial calculations (e.g., percentages, ratios)

**Step 1: Gather and Organize Data (Timeframe: 1-2 hours)**

1. Gather the following information:
	* Income sources (e.g., salary, investments, freelancing)
	* Fixed expenses (e.g., rent, utilities, insurance)
	* Variable expenses (e.g., entertainment, transportation, hobbies)
	* Savings goals (e.g., emergency fund, retirement)
	* Debt obligations (e.g., credit cards, loans)
	* Other relevant expenses (e.g., phone bill, subscription services)
2. Create a list or spreadsheet to organize the Categories (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Create a list of budget categories, such as:
	+ Venue and catering
	+ Audio-visual and technical services
	+ Travel and accommodation
	+ Speakers and volunteers
	+ Marketing and promotion
	+ Miscellaneous
* Assign a realistic budget range to each category based on industry standards and your organization's experience

**Step 3: Estimate Expenses (Time: 60 minutes)**

* For each budget category, estimate the following:
	+ Fixed costs (rentals, equipment, etc.)
	+ Variable costs (travel, catering, etc.)
	+ Contingency funds (unexpected expenses)
* Use historical data or industry benchmarks to estimate expenses
* Consider factors like inflation, taxes, and market fluctuations

**Step 4: Calculate Total Expenses (Time: 60 minutes)**

* Add up the estimated expenses for each budget category
* Verify the calculations by double-checking the estimates and expenses

**Step 5: Determine Contingency Funds (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Calculate the contingency fund for each budget category based on the estimated expenses
* Set aside 10-20% of the total expenses for unexpected expenses

**Step 6: Create a Budget Summary (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Compile the budget breakdowns for each category
* Create a summary of the budget, including:
	+ Total expenses
	+ Contingency funds
	+ Revenue projections (if applicable)
2. Calculate the total budget for all categories
3. Verify that the total budget does not exceed 100% and is realistic
4. Review the budget plan and make any necessary adjustments

**Prerequisites verified**

1. You have gathered and organized the necessary data
2. You have calculated the total income and total expenses
3. You have determined the percentage of total income for each category
4. You have created a budget plan for each category
5. You have verified the budget plan (completed)
