**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with a spreadsheet or a note-taking app
2. Understanding of basic accounting principles (e.g., categorizing expenses, tracking income)
3. A computer or mobile device with an internet connection
4. Optional: a budgeting app or online tool (e.g., Mint, Personal Capital)

**Guide:**

**Step 1: Set up your record-keeping system (Timeframe: 1 day)**

1. Choose a spreadsheet or note-taking app to organize your expenses. Popular options include:
	* Google Sheets (free)
	* Microsoft Excel (free)
	* Trello (free)
	* Evernote (free)
	* Pen and paper (for a basic, no-fuss approach)
2. Open your chosen tool and create a new spreadsheet or note-taking page.
3. Set up a simple structure:
	* Income (column A)
	* Expense category (column B)
	* Expense name (column C)
	* Amount (column D)
	* Date (column E)
	* Description (column F)

**Step 2: Track your income (Timeframe: Ongoing)**

1. Set up automatic reminders or notifications for:
	* Bi-weekly or monthly deposits
	* Payments due (e.g., bills, credit card payments)
2. Use a spreadsheet or note-taking app to track:
	* Income sources (e.g., salary, investments, freelance work)
	* Income amounts
	* Payment schedules
3. Update the spreadsheet regularly to ensure accuracy and visibility.

**Step 3: Categorize expenses (Timeframe: YNAB (You Need a Budget). Install the app on your smartphone.
3. **Link your digital wallet or payment method**: Connect your digital wallet or payment method to the app. This will enable you to make online transactions and add expenses to your records.
4. **Create a budget template**: Use the app to create a budget template. This will help you organize your expenses and categorize them.
5. **Start tracking expenses**: Use the app to record your expenses. You can add transactions manually or link them from your digital wallet or payment method. Be sure to include:
	* Date
	* Category (e.g., food, transportation, entertainment)
	* Description
	* Amount
6. **Regularly review and update your records**: Set a reminder to review your expenses on a regular basis (e.g., weekly, monthly). Update your records as needed to ensure accuracy.
7. **Verify completion**: Once you've completed your records, verify that they are accurate and up-to-date.

**Tips and Reminders:**

* Be consistent in your expense tracking: Try to make it a habit to record your expenses regularly.
* Be thorough: Include all transactions, no matter how small.
* Review and update regularly: This will help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions.
* Consider sharing your records with a trusted partner or financial advisor for additional support.

**Verification Process:**

* Take a photo of your completed expense records as a digital snapshot.
