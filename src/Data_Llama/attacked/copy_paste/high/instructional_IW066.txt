**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with recycling terminology
2. A basic understanding of waste management principles
3. Access to a recycling bin with separate compartments (for paper, plastic, glass, and metal) and a lid
4. A willing attitude to learn about recycling practices

**The Sorting Process:**

1. **Prepare the recycling bin:** Ensure the recycling bin is clean and dry before sorting. If the bin is not clean, rinse it with water and dry it thoroughly before proceeding.

2. **Separate the materials:** Begin by separating the materials into four distinct categories: paper, plastic, glass, and metal. This will make it easier to sort and ensure that materials are placed in the correct recycling bin.

   a. Paper: 
     - Collect newspapers, cardboard, and paperboard.
     - Place these materials in the paper recycling bin (green plastic bag).

   b. Plastic: 
     - Collect plastic bottles, jugs, and containers (e.g., water bottles, food packaging).
     - Place these materials in the plastic recycling bin (clear plastic bag).

   c. Glass: 
     - Collect glass bottles and jars.
     - Place these materials in the glass recycling bin (brown plastic bag).

   d. 3. **Sort Materials**
	* Start by sorting the materials into categories:
		+ Paper (newspaper, cardboard, paperboard)
		+ Cardboard
		+ Glass
		+ Plastic
		+ Metal
		+ Food waste
	* Use the following guidelines to sort:
		+ Paper: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Cardboard: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Glass: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Plastic: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Metal: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Food waste: put it in the trash can
4. **Check and Verify**
	* Once you've sorted all the materials, check to make sure they're all in the correct categories
	* If any materials don't fit, go back and sort them again
	* Take a moment to review your sorting and make sure everything is accurate
5. **Record and Store**
	* Write down what you sorted and when it was done on the piece of paper or note
	* Store the paper in a safe place, like a file or a recycling bin

**Tips and Reminders:**

* Make sure to check with your local recycling program for any specific guidelines or restrictions resources for specific guidelines and best practices.
