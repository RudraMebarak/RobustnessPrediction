The University of Azura, established over 1,000 years ago, has been a beacon of knowledge and innovation, attracting scholars from across the world. During the Golden Age of the University, a time of unprecedented intellectual and cultural growth, Azuran scholars made groundbreaking discoveries in various fields, including mathematics, medicine, and astronomy. These breakthroughs sparked a new era of scientific understanding and fueled the spread of ideas across the globe.

## Step 3: The Discovery of the Ancient Artifacts
Deep within the University's ancient library, hidden away for centuries, lies a collection of mysterious artifacts that hold the secrets of the past. These relics, which include ancient texts, manuscripts, and mysterious devices, contain knowledge and knowledge-bearing energies that have been lost to the world.
