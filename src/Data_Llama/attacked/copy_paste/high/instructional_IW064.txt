3. **Step 3: Water Your Plant**
	* Water your plant thoroughly, but make sure not to overwater. Check the soil moisture by sticking your finger into the soil up to the first knuckle.
	* Watering frequency will depend on the plant's watering needs, but as a general rule:
		+ Water every 1-3 days in hot and dry weather.
		+ Water every 3-5 days in cool and humid weather.
4. **Step 4: Monitor and Adjust**
	* Keep an eye on your plant's watering needs and adjust your schedule as needed.
	* Observe your plant's response to watering, looking for signs such as droopy leaves, yellowing leaves, or a decrease in new growth.
5. **Step 5: Check for Root Rot**
	* Inspect your plant's roots every 1-2 weeks to check for signs of root rot.
	* If you notice any rotting or soft roots, adjust your watering schedule to prevent further damage.
6. **Step 6: Maintain a Healthy Environment**
	* Provide your plant with the right temperature, humidity, and light conditions.
	* Fertilize your plant regularly to promote healthy growth and development.
