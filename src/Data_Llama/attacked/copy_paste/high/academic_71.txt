**Methodological approach**

1. **Data collection**: Gather information on the industrial accident, including the date and time of the accident, the location, the materials involved, and any other relevant details.
2. **Text analysis**: Analyze the texts or articles on the causes and consequences of the industrial accident to identify the role of human factors and organizational factors.
3. **Organizational factor analysis**: Analyze the organizational factors that contributed to the accident, including management decisions, safety policies, and training programs.
4. **Human factor analysis**: Analyze the role of human factors such as decision-making, teamwork, and work environment factors in the accident.
5. **Case study design**: Use a case study approach to analyze the specific industrial accident, focusing on the causes and consequences.

## Step 3: Identify the causes of the accident
The causes of the accident should be identified, including the physical and chemical conditions that led to the disaster. The causes may include:
- Human error or negligence
- Equipment failure or malfunction
- Design or manufacturing flaws
- Environmental factors, such as weather or water pollution

## Step 4: Analyze the consequences of the accident
The consequences of the accident should be analyzed, including the physical and health impacts on workers and the surrounding community. The consequences may include:
- Death or injury to workers
- Environmental damage, such as air or water pollution
- Economic costs, including lost productivity and repair costs
