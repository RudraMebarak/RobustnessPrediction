Here's the initial draft:

**Prerequisites:**

* You should have a laptop, a comfortable chair, and a quiet space.
* You should have a reliable internet connection.
* You should have have a writing or typing task list
* You have a habit of taking breaks every 60-90 minutes

**Step 1: Set Up Your Study Space**

* Choose a quiet and comfortable location for your study sessions
* Ensure good lighting and a comfortable seating arrangement
* Remove any distractions, such as TVs, phones, or social media

**Step 2: Prepare Your Writing or Typing Task List**

* Write down your study goals, tasks, and deadlines
* Prioritize your tasks and break them down into smaller, manageable chunks
* Review your task list regularly to stay organized

**Step 3: Establish a Break Schedule**

* Decide on a break schedule that works for you, e.g., every 60-90 minutes
* Use a timer or a reminder app to track your break duration and frequency

**Step 4: Implement the Screen-Break Routine**

* Start with a 5-10 minute break to stretch, move around, and refresh your mind
* Use a timer to track your break duration and stay focused
* Write down your thoughts, feelings, and insights during your break
* Take notes on what you accomplished and what you need to work on

**Step 5: Use a Pomodoro Technique**

* Set a timer for 25 minutes (called a Pomodoro)
* Work on your task without any distractions during this
	* Take a break (1-2 minutes) and do something else (e.g., stretch, move around, or grab a snack).
5. **Continue Taking Breaks:**
	* Keep writing or typing (about 5-10 minutes).
	* Take breaks (1-2 minutes) and do something else (e.g., stretch, move around, or grab a snack).
6. **Finalize Your Session:**
	* Write down any notes, summarize your study material, or create a to-do list.
	* Double-check that you have everything you need and you're ready to move on to the next session.

**Verification:**
* Take a few minutes to review your screen break routine on the laptop (if using a timer).
* Double-check that you have everything you need and you're ready to move on to the next session.
* Make sure you've completed your screen break routine (you should have a clean slate for your next session).
