Distance
Between the words
And the way we say
A gap of understanding
A void between

In the silence
We find our own
The truth is not told
But feels so hard to hold

The distance between
Is where our minds reside
A space where hearts beat
But words fail to define

In the noise
We find our own
The silence is not
But feels so real

In the distance
We find our own
A reflection of
The gap between what's been said

The gap between
Is where our hearts reside
we must find our place

The distance between what we say and what we mean
Is a mystery, a riddle to unseal
A puzzle to solve, a key to unlock
A chance to connect, to make us whole and whole
But often, it's a challenge we must face
And in the silence, we must find our place.

This poem explores the theme of the distance between what people say and what they mean, and how this distance can be a powerful metaphor for the complexities of human communication. It highlights the importance of being honest and authentic in our words and actions, and the risks of misunderstandings and hurtful words. The poem also touches on the idea that suggests that the gap between understanding and communication is a fundamental aspect of human connection, and that it can be overcome through self-reflection, empathy, and understanding.
