A vase.
The vase stands alone, a silent witness
to family moments, past and grown
Its ceramic curves, a gentle face
holding memories, a secret place
For laughter, tears, and all in between
a silent keeper of family sheen

Its stem is strong, its base is deep
a foundation for the stories it keeps
The family's history, a thread vase sits alone,
A family heirloom, its story untold.
But as the years go by, the stories unfold,
A family's history, a story to be told.

The vase has been passed down, its story to share,
A family's legacy, a love beyond compare.
From the cracked surface to the faded hue,
The vase has carried the stories, its beauty to renew.

In the living room, the book ordinary object like a vase can hold a deep and intimate significance for a family's history and memories. The poem delves into the idea that the vase is not just a passive object, but a active participant in preserving and transmitting the family's history and stories. The poem uses imagery and metaphor to convey the vase's role as a silent keeper of memories. The structure and tone of the poem are intended to evoke a sense of quiet contemplation and reverence, as if the vase is a sacred object that holds the weight of family history.
