**The Relaxation Exercise: "Breathe in Calm"**

**Preparation:**

1. Find a comfortable and quiet place to sit or lie down, where you won't be disturbed.
2. Close your eyes, take a few deep breaths, and relax your body. Feel the weight of your body on the ground or on a cushion.
3. Write down any feelings, thoughts, or physical sensations you experience during the exercise in a journal or dedicated note-taking space.

**The Sequence of Actions:**

1. **1. Grounding** (1-2 minutes): Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and focus on your physical sensations. Notice the feeling of your feet touching the ground, the sensation of the air on your skin, and the sounds around you. Allow any thoughts, worries, or distractions to pass through your mind, like clouds in the sky.
2. **2. Breathe in Calm** (1-2 minutes): Take slow, deep breaths in through your nose, filling your lungs completely. Hold the breath for a second, and then exhale slowly through your mouth, emptying your lungs. Repeat this process several times, focusing on the sensation of your breath moving in and out of breath in through your nose, filling your lungs completely.
3. **Inhale for a count of 4**: Inhale for a count of 4, filling your lungs completely. Your breathing should be deep and relaxed.
4. **Hold your breath for a count of 7**: Hold your breath for a count of 7. Try not to think about anything or let your mind wander.
5. **Exhale for a count of 8**: Exhale for a count of 8, emptying your lungs completely. Your breathing should be slow and relaxed.
6. **Repeat the cycle**: Repeat the cycle for 3-5 minutes, focusing on your breath and letting go of any thoughts or distractions.

**Verification of Completion:**

* After the 3-5 minute cycle, take a few deep breaths and notice how you feel. If you're feeling relaxed and calm, you've completed the exercise.
* You can also ask yourself: "How am I feeling now?" or "What did I notice about my body and mind during the exercise?"

* Start with shorter or longer sequences of the exercise as you become more comfortable and confident.
* Use guided recordings or meditation apps to help you get started or stay focused.
* Try different relaxation techniques, such as progressive muscle relaxation or visualization, to find what works best for you.
* Incorporate this exercise into your daily routine, such as during meditation or yoga practice, or as a quick stress-relief technique when you feel overwhelmed.
