**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with travel documents and international travel.
2. A valid passport, visa (if required), or travel documents (check with the destination country).

**Guide:**

1. **Check with your airline or travel provider:** 
	* Visit the official website or contact their customer service to ensure you have all necessary documents.
	* Verify the required documents for your route, including any additional requirements (e.g., visas).
2. **Gather essential documents:**
	* Valid passport
	* Visa (if required)
	* Travel itinerary
	* Hotel reservation
	* Travel insurance documents (if applicable)
	* Travel adapter or converter (if necessary)
	* Any other required documents for your route (e.g., boarding passes)
3. **Organize your documents:**
	* Place all documents in a secure, easily accessible location (e.g., a travel wallet or a dedicated travel bag).
	* Keep a copy of each document (in case of loss or theft).
4. **Verify completion:**
	* Visit the official website of each country you plan to visit (or contact their embassy or consulate if you're unsure).
	* Check the validity and expiration date of your passport and any other required documents.
	* Ensure you have any necessary visas or travel arrangements**: Check your flight, accommodation, and travel insurance arrangements online to confirm that everything is in order.
3. **Print or download documents**: Print or download your digital copies of your passport, flight itinerary, and accommodation booking to have ready for check-in.

**Verification and Completion (the day before departure)**

1. **Check with your airline**: Contact your airline to confirm your flight details and ensure that your documents are complete.
2. **Print your boarding pass**: Print your boarding pass or check-in online to receive your boarding pass.
3. **Download airline apps**: Download steps, you'll be well-prepared to travel internationally and have peace of mind knowing you have all necessary documents in order.
