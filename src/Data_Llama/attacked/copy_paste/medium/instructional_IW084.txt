**Example:**

Suppose you're a project manager for a marketing campaign, and you need to update the status of the "Social Media Advertising" task.

1. **Identify the task and its status**: Recognize the task you want to update, "Social Media Advertising", and note its current status, "In Progress".
2. **Check the task's due date and any relevant deadlines**: Verify that the task's due date is tomorrow and that there are no specific deadlines mentioned.
3. **Determine the required actions**: Decide on the to update.
3. Click on the **Edit Task** button.
4. In the **Status** field, select one of the following options:
	* **To-Do**: indicating that the task is not complete.
	* **In Progress**: indicating that the task is currently being worked on.
	* **Done**: indicating that the task is complete.
	* **Waiting on:** if you're waiting for something (e.g., to respond to a comment or to receive a comment).

### Step 3: Verify completion (optional)

1. If you want to verify that the task was actually completed, you can click on the **Edit Task** button again.
2. In the **Status** field, select **Done**.
3. You can then mark the task as **Completed** and confirm that it's no longer visible as incomplete.

### Step 4: Update the task's title and description (optional)

1. If you want to update the task's title or description, select the **Edit Task** button again.
2. In the **Title** and **Description** fields, enter any updates you'd like.
3. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

### Step 5: Close the task (optional)

1. If you want to delete the task, select the **Delete Task** button.
2. Confirm that you want to delete the task and its associated history.

That's it! By following these steps, you can update the status of an ongoing task and keep track of your progress. Remember to regularly review and update your tasks to ensure you're on track to meet your goals.
