* **Differentiated Learning:** Use the language learning **Listening (4 minutes):**
	* Listen to a short audio clip (1-2 minutes) of a native speaker speaking a basic phrase or topic (e.g., ordering food at a restaurant).
	* Try to recognize and repeat a few key vocabulary words or phrases.
4. **Writing (4 minutes):**

5. **Practice (5 minutes):**
	* Engage in a conversation (with a language exchange partner, online tutor, or by speaking with a native speaker) using the phrases or vocabulary you learned during reading and listening activities.
