As I observed these individuals, I realized that their struggles were not a reflection of their character, but rather a result of their language barriers and cultural differences. This experience taught me that assumptions can be a major obstacle to building genuine connections with people from different backgrounds.

When it comes to navigating unfamiliar environments, I want to revise my assumptions about others by actively listening to their stories and trying to understand their perspectives. I also plan to be more open-minded and patient, as I have discovered that cultural differences can be vast.
