3. Consider the 80/20 rule: 80% of the time, you likely use 20% of your belongings.

**Step 3: Organize and Categorize (Time: 60 minutes)**

1. Once you've decluttered and sorted, organize your remaining items into categories (e.g., books, papers, office supplies).
2. Use storage containers, labels, and folders to keep items tidy and easy to find.
3. Label each container and folder to ensure you can quickly identify what's inside.

**Step 4: Maintain and Review (Time: Ongoing)**

1. Set aside 10-15 minutes each week to maintain your study and folders to categorize and store files
2. Sort through the "To-Keep" box and categorize items
3. Create a system for labeling and organizing files
4. Set aside time to work through the sorted items and create a filing system

**Step 3: Set Up a Maintenance System (1-2 hours)**

1. Set up a routine for cleaning and organizing (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly)
2. Create a schedule for tasks (e.g., tidying up, dusting)
3. Consider investing in a paper tray or file organizer
4. Set aside time to work through any remaining tasks

**Step 4: Implement Storage Solutions (1-2 hours)**

1. Use storage bins or containers to store items
2. Label the bins or containers to keep track of contents
3. Create a system for maintaining storage (e.g., tidying up regularly)

**Step 5: Review and Refine (30 minutes to 1 hour)**

1. Review your space and systems
2. Identify any areas for improvement
3. Refine your systems and make adjustments as needed
4. Take a final walk-through to ensure everything is tidy and organized

**Verification and Completion (1 hour)**

1. Take a final walk-through to ensure everything is tidy and organized
2. Write down any notes or reminders for future tasks
3. Take a moment to admire your newly organized space!

**Additional Tips:**

* Break tasks into smaller, manageable chunks
* Use music or a timer to stay focused
* Take breaks to avoid burnout
* Be patient with yourself as you work through the process
* Consider enlisting help from a friend or family member (if needed)
