**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with basic incident response concepts (e.g., identifying potential risks, understanding organizational policies)
* Access to a computer or mobile device with an internet connection
* Minimal to no prior experience in incident response (but some knowledge of basic computer systems and cybersecurity is beneficial)

**The Incident-Response Checklist for a Small Team**

**Pre-Check (1)**

1. **Identify the incident type and scope**: Determine the nature and extent of the incident (e.g., data breach, software outage, security alert).
2. **Notify relevant teams and stakeholders**: Inform authorized personnel about the incident (e.g., IT department, security team, management).

**Incident Response (2-5)**

1. **Stop the incident (if necessary)**: If the incident is severe, interrupt its progression to prevent further damage.
2. **Contain the incident (if necessary)**: Isolate the affected system or data to prevent further spread - Define key performance indicators (KPIs)
	+ Incident Classification and Prioritization:
		- Determine incident type and severity
		- Assign incident classification and priority level
		- Document findings
	+ Incident Response and Recovery:
		- Develop incident response plan
		- Assign incident response specialists
		- Establish incident response timeline
	+ Post-Incident Activities:
		- Document lessons learned
		- Identify areas for improvement
		- Update incident response plan

**Step 3: Create a Verification Process**

* Develop a simple verification process to ensure completion of the incident response checklist:
	+ Create a checklist template with the same sections as in Step 2
	+ Assign a team member to review and verify each section
	+ Use a checklist template

**Post-Check (5)**

1. **Dispose of evidence**: Remove any sensitive information or data from the affected systems or data.
2. **Update incident response plans**: Review and update the incident response plan to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
3. **Monitor the incident response process**: Continuously monitor and review the incident response process to identify areas for improvement.

**Additional Tips**

* Encourage team members to review and familiarize themselves with the incident response checklist regularly.
* Consider using a standardized incident response checklist or template to streamline the process.
* Emphasize the importance of prompt and thorough incident response to minimize downtime and data loss.

By following this step-by-step guide, small teams can create a basic incident-response checklist that ensures they are prepared to respond to potential incidents in a timely and effective manner.
