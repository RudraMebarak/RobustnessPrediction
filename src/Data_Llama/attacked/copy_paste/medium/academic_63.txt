## Step 1: Understanding the types of assessment methods
Formative assessment involves regular, ongoing assessments during the learning process to check progress, adjust instruction, and improve student learning. Summative assessment, on the other hand, is the overall evaluation of student learning at the end of a course or program to determine whether students have achieved the learning objectives.

## Step 2: Examining formative assessment effectiveness
Formative assessment is known to enhance student learning and understanding. By regularly checking student progress and adjusting instruction, formative assessments help students to better understand the material and retain information more effectively.
