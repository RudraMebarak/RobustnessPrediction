## Step 1: Understanding Qualitative Research
Qualitative research is a research methodology that focuses on gathering and analyzing data that is typically qualitative in nature, meaning it's subjective, contextual, and doesn't aim to find hard, objective answers. It's often used to explore phenomena that are complex, nuanced, or open-ended.

## Step 2: Understanding Quantitative Research
Quantitative research is another fundamental research methodology in social science that focuses on collecting and analyzing numerical data to draw conclusions about a population or phenomenon. It aims to find hard, objective answers and is often used to test hypotheses or evaluate the effectiveness of programs or interventions.

## Step 3: Understanding Mixed-Methods Research
Mixed-methods research combines elements of both qualitative and quantitative research. It aims to address both the complexity and subjectivity of the research question and also to find hard, objective answers through quantitative data analysis.

## Step 4: Key Differences
- **Purpose**: Qualitative focuses on exploring phenomena, quantitative focuses include providing a balanced approach, allowing researchers to leverage the strengths of both qualitative and quantitative methods. Limitations include the complexity of combining different methods and the need for careful analysis.

## Step 5: Conclusion
In conclusion, each research design has its unique strengths and limitations. Qualitative research designs are ideal for exploring complex, subjective phenomena, while quantitative research designs are suited for establishing cause-and-effect relationships and providing numerical data. Mixed-methods research designs offer a balanced approach, allowing researchers to combine the strengths of both qualitative and quantitative a bridge to move between the two.
- **Quality of Evidence**: Qualitative provides rich, contextual insights into social behaviors or phenomena; quantitative offers a clear, objective understanding of population dynamics or outcomes; mixed-methods aims to balance these, providing both depth and breadth.
