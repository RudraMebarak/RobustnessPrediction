## Step 1: Definition of Algorithmic Complexity
Algorithmic complexity refers to the amount of time and/or space a computer program needs to execute in order to solve a given problem or perform a specific task. It's a measure of efficiency and optimality in solving a problem.

## Step 2: Types of Complexity
There are two primary types of complexity: time complexity (TC) and space complexity (SC). Time complexity is a function of the size of the input and is often measured in terms of the number of steps (e.g., number of operations) required to solve a problem. Space complexity is a function of the size of the input and is often measured in terms of the amount of memory required.
