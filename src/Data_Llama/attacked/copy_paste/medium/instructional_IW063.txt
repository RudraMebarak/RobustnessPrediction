**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with a computer or mobile device.
2. Basic understanding of file management and organization principles.
3. A clean and organized workspace (for example, a dedicated file or desktop for documents).

**The Process:**

1. **Prepare Your Workspace**
	* Clear any clutter or distractions from your workspace.
	* Create a designated area or file organizer for documents (e.g., file cabinet, folder, or cloud storage).
2. **Categorize and Group Documents**
	* Sort documents into categories (e.g., bills, receipts, financial documents, or papers to be shredded).
	* Group documents by type (e.g., all bills together).
3. **Organize Files Within Categories**
	* Create subfolders or subdirectories to further organize files within each category.
	* Use clear and descriptive file names to label files (e.g., "Rent Receipts - 2022" or "Tax Documents - 2021").
4. **Implement a Maintenance Routine**
	* Schedule regular time slots (e.g., weekly or monthly) to maintain and update your document organization system.
	* Set reminders or notifications for tasks like filing, updating, or reviewing documents.
5. **Verify Your Organization**
	* Regularly review your documents to ensure they are organized and easily accessible.
	* Make adjustments as needed to maintain an up-to-date and efficient system.
