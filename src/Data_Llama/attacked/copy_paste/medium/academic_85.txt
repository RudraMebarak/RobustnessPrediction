## Step 1: Understanding the concept of sustainable development
Sustainable development is a concept that aims to balance economic, social, and environmental needs by using natural resources and generating waste in a way that does not threaten the long-term viability of the planet.

## Step 2: Identifying the three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development
The three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development are economic, social, and environmental.

## Step 3: Describing the economic dimension
The economic dimension of sustainable development involves the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services. It considers the economic costs climate change, biodiversity, and resource depletion.
