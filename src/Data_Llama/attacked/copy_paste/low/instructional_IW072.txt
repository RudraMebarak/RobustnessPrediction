**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with online banking or mobile banking.
2. Understanding of basic financial concepts (budgeting, saving, etc.).
3. Ability to enter passwords and login credentials (not required but recommended).

**Sequence of Actions:**

1. **Access the bank statement online or through the mobile app**.
2. **Log in using your username and password**.
3. **Sort the transactions by date** (usually in chronological order).
4. **Review the account summary**, including your account balance, deposits, and withdrawals.
5. **Identify unfamiliar transactions**, such as:
	* Unusual or frequent transactions (e.g., high-dollar amounts or frequent withdrawals).
	* Unexplained transactions (e.g., deposits into a foreign bank account or wire transfers).
	* Transactions with unclear or missing information (e.g., date, amount, or recipient).

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

**Step 1: Access the Bank Statement**

1. Open your web browser or download and install the bank's mobile app (if not using online banking).
2. Enter your login credentials (username and password).
3. If you are using the bank's mobile app, you will likely be prompted to enroll in online banking (if not, you will be redirected to online banking).

**Step 2: Log in and Sort Transactions**

1. Once logged in, sort the transactions by date (usually in chronological order).
2. Use the navigation menu or search function to find specific transactions (e.g., to find a missing transaction).

**Step 3: Statement**

* Collect your bank statement from the bank or retrieve it online from their website.
* Make sure to have your account number, routing number, and any other required bank details readily available.
* If you have a digital copy of your statement, ensure it or recipient).

**Step 4: Identify Unusual or Frequent Transactions (Step 4)**

1. Look for transactions with:
	* High-dollar amounts (e.g., $100 or more).
	* Frequent withdrawals (e.g., multiple withdrawals within a short period).
	* Unusual or unclear information (e.g., missing recipient information or unclear transaction details).

**Step 5: Investigate Unexplained Transactions (Step 5)**

1. Check the following:
	* Check the transaction details (e.g., date, amount, and recipient information).
	* Contact the bank's customer service (if the information is unclear or missing).
	* Verify the transaction's legitimacy (e.g., confirm the recipient's identity and account information).

**Step 6: Verify Completion (Step 6)**

1. Once you have investigated and resolved any issues, you can verify completion by:
	* Re-checking the transaction history.
	* Confirming the account balance.
	* Re-checking any suspicious transactions.

**Additional Tips:**

* Set up alerts or notifications to inform you of unusual or suspicious transactions.
* Regularly review your account statements to detect and respond to potential issues.
* If you are unsure or uncomfortable with any transactions, contact the bank's customer service for assistance.

By following these steps, you will be able to effectively read and understand your bank statement, identify unfamiliar transactions, and verify any concerns you may have.
