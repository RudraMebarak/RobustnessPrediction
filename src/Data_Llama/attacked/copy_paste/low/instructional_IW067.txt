**Prerequisites:**

1. Familiarity with basic accounting and budgeting concepts
2. Understanding of the 50/30/20 rule ( allocating 50% for essential expenses, 30% for discretionary spending, and 20% for saving and debt repayment)
3. A basic understanding of categories and subcategories (e.g., housing, transportation, food, entertainment)

**Guide:**

**Step 1: Gather and Organize Financial Information (1-2 hours)**

1. Collect all financial documents (pay stubs, bills, credit card statements, etc.)
2. Calculate your total monthly income and fixed expenses (rent, utilities, insurance, etc.)
3. Categorize your expenses into essential (housing, transportation, food), discretionary (entertainment, hobbies, travel), and savings (emergency fund, retirement, etc.)
4. Create a list of all your fixed expenses, including amounts and due dates

**Step 2: Determine Your Budget Goals (30 minutes)**

1. Based on your income and expenses, determine how much you want to allocate for each category
2. Consider your short-term (less repayment.

* Prioritize needs over wants to avoid overspending.

2. Calculate your total monthly income and allocate it according to your budget goals
3. Enter your fixed expenses and calculate the remaining amount for each category
4. Create subcategories and track your spending in each category

**Step 4: Review, Adjust, and Refine (30-60 minutes)**

1. Review your budget to ensure you're allocating enough for each category
2. Identify areas for reduction or increase (e.g., allocate more for transportation or entertainment)
3. Refine your budget as needed to reflect changes in income or expenses

**Step 5: Verify and Track Progress (ongoing)**

1. Set up automatic payments or reminders for bills and savings
2. Track your spending and income regularly (e.g., every month or every two months)
3. Review your budget at regular intervals (e.g., every 3-6 months) to ensure you're on track
