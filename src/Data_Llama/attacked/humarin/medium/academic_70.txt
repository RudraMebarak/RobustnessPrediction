Step 1: Introduction to Machine Learning and Deep Learning (ML&D) Machine learning is a subfield of artificial intelligence that involves training algorithms to make predictions or decisions based on data. Traditionally, classical machine learning relies heavily on intuition and rule-based systems, while deep learning uses artificial neural networks (ANNs) to build models that are more adaptable and efficient. This is especially true for tasks like medical image classification, where the data can be noisy or contain outliers. Deep learning systems face challenges such as data requirements, interpretability issues, and overfitting.
