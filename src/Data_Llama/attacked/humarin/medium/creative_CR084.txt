Despite being a portly, middle-aged man known for his love of designer suits, Auguste Dumont's methods are questionable and may even lead to more harm than good. His approach is marked by authoritarianism and manipulation, with an emphasis on propaganda and intimidation to keep his community safe from oppression. The community is witnessing a surge of rebellion among its young people against Dumont's leadership, as some view him as an impotent tyrant holding them back.
