The story takes place on a warm, sunny day, as you make your way to the mysterious lighthouse. The sun glows brightly over the beach, and you can't help but wonder if the place is actually empty. You encounter scattered objects such as a broken chair, empty water bottle, and paper with an incant message: "Don't leave." But as you explore the darkness of the lighthouse, you discover that someone else is watching. You don’t know if you’re going to survive. All you know is that you are stuck there, nothing will ever leave you.’ You are at this lighthouse. There is something with you beneath you and as you get pulled towards the light, you feel a stirring. The dust has accumulated so far. You try to push back, but it is too overwhelming.
