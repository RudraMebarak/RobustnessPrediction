EcoCycle Inc. introduced its flagship product, the Smart Recycling Bin, which uses AI and IoT sensors to track and prioritize recyclable materials in real-time. The company struggled to gain a significant market share despite strong marketing and favorable user reviews, as well as high abandonment rates. The failure resulted in significant financial losses, including: - Revenue generated from the product, which was lower than expected; a lack of cash flow due to delayed payments to suppliers and partners, leading to poor financial results, poor business practices, and the need to reassess the financial situation.
