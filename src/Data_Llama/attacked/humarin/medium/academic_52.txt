Peer review is the process by which experts evaluate the quality of research by examining the methodology, data, and conclusions presented in a paper. It serves to reduce the risk of errors and omissions that could lead to false or misleading conclusions. Furthermore, peer review promotes scientific integrity by making sure that all parties involved are accountable for the work submitted. This helps prevent misconduct in scientific research. Additionally, it encourages transparency and reproducibility. By reviewing and commenting on each other's work, reviewers can encourage critical thinking and skeptical thinking.
