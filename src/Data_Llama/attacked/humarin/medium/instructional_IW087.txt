Basic knowledge of spreadsheet operations (e.g., SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT) * Understanding of how to enter data in a spreadsheet using basic tools. By using the AutoSum tool, you can apply a formula to any set of cells and automatically enter the result. For instance, if you want to find the average salary of employees in ONE department by simply selecting the range A1:A10 from your Excel sheet, applying the formula and repeating the process for multiple employee ranges or departments.
