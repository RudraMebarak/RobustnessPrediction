A meta-analysis found that text-based learning materials are more effective than hands-on laboratory courses. By using a pandas DataFrame, it is possible to determine the most effective approach for teaching programming to beginners by analyzing the mean student satisfaction, learning outcomes, and time-to-competency metrics. The results indicate that the most successful approach is to use interactive coding environments (e.g., playing games or utilizing online platforms) before delving into laboratory courses with peers or instructors.
