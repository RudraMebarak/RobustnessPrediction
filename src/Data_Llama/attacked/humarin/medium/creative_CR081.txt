As a Guardian, I must face some challenges that can make her feel like she's not quite ready for the challenge. Lyra, who is 25 years old, has been granted supernatural powers that allow her to harness the energy of the land and redirect it to protect innocent people from supernatural forces. Her closest allies, the vampire hunters known as the "Ordre des Viet" (Sabrina and Elijah Jones) often question the wisdom of her abilities, but she struggles with how to balance her role in protecting the world. Suitable for young adults with an interest in The Mortal Instruments series and the Harry Potter series.
