As a high school history teacher, an older woman shared with me how she tried to teach history to her students in the 21st century by incorporating current events, social studies, and cultural themes. She shared her struggles with understanding the complexity of historical context and how it affects her identity and relationships. She narrated that during a period when the nation was transitioning towards globalization and economy, she described being in "globalized form". Moving towards a globalized economy and reverting to the past.
