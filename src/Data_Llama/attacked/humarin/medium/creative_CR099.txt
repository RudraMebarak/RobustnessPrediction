The Wright brothers' invention, the Wright Flyer, was not as successful as anticipated by early aviation enthusiasts. Instead, Emmet Thompson, an engineer and aeronautical enthusiast, modified the design to create the "AirRail," which connected high-speed rail cars between cities across the country. This innovation led to faster and more efficient transportation, economic growth, and the development of parks and green spaces. The AirRail's growth and innovation during the early 1900s are depicted in this alternate timeline, with the Wright brothers' Wright Flyer meeting the expectations of aviation pioneers.
