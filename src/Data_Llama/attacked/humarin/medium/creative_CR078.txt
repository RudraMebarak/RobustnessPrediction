Music is the ultimate form of currency in Elyria's music industry. It is not just about its monetary worth but also its emotional impact, cultural significance, and historical context. People rely on music to build their economies. The process of creating music involves talented individuals, studios, record companies, labeled artists, musicians, producers, marketers, traders, etc. Each MP type represents a specific genre of music, with varying prices. The preservation and restoration of cultural heritage music is strongly encouraged by its value, as those who own and perform these works can expect high prices for their rights. Furthermore, the music economy offers opportunities for music education, journalism, and programming, making it a prime example of globalization and migration.
