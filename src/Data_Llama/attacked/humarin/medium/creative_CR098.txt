The University of Azura, a mystical institution, is located in the heart of an unknown forest. From its inception until the present day, the university has been renowned for its exceptional collection of ancient artifacts. A journey through time will guide the explorers back to the past, uncovering clues and secrets that will never be seen again. As they touch the relics, they are inundated with visions of the past and discover new technologies and discoveries that will change the course of human history. This event is known as the New Era of Scientific Understanding, and it marks the beginning of a new era of scientific understanding.
