The study examined the extent and characteristics of plastic pollution in marine ecosystems along the 100-kilometer coastline in the Gulf of Mexico using 200 beach surveys and 100 water samplings over a 12-month period. Plastic debris was found to be a significant source of pollution in marine ecosystems, with an average of 3.5% of beach samples and 1.8% of water samples containing this type of debris. Our findings highlight the importance of education campaigns, producer responsibility, and targeted cleanups for reducing plastic pollution, as well as the relationship between plastic debris and different environmental conditions. Additional research is necessary to comprehend the intricate connections between plastic debris and various environmental factors, as well as to devise effective strategies for reducing plastic pollution.
