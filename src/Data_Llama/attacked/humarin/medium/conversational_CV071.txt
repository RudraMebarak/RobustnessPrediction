The old town square market last weekend was an unforgettable experience, with a vibrant display of local produce and handmade goods, as well as the presence of freshly baked bread, fresh fruit, and cheese. The square was filled with people lingering around, while the smell of coffee and freshly ground was exuding creativity and beauty. I felt transported back in time and grateful for the opportunity to experience the joys and wonders of the past. It's rare to witness a market like this every day, but the one I went to last weekend was truly remarkable. As I left the square, I felt as though I had forgotten something from my life, and I couldn't be happier about returning to the old town square.
