The constant presence of people and noise in the city causes Emily to feel drained and disconnected, leaving her questioning why she chose to move to the new city. As she spends time alone in her bookstore, she realizes that she didn't have to be connected to anyone for happiness. She also finds joy in noticing the quiet moments and reconnecting with old friends and strangers. She acknowledges that she has been given something, and she's resolute in preserving it. The novel Emily reads serves as a guide for her life. She realizes instead that being alone is not to be feared, but rather treasured and nurtured. At times, she learns from taking time alone and connecting with nature, which is the best way to find herself.
