To understand basic plant care, you must have a basic understanding of plants and be able to observe them. You should also be aware of the different indoor plant types and their watering needs. To ensure the proper watering schedule for indoor plants, it is recommended to water them every 1-2 weeks in bright light, moderately in winter, and every other week in low light or medium light. Adjust the light temperature to suit the plant's needs by gently transferring water from one location to another, such as a warmer location.
