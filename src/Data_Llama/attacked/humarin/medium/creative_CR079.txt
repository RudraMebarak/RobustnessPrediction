The city is a melting pot of green and yellow, with skyscrapers gazing up at night and streets brimming with neon signs. Neo-Earth's inhabitants are made up of families from far-off regions who left behind after the climate began to fall apart. They live in an underground bunker that was equipped with sustainable technology, including hydroponics, solar panels, and advanced desalination systems. The city's water supply was constantly in danger, and the residents had to work together to preserve the balance of their ecosystem. As Aria grew older, she discovered that the city was not just a product of human creativity and innovation, but also an example of how humanity could achieve great things with our shared knowledge. This discovery placed her at odds with some of the leaders of The Architects, who were willing to risk their lives if they didn't disclose their secrets. She was the catalyst for a new era of human evolution, where humanity would rise to the challenges of future destiny and forge something good and bad about Neo-Earth: The future is uncertain, but Aria stood out as the hero who would be the savior shaping the destiny of one planet and city.
