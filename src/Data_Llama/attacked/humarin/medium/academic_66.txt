Social media platforms have become a significant source of information and disinformation, leading to concerns about the reliability of online sources and the spread of false information. This review reviews various methods for detecting misinformation on social media sites, including natural language processing methods, behavioral analysis, and machine learning algorithms. The use of NLP and deep learning techniques can reveal patterns and anomalies in online data, but there are limitations to these approaches. These methods may not always be effective in detecting misinformation on social media platforms, particularly when the text is ambiguous or the linguistic structure is complex. Machine learning algorithms may also be biased and susceptible to overfitting, which can lead to poor performance.
