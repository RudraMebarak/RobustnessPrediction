The story of "The River's Lament" begins in the heart of an ancient forest where a potent river once flowed silently through the land, bringing with it the rhythms of nature to the people who lived along its banks, the calm sun rising over the still waters that provided sustenance for the crops and livestock that thrived there, and the children who played on the banks of the river after the harvesting season starting to notice the changes in landscape and climate, which eventually led to difficulties as the floods became more frequent. The river's banks continued to weaken, jeopardizing the livelihoods of the communities that depended on it. Despite the challenges it faced, the forests remained silent as the community struggled to maintain their traditions and ways of life. As the seasons passed, they fought to keep the river together, using their knowledge and abilities to find solutions to the problems presented by their environment. And so, with the people who lived alongside them, their grievances persisted, as they recognized the importance of preserving the ancient and sacred river.
