The importance of incorporating mandatory data privacy education into university curricula has led to discussions about the role of universities in raising public awareness and promoting responsible data management practices. Mandatory data privacy education tends to overemphasize technical solutions and neglect social and cultural dimensions, according to Kumar and Srivastava.
