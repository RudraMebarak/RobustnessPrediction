I am a young man named Jack, and I have just one more task before it. I carry my letter with me to the Great Hall of the city, where the Festival of Lights is scheduled to begin at dawn. As I walk through the winding streets, I can sense the excitement that builds up as I go past the stalls selling colorful fabrics, sweets, candy, etc. My heart races with excitement because I know I will not be there on time, but I must wait for the mysterious stranger who will disrupt the festival's activities. The dignitary declares, "You have been chosen to deliver a warning to the fire's source." As I nod off, I accept the challenge with pride and courage. A small pouch is given to me along with an enigmatic note written on it. "Keep this safe, Jack. This may not be the time to ruin the festival," he says. I take the pouch with me. My heart races with anticipation as I walk towards the city gates, determined not to let the flames disturb my spirit.
