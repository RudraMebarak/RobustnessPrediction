The islanders of Kalia, a remote island situated between two major oceans and once populated by massive storm surges, have developed a distinctive linguistic structure and cultural practices that reflect the island's changing landscape. The language known as "Kaelin" is characterized by its expressive nature, with a complex system of suffixes and prefixes that reflect different aspects of the island environment and its inhabitants.
