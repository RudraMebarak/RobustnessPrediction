The shopkeeper's visit to see his troubled but brilliant son stirred feelings of his own pride and determination within the family. Despite feeling the pressure of a job, he chose not to do it for him. Instead, allowing him to focus on his studies and growing up as an independent contractor for years. He would always be there for him, there was no place for him to hide and he felt a sense of peace as he stood up. He knew that his son would be greatly appreciated after all the hard work he had done in this shop and his son's future in the business had come to light. He looked back at the shop with pride and gratitude, knowing that his family would always have the privilege of being here with him, and even though they were gone, he still could never forget about anything that was important to him.
