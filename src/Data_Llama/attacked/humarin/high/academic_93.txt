The importance of ensuring mandatory data privacy education in university curricula is recognized by many to encourage citizens to act responsibly with their information, as well as to foster their understanding and compliance with regulations. Strong Cases:** There are some strong arguments against mandatory data privacy education, but they do not give enough attention to technical solutions that affect student attitudes. The Journal of Information Systems Education, 31,(1), 1-13 (1986), suggests that Standardization and Accessibility in Mandatory Data Privacy Education should be considered in academic position papers.
