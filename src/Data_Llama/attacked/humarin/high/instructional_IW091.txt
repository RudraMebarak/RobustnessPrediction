Prior to completing Stage 1: Planning and Design, candidates must possess at least two basic building skills: (1) a strong groundworking foundation; (2) someone who can work in any workshop or has enough tools for the task; (3) a minimum number of tools needed to operate wood and materials required for the job; and, if necessary, an adequate budget and safety measures should be taken; and (3) 1-2 hours are needed to prepare a detailed estimate for a project type and outline how to proceed with a preliminary sketch. The second stage requires a basic knowledge about the operation. Inventory materials and ensure quality, condition, and availability by creating a list of materials and quantities.
