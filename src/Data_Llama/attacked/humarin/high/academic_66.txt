The prevalence of misinformation and disinformation on social media platforms has prompted the development of various methods to detect misinformation. This review investigates several approaches, including natural language processing (NLP) methods, behavioral analysis, and machine learning algorithms, human oversight and editing, and deep learning techniques. Despite the potential advantages of NLP and deep learning techniques, they can still be flawed as it may not be entirely efficient in detecting misinformation on social media platforms due to limitations such as inconsistent data analysis.
