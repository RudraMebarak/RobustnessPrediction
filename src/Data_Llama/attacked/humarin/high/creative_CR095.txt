Throughout history, the River "Kaelin" had been a peaceful retreat among many people, but in its original location, it was not easily approachable by modern means. Instead, one could appreciate the calm and tranquil waters of the river, which acted as a connection between the land and its inhabitants. The river's rhythms changed over time, and those around it became more turbulent; farmers and children were the first to experience the joys of playing on its banks and rivers, while the floods became more frequent and difficult for them to sustain their lives. The erosion that engulfed the river's banks persisted, making it impractical for the communities it posed. Even as Kaelin's people struggled to survive in the changing seasons, they continued to work diligently to protect and cherish the natural world within its precious ecosystem.
