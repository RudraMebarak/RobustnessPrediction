The study notes that the emphasis on transparency and elaboration in AI evaluations may hinder the creation of fully AI-equipped systems. ### Abstract Artificial Intelligence (AI) has made significant progress since its founding in the 1950s; companies using it can use it to judge their own decisions or add new ones without taking into consideration a range of limitations including shortcomings such as human expert opinion, system evaluation methods, and weaknesses like expert feedback loops. **Problems of Expert System Evaluation**: The process of evaluating AI systems through expert judgment can be time-consuming and inadequately accurate, as it requires an understanding of the user's experience and interoperability with the system.
