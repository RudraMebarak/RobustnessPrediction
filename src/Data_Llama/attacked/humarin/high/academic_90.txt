Urban planning is crucial in influencing the environment. It involves designing cities to minimize negative impacts on the environment and maximize benefits. ## Step 3: Evaluate the impact of population density on urban planning. Urban planning with lower population densities can have beneficial effects on public health, education, and quality of life. Urban sustainability must be balanced between low levels of environmental harm and higher levels of sustainable practices. Urban planners should actively participate in community engagement and civic outreach efforts to reduce conflict and improve living conditions within urban neighborhoods.
