During the Grounding phase, you should sit and breathe deeply, observe your feet being lifted off the ground and use your nose to breath in a relaxed state. You can also practice breathing deep under your tongue. Simply observe and adjust it, without attempting to modify or react to it. **Verification of Finish:** Conduct yourself by opening your eyes and taking brief breaths and testing out how calm and relaxed you feel; also, what feels like being fully present, conscious and aware in your demeanor and presence; your thoughts, feelings, physical balance and awareness are important indicators of progress and confidence. Try using meditation apps or guided exercises such as hypnosis or visualization to focus on improvement when feeling stressed or have an excess of tension; and this can be used for other activities such as relaxation during yoga
