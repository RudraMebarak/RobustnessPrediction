For a brief informational interview, you must be employed in a field or affiliated with that organization, and at the time of your appointment, write down your interest. If they accept your offer to attend, follow up by writing a concise email, giving yourself three chances to answer questions and taking notes. You will then be required to confirm whether you agreed or disagree with the person or schedule an appointment, following which you will meet again after one week. Following an informational interview, a summary report can be prepared in five stages: 1) Identify yourself and your background; 2) Reflect on the role and industry you are applying for; 4) Evaluate the person's skills and experiences while you're hiring; 5) Prepare detailed notes to add important data points that will help guide future steps. 6) Use bullet points to highlight connections between each individual and their project/job, including any changes they may have encountered during the process or during the actual undertaking.
