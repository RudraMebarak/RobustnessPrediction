The old town square market of last weekend was an incredible experience filled with local flavors and aromas. Pedestrians, tourists and pedestrians alike gathered to enjoy the many different vendors offering food and drinks. The peaceful ambiance and charming atmosphere of this area made for an unforgettable experience. I was truly transported back in time and felt like home, as a group of people enjoyed the aroma of freshly baked bread, cider vinegar, and honey from the street vendor booths and tables. There were also vendors selling traditional crafts such as baby seats, handmade jewelry, and even a young women selling toys. It's rare to witness a market of this nature every day, but the one I went to last weekend was all torouse me. As soon as I left the square, it was like leaving something behind and I couldn't wait to go back again.
