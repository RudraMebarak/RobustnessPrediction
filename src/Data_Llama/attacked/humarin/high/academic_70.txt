## Step 1: Machine Learning and Deep Learning » Introduction Machine learning is another area of AI practice that involves training algorithms to make predictions or decisions from data—rather than classical machine learning which has traditional models, and which makes better use of human intuition and rule-based systems. ##Step 3: Deep learning Approaches are advanced, often using decision trees, linear regression methods, or simpler neural networks (#methods) built on pre-existing hypotheses. They can learn complex patterns but may not be as efficient as classical machines when it is more efficient. Despite the advantages of classical machine learning systems, deep learning systems face challenges such as data requirements, interpretability issues, and overfitting.
