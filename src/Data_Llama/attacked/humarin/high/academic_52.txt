Peer review is the process by which experts evaluate the quality of research based on the methodology, data, and conclusions presented in a paper. It serves to lower the risk of errors and omissions that might lead to false or misleading conclusions. Furthermore, peer review ensures scientific integrity by ensuring that all parties involved are accountable for the work submitted. This helps prevent misconduct and promotes transparency, as it also encourages transparency and reproducibility. Additionally, peer reviews fosters critical thinking and skepticism by encouraging rigorous evaluation of work.
