As he watches the train passing through the heart of the town, a group of people unwind and marvel at the sight of a fishing boat docking. The scene feels so exciting that Jack Harris eagerly anticipates the arrival of the railway and its aftermath, but also acknowledges the presence of uncertainty and doubt in the process. The story of Jack Harris begins in Ravenswood, a coastal town where the railway line will pass through and alter life for its inhabitants. Inspired by literature such as J.M. Barrie's on the impact of change upon small coastal communities, and Willa Cather's portrayal of those who built rural American lives, this is an opening prompt to write a story about identity and community.
