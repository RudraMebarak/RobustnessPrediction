"Goldman Sachs" conducted a study on the implementation of cybersecurity framework in a financial institution, revealing that it lost significant amounts of money due to cyber breaches and damaged its reputation. The strategy involved implementing a comprehensive cybersecurity framework that combined various security controls, including firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and encryption, as well as improved network security controls. This resulted in 85% reductions in breach incidents over two years, and marked an improvement in the effectiveness and speed of response times for new security measures. The lack of resources for maintaining and updating the security framework was a significant issue, as some employees did not receive adequate training on cybersecurity best practices.
