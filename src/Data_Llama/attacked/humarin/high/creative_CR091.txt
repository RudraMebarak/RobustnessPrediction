The forest displays a variety of moods and textures as the sun rises high above it. Once it has awakened, its ancient trunks creak in soft breezes, and the sunlight filters through and leaves a crisp coating of green that stretch out like glue before reaching the ground. The trees, now covered in dark rust, grow within the bushes and now glow with golden light. As time passes, the sun becomes more active due to the wind and snow falling over the area. The air is thick and fragrant with the smell of dead creatures. The forest suddenly becomes a more serene and introspective place, as the sun rises above it.
