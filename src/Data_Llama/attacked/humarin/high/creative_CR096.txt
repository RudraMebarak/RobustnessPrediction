Life as we know it today would be different if the speed of news and information had been restricted to the wealthy and elite. Governments would have had to wait for months or even years to spread letters, while those who were educated could still read and share articles. It's all a bit of speculative stuff, and one doesn't know for sure how different life would have been with the advent of printing press -- but it's interesting to consider the effects that it could have had on society, culture and progress as a whole.
