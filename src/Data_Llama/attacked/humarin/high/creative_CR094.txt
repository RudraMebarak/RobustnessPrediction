I stood at the window and felt the heat rise up from the pavement like a slow-moving, primal smell, which came from everywhere in the world...Thured – it was a wet torrent of thunder to my feet: light against city walls and dark fluffy sunshine escaping me... A thousand tiny water droplets clinging onto my skin...I feel like ... an artist manipulating a stage show...and yet still connected to the primal forces that shaped the world around me — a man made a grand masters playing in stages.
