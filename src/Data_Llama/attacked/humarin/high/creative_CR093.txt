I was filled with excitement, calmness, and pleasure when I gazed at the saltwater, the sway of sea breeze, and a stirring dolphin-like sound. The sun shone through my hair as I laid down on the white sand and laid out waves that blended together into a misty fog. As I prepared to walk away from the sea, there seemed to be no way for me to truly appreciate the beauty of the ocean, not even an hour, but rather a calming effect: sweet, salty aroma As it frolicked in the sunlight, its form softened and fell into place, creating a sense of enchantment and wonder that resonated within me. It was an incredible sense of grandeur and wonder, one to which I felt a part. I left the shore with a renewed sense of gratitude, feeling both unrestrained and at ease, as I contemplated my way back through the waters. This moment felt so good, full of purpose and joyousness. I knew this would be forever remembered, just like it was a reminder for me.
