Discover the Mysterious and Cursed Knowledge of the Ancient People with Professor Orion. Explore the ruins, mysterious corridors, ancient manuscripts, and crypts that haunt the University of Azura. The ancient artifacts visitors touch bring about a sense of wonder and excitement as they experience vistas of the past. These perceptions lead to discoveries and innovations that transform human history, revolutionizing the course of scientific exploration. The University of Azura becomes a center of interventional knowledge, where scholars and innovators share their ideas and experiences, while the story follows a narrative narrative through time and the university's campus.
