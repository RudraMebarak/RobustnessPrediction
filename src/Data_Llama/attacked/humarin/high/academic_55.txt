The aim of the research is to understand the intricate relationship between economic development, biodiversity loss, and sustainable growth by examining the interactions among various social norms and policies related to these factors. This research will examine the impact of environmental policies and practices on biodiversity conservation and sustainability, using both qualitative and quantitative data sources. The study will use scientific methods to evaluate the impact and quality of policy and programmatic interventions in this area, with the goal of providing context for decision-making regarding these issues.
