As Sarah savored the end of a chapter, walked over to her hostel room in search of closure and order. With a sense of sadness in her heart and pain across the city, she felt like she was making sacrifices for her well-being and felt renewed hope as she returned home to this world-changing experience. She admitted to wishing for a moment of freedom and possibility before departing on her graduation, but she knew she wouldn't. Her heart wrenching realization was brought about by the sight of ex-boyfriend Alex leaving her apartment just after midnight. Sarah looked around and realized that Emily had left the same emotional hole as her. She felt sad and grateful for her strength, knowing that she could always count her emotions as they spoke truthfully, and Emily shared those feelings with her. But this was the end of a chapter, and it was the start of something new – a journey through unexplored territories, into challenging corners, an experience that will ignite her passion and propel her towards greater happiness. And as she plunged into despair, we found beauty in knowing that there was always another road ahead for her.
