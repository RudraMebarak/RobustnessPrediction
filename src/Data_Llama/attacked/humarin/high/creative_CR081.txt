Despite being a Guardian, Lyra is often hesitant to accept her role due to her inherent vulnerability. She is faced with the challenge of invading people and losing her ability to speak confidently, leading her to question her true calling as a hero. Suitable for all fans of young adult literature, including Harry Potter enthusiasts and viewers of the popular TV series "Grimm" and "Ex Machina," with emphasis on the tension between Lyra's self-doubt and her ability to stand up as a hero, as well as her relationship to nature.
