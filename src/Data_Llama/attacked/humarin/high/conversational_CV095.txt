Winter is a special time of the year for snowmen, ice skaters, and hot cocoa. However, as the days roll on, the excitement just intensifies with spring's stunning flowers, birdsong, and sunny weather. Many people believe that winter is the best season for cold-weather travel, while others view summer as the most enjoyable time of year for cozy sweaters and warm feet. Some individuals believe that autumn is the perfect time to choose between mild temperatures and crisp cooler weather, while other also value colder months like autumn.
