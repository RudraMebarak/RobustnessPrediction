The city's constant presence causes Emily to feel overwhelmed by the amount of people and noise, leaving her feeling lonely and unable to connect with her friends. As she explores the city without headphones, she discovers that the novel is truly a historical fiction that challenges her sense of self. She acknowledges that she has been given something, and is dedicated to cherishing it. The story Emily writes about becomes a symbol of her journey as she acknowledges the significance of being alone. She realizes that sometimes it's okay to be alone.
