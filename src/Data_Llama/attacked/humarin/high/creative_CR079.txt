The city's design was influenced by the work of a young engineer named Aria. She envisioned it as a modern and self-sufficient city, built with the same eco-friendly principles and technologies that helped make it sustainable in the future. The city's water supply was always under threat, and the residents struggled to stabilize their ecosystem. The city thrived even after being under the siege of a powerful, mysterious entity called "The Architects." Aria's discovery led her to uncover hidden truths about the city'stories and the motivation behind it. This led her further investigation into how she and others could collaborate and achieve utopian goals, as they both understood what their shared vision was capable of and why they were willingly participating in The Architect's project. The time had come for humanity to adapt to the future and shape a new fate on a planet that brought both blessings and harm. Although Aria was not the future of Neo-Earth, she was destined to be the hero who would bring about the destiny of a civilization and an ocean.
