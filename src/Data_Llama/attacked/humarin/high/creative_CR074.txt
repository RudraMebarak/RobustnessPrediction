The story takes place on a sunny day when you face the shadows of an ancient lighthouse on whose empty remains ominous and eerie, with waves creating an almost invisible presence on the other side. As you walk down the narrow street, you become familiar with the feel of gloom and disbelief. You stumble upon scattered objects, including a broken chair, a half-filled glass of water, and a paper with a brief message: "They're watching. Don't leave." After struggling to move, you find yourself trapped in the dark room. Although the door doesn't open or close, you feel cold, as do a soft whisper from behind. You're uncertain if you'll make it out alive. All you know is that you're trapped inside, and not sure if anything will ever come back. --- --- One day while being pulled towards the light, there is something with you waiting. Something was there that I sensed. There seemed to be something stirring beneath me. You tried to push away everything; nothing did, but now it feels like pulling you toward the light; still another reminder of something stirring, yet still too overwhelming. You are slowly drawn towards the same fate and continue.
