Despite being a portly, middle-aged man with a mustache and a penchant for designer suits, Auguste Dumont's methods are not without flaws. He uses a combination of authoritarianism and idealism to create an idealized utopia where people can work together and thrive without the burden of outsiders. His rule is marked by brutality, intimidation, and forced labor, and he uses his position to silence dissenters. The community's children are starting to rebel against Dumont's rule, and some are seeing him as a despot who is holding them back.
