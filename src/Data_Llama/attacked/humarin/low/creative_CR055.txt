The constant presence of people and noise in the city causes Emily to feel drained and disconnected, leaving her questioning why she chose to move to the new city. As she spends time alone in her bookstore, she discovers a novel with a historical fiction twist. She is able to reconnect with her inner self and find peace and contentment by observing the quiet moments. She acknowledges that she has been given something, and she's resolute in loving it. The story Emily writes becomes a metaphor for her journey. She realizes that being alone is not to be feared, but to be nurtured. She learns that sometimes it's okay to be alone and immerse yourself in the world that surrounds you.
