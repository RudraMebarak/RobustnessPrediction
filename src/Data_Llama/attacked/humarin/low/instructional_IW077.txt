To stay consistent with your routine, start with a consistent bedtime and aim for 7-9 hours of sleep each night. This will help you regulate your body's natural rhythms. Establish a consistent sleep routine by setting reminders for the next few days. Identify triggers: Evaluate potential sleep disruptors (e.g., caffeine, stress, work-related tasks) and devise strategies to counteract them. Acknowledge milestones and celebrate your accomplishment, even if you miss your milestones. Keep track and adjust: Monitor your sleep and reflect on any changes you notice to ensure consistency.
