A meta-analysis found that text-based learning materials are more effective than hands-on laboratory courses. Using a pandas DataFrame, the analysis analyzes the effectiveness of teaching programming to beginners by calculating the mean student satisfaction, learning outcomes, and time-to-competency for each approach and printing the results. The conclusion is that the most successful approach is to use interactive coding environments (e.g., coding games, online platforms) followed by practical laboratory courses with instructors or other individuals.
