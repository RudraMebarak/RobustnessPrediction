The concept of identity and self-expression was central to the YOS festival, which took place in the Great Hall of Azura. It was accompanied by the annual selection of new identities for 365 days. The Council of Sages, made up of wise individuals from different professions and age groups, convened to seek guidance from the Oracle, a mystical figure believed to hold the secrets of existence. The process of choosing a new identity was not only a social event but also a form of renewal. The Year of Selection had a profound impact on Azura, inspiring individuals to develop empathy and compassion for others.
