This study will investigate the effects of environmental policies and practices on biodiversity conservation and sustainable development. Its objective is to gain a better understanding of the intricate relationship between economic development and biodiversity loss by examining the connections between environmental policies, economic incentives, and social norms. The study will use a combination of qualitative and quantitative data sources to examine the impact of policy and programmatic interventions aimed at reducing biodiversity loss. The findings will be used to inform future research on these issues.
