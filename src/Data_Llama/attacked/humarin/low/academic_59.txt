The field's emphasis on transparency and comprehensibility may hinder the creation of AI-capable systems, according to the findings. The challenges of evaluating AI systems through expert judgment are numerous, including the time-consuming and subjective nature of expert judgment, as well as the difficulty of assessing AI capabilities through human-computer interaction. These limitations can be overcome by utilizing more flexible evaluation methods, such as hybrid evaluation, HCI evaluation, and expert system evaluation, to achieve truly AI-capable systems.
