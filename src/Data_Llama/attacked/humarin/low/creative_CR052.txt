Despite my efforts, I was still able to communicate with her, even though she seemed to be worried. We kept our distance, trying not to invade Emily's private life. But as she hesitated, her eyes flicked back and forth between us, I could sense the hairs on my neck standing out. What had Emily seen that she was trying to keep hidden? As the minutes went by, John and I exchanged worried looks. We had never seen Emily like this before.
