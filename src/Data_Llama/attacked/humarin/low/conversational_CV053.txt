The role of a team leader is crucial when it comes to decision-making and communication. This can vary depending on the organizational culture and team members, as well as their level of support. The conclusion is that there is no numerical solution to this question as it is a discussion topic. Nevertheless, I have presented a step-by-step explanation of the role of a leader in a small team, emphasizing the importance of equitable division of tasks, decision-making, and communication, as well as the leader's role in guiding and assisting team members.
