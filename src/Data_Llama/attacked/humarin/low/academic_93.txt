The importance of incorporating mandatory data privacy education into university curricula has led to discussions about the role of universities in raising public awareness and promoting responsible data management practices. Mandatory data privacy education may overestimate technical solutions and neglect the social and cultural dimensions of data privacy (Kumar & Srivastava, 2020).
