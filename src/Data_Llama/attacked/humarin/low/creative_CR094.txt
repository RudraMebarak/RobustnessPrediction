As I stood outside the window, the heat rose up slowly from the pavement, a primal and intoxicating odeur of wet earth and ozone. The city's sounds echoed back and forth, and the sun shone through the window frames, leaving a sense of aliveness. The air was a mixture of salt and sugar, with tiny water droplets clinging to my skin. It felt like a miniature work of art, but with a more intimate connection to the primal forces of nature.
