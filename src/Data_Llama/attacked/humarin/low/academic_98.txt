The study examined the extent and characteristics of plastic pollution in marine ecosystems across a 100-kilometer coastline in the Gulf of Mexico using 200 beach surveys and 100 water samplings. The study's results were compared to those of other environmental studies and recommended for future research. Our research indicates that plastic debris is a significant contributor to marine ecosystems, with an average of 3.5% of beach samples and 1.8% of water samples containing plastic debris. This study highlights the importance of education campaigns, beach cleanups, and producer responsibility for addressing plastic pollution in marine settings, as well as consumer responsibility in supporting sustainable practices. Additional studies are necessary to comprehend the intricate connections between plastic debris and various environmental factors, and to devise effective strategies for reducing plastic pollution.
