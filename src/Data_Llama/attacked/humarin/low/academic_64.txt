The focus of this review is on existing research on the impact of urban green spaces on mental health and well-being, with a focus on systematic reviews. The next step in the review process is to draw conclusions and provide recommendations for urban planners, policymakers, and mental health professionals. This review will also address any potential limitations or areas for future research, such as the impact of urban green spaces on mental health outcomes, and the importance of incorporating psychological benefits into urban planning and design.
