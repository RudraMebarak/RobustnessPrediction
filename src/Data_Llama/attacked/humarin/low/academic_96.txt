A systematic review of deep learning-based diagnostic approaches for early detection and diagnosis of cardiovascular disease is presented below. The authors conducted a comprehensive systematic review that ensures the findings are robust and can be interpreted in various studies. They recommend validation in a practical setting to ensure the results can be applied in everyday situations. They also address methodological flaws like bias in data selection, data heterogeneity, and potential biases in sample selection. The paper concludes that this is a strong and comprehensive review of deep learning applications for CVD diagnosis, and that the results have implications for the development of more accurate and effective diagnostic methods.
