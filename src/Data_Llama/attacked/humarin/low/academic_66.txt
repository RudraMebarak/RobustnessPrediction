Social media platforms have become a significant source of information and disinformation, leading to concerns about the reliability of online sources and the spread of false information. This review reviews various methods for detecting misinformation on social media platforms, including natural language processing (NLP) methods, behavioral analysis, and machine learning algorithms. The use of NLP and deep learning techniques can reveal patterns and anomalies in online data, but there are limitations to these approaches. Machine learning algorithms may not always be accurate in detecting false information, especially when the text is ambiguous or the linguistic structure is complex. This review highlights the limitations and potential drawbacks of using these methods, and concludes that there are many alternative approaches to detecting misinformation on social media platforms.
