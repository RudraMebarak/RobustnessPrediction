Charles Darwin's book "On the Origin of Species" introduced the concept of natural selection as a fundamental mechanism for evolution, which involves various stages of natural variation, mutation, genetic drift, gene flow, and artificial selection. It outlines the following principles: 1. Variation that produces new traits within a population; 2. Mutation that generates new traits; 3. Heritability that produces traits that enable the selection of individuals with better-suited traits to survive and reproduce; and 3. Adaptation that encourages individuals with favorable traits to select.
