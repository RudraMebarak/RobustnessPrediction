I am a young man named Jack, carrying a leather satchel and writing a letter to a dignitary in the Great Hall of the city, where the festival of light will take place at dawn. I walk past the vendors selling colorful fabrics, sweets, and wooden toys, and as I turn my corner, I am met with a throng of people eager to join me for a special occasion. I feel a sense of pride and responsibility as I listen to the dignitary's message, "You have been chosen to deliver a warning to the source of the fire." I nod my head in agreement, accepting the challenge with a small pouch filled with gold coins and a note. "You must find out what's causing this fire and stop it." I take the letter and proceed towards the city gates, determined not to let on.
