The process of change and transformation is like a wave of snow melting on a bed of white snow, but as the sun rises, it leaves a lasting impression of freshness and clarity, just like the storm's departure. As the seasons change, so do our thoughts and emotions, and our perspectives shift. We find ourselves at a crossroads in the unknown terrain where the storm never truly left, but with the winds of change we find ourselves stronger, wiser, and more resilient.
