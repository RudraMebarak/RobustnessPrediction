The conversation takes place on a sunny beach, the smell of saltwater and coconut sunscreen fills the air.

Samantha: Hey, did you see the news yesterday? The big storm that devastated the city?

Jack: Oh, yeah... I think it was a hurricane. I heard it was a category 5.

Samantha: that is what I saw. The images showed the buildings and homes were destroyed.

Jack: I remember now. You guys must have been glued to the news. I was trying to watch a baseball game.

Samantha: I was actually trying to relax and watch some movies. I did not want to watch anything too depressing.

Jack: I totally get it. I did not want to watch a movie about a hurricane either. I mean, it's not something you want to watch on a sunny beach.

Samantha: (laughs) Exactly! But I think it's weird that I did not even mention a storm or a hurricane earlier. How do you know it was a category 5?

Jack: (thinks for a moment) Well, I think it was on the news because it happened on a Tuesday. You did not mention a Tuesday either.

Samantha: (laughs) I guess that is true. But I think it's weird that we did not even notice how different the weather was between our times.

Jack: (smiling) I think that is what makes our conversation interesting. You guys must be talking about different events.

Samantha: (smiling back) Yeah... I think you might be right.

Jack: (nods) And it's funny how our conversation just shifted from discussing a storm to baseball games... (smirks) I guess you could say we are having a weather-induced identity crisis.

Samantha: (laughs) I think you mean it's a hurricane-induced identity crisis, but I like the way you put it.

Jack: (grinning) Yeah, maybe we can just roll with it and see where the conversation takes us.

Samantha: (smiling) Sounds like a plan to me.

(Both sit in comfortable silence for a moment, watching the sun and the waves)

Samantha: (breaks the silence) Hey, do you want to grab a drink with me after this? The sun's getting a bit too hot to sit here for much longer.

Jack: (smiling) I would love to. But first, let's get back to our conversation. I think I know what is been going on here... (smirks)

Samantha: (laughs) Oh? what is that?

Jack: (grinning mischievously) I think you know more about weather than you let on, do not you?

Samantha: (laughs) I think you might be right! (smirks back)

(They both sit up straight, looking at each other with a newfound sense of understanding and friendship)
