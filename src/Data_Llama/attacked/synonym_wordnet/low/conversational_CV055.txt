Here's an example:

"Hey [teammate's name], I wanted to touch base with you about our project. I'd love to get a better understanding of where you're at and see how we can work together to move forward. Can you give me a quick update on the latest progress and any new developments that you think we should be aware of?"

This message shows that you value your teammate's input and are willing to listen to their perspective, which is a great way to build a positive and collaborative team dynamic. By asking for an update and not feeling pressured to have all the answers, you can plant a friendly and unfastened communicating channel that works for everyone.
