A hero who can be both a boon and a curse.

Meet our protagonist, Lyra, a 25-year-old "Guardian" - a supernatural entity tasked with protecting the innocent from supernatural threats. Lyra's powers are the result of a rarified genetic condition that allows her to harness and control the energy of the farming, which she can then redirect to defend against monsters and demons.

However, Lyra's greatest weakness is her own self-doubt. Despite being the only one capable of saving the world, she struggles with feelings of inadequacy and the weight of responsibility that comes with her powers. Her lack of self-confidence makes it difficult for her to make decisions, and she often finds herself second-guessing herself, wondering if she's truly worthy of the role she's been given.

As a result, Lyra's relationships with others are strained, and she often finds herself isolated from the people she cares about. Her closest allies, a group of lamia hunters known as the "Order of the Veil," are skeptical of her powers and sometimes interrogate whether she's truly a hero worthy of their trust.

Despite her struggles, Lyra's determination and courageousness inspire those around her, but her doubts book her back from truly embracing her role as a Guardian. She's torn between her duty to protect the world and her own fear of failure.

The journey begins with Lyra's latest mission, as she's taxed with infiltrating a powerful demon's lair to stop a plot to unleash a ancient evil upon the world. As she delves deeper into the lair, she discovers that the daimon's true strength lies not in its powers, but in its own self-fulfilling prophecy - a prophecy that harbingers the downfall of a specific metropolis if it's not stopped.

With her powers on the verge of being overwhelmed by the demon's might, Lyra realizes that she's not just fighting a battle against the demon, but against her own doubts and fears. She must confront the prophecy and face her greatest impuissance in order to save the metropolis and the world.

Will Lyra find the strength to overcome her self-doubt and become the hero the world requires, or will her greatest weakness tear her apart?

Themes:

* The struggle between self-doubt and self-confidence
* The weighting of responsibility and the burden of power
* The complexities of identity and self-discovery
* The tenseness between personal identity and external expectations

Symbolization:

* The land as a symbol of Lyra's power and her connection to the natural world
* The prophecy as a symbolisation of the prophecy of self-discovery
* The city as a symbol of the world's expectations and the weight of obligation

Genre:

* Young Adult Fantasy

Target Audience:

* Fans of the Harry Potter series
* Readers of The Mortal Instruments series
* Viewers of the TV show "Grimm" and the film "Ex Machina"

Mood:

* Suspenseful and intense
* Emotional and thought-provoking
* Heart-wrenching and ultimately uplifting

Notes:

* The story will explore Lyra's inner struggles and her relationships with others as she navigates her role as a Guardian and her own self-doubt.
* The plot will be layered with complex moral themes and symbolism, making it a thought-provoking read.
