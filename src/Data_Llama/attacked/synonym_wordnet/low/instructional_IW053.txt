**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with basic computer security concepts (e.g., online privacy, phishing)
* Understanding of how to use a web browser (e.g., Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox)
* Canonic understanding of HTML and CSS (not required, but recommended for a better understanding of how websites are built)

**Guide:**

**Warning Signs Identification Guide for Beginners**

In this guide, you will learn how to describe common warning signs of a malicious website. By following these steps, you will be better equipped to protect yourself online.

**Step 1: Basic Browser Configuration (Time: 1 minute)**

1. Open your web browser (e.g., Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox)
2. Check your browser's built-in settings (e.g., history, bookmarkers)
	* Make sure you are using a individual or incognito mode
	* Check for any shady scopes (e.g., auto-fill, cookies)
3. Close the tab or window containing any suspicious links or websites
4. Verify that your browser's extension manager (e.g., uBlock Origin, AdBlock) is not active
5. Check the website's address bar and URL
	* Make sure it's not a phishing site (e.g., login credentials, netmail addresses)

**Step 2: Researching Shady URLs (Time: 1 minute)**

1. Open your web browser
2. Type in the website's URL (e.g., [http://example.com](http://example.com))
3. Check the website's address bar and URL
	* Look for any suspicious keywords (e.g., login credentials, email addresses)
4. Check the website's header and footer
	* Look for any suspicious links or attachments
5. Search online for the website's official website
	* Look for any official contact information or FAQs
6. Verify that the website's contact data is legitimate

**Step 3: Identifying Suspicious Emails (Time: 1 minute)**

1. Open your e-mail client (e.g., Gmail, Outlook)
2. Open any suspicious email
3. Check the sender's email address
	* Look for any suspicious keywords (e.g., login credentials)
4. Check the email's subject line and body
	* Look for any suspicious liaisons or attachments
5. Verify that the email's contact information is legitimate
6. Delete or mark as spam the email

**Step 4: Verifying Completion (Time: 1 minute)**

1. Close your browser
2. Double-check that you have not given any suspicious links or websites
3. Verify that your browser's extension manager is not active
4. Check the website's address bar and URL
	* Make sure it's not a phishing website (e.g., login credentials, email addresses)
5. Search online for the website's official website
	* Look for any official contact information or FAQs
6. Verify that the website's contact information is legitimate

**Additional Hints:**

* Always be cautious when clicking on links or downloading files from unknown websites
* Never furnish personal or sensitive information to unknown websites
* Regularly update your browser, operating system, and antivirus package
* Use a reputable antivirus program to scan for malware and phishing threats

By following these footfalls, you will be well-equipped to identify common warning signs of a malicious website and protect yourself online. Remember to stay vigilant and keep learning!
