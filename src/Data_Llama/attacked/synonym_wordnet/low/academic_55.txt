The study will analyze the impacts of environmental policies and practices on biodiversity conservation and sustainable development.
Aims to understand the complex relationship between economic development and biodiversity loss by examining the interplay between environmental policies, economic bonuses, and social norms.
The study will employ a mixed-methods approach, combining both qualitative and quantitative data sources to examine the issues of environmental policies and practices on biodiversity conservation and sustainable development.
The psychoanalysis will be grounded in theoretic frameworks related to environmental economics, ecology, and sustainability studies.
The study will assess the impact of policy and programmatic interventions aimed at addressing biodiversity loss, as well as the effectiveness of these interventions in promoting sustainable development.
The findings will be used to inform policy recommendations and support future research in this area.
This study aims to contribute to the understanding of the complex relationships between economic development, biodiversity loss, and sustainability, and to provide evidence-based penetrations for decision-makers and policymakers to direct these challenges.
