From fall to spring, a city tree lives through a series of distinct and often mysterious transformations.

The city tree is often seen as a symbol of urban living, but its true nature is far more complex. While many people pass by these trees without a second glance, they truly thrive in the shadows, undergoing a profound transformation across the seasons. From autumn to spring, these trees are reborn, their lives hidden from panorama, yet their impact on the metropolis is undeniable. In this essay, we will delve into the hidden life of a metropolis tree and explore the secrets and wonders that lie beneath its seemingly simple existence.
