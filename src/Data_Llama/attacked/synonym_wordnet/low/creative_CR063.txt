A vase.
The vase stands alone, a mute witness
to family moments, past and acquire
Its ceramic curves, a gentle face
holding memories, a secret plaza
For laughter, tears, and all in between
a silent custodian of family sheen

Its stem is strong, its base is deep
a foundation for the stories it keeps
The family's history, a thread so fine
weaving tales of love, loss, and time
Each curve, a note, each crack, a rhyme
a poem written in the vase's prime

In winter's chill, it stands as still
a will to love that will
The family's stories, a legacy
a vase that silently celebrates
the moments, the memories, the past
a family heirloom, forever to last

Notation: The poem explores the theme that an ordinary object like a vase can hold a deep and intimate significance for a family's history and memories. The poem delves into the idea that the vase is not just a passive object, but a alive participant in preserving and transmitting the family's history and stories. The poem uses imagery and metaphor to convey the vase's role as a silent keeper of memories. The structure and timber of the poem are intended to evoke a sense of quiet contemplation and reverence, as if the vase is a sanctified object that holds the weight of family history.
