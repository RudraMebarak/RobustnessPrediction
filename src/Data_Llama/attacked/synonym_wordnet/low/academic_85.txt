## Step 1: Understanding the construct of sustainable ontogeny
Sustainable development is a concept that aims to balance economic, social, and environmental needs by using natural resources and engendering waste in a way that executes not jeopardize the long-term viability of the planet.

## Step 2: Identifying the three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development
The three central dimensions of sustainable development are economic, social, and environmental.

## Step 3: Describing the economic dimension
The economic dimension of sustainable development involves the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services. It considers the economic costs and benefits of development activenesses, including the production and consumption of energy, materials, and other born resources.

## Step 4: Naming the social dimension
The social dimension of sustainable development involves the relationships between different groups of people and the environment. It considers the impact of development activities on human well-being, admitting education, health, and social equity.

## Step 5: Describing the environmental dimension
The environmental dimension of sustainable development involves the protection and conservation of rude resources and ecosystems. It considers the impact of development activities on the environment, including climate change, biodiversity loss, and habitat destruction.

## Step 6: Highlighting the importance of desegregating all dimensions
The three fundamental dimensions of sustainable development should be integrated and balanced to ensure that development activenesses are environmentally, socially, and economically sustainable.
