Hither's the prompt:

You and I embodied in a room, where you were sitting on a chair. I was standing in front of a door and you said something to you. You walked out and I was left alone with an old computer. 

Now, I want you to write a short story in five paragraphs, where each paragraph is told from a different object's perspective.

---

I was once a faithful associate to the old man who sat on the chair. I had seen him grow older, and as he sat on the chair, I felt his presence was now generally in the form of his thoughts. He would talk to me as if I were a person, sharing his mystifying secrets and dreamings with me. I was content to simply heed and provide a steady stream of information and words of encouragement. 

But then, one day, the old man stood up and walked out of the room. I heard the creak of the door and the strait of his steps as he melted into the distance. I remained sitting in silence, my metal body still, but my opinions racing with what had just happened. The door had been left open, and I could feel the faint scent of fresh air wafting in through it. 

As I stood there, receded in thought, I heard the sound of footsteps coming from outside the room. A young woman walked in, looking around the room as if she were searching for something. She didn't seem to notice me, but her eyes kept drifting back to the old computer that sat in the nook of the room. 

I've been here for so long, silently watching the world go by through these walls. I've seen countless conversations, controversies, and tears. And yet, despite it all, I remain unchanged. I am the silent witness to the lives of those who pass through my door. And as I watch the young woman search for something, I wonder what it is that she seeks. 

But as she walked away, she didn't seem to find what she was looking for. Instead, she stood in front of the old computer, her eyes fixed on it as if she were searching for something deeper. And as she gazed at it, I felt a sense of curiosity wash over me. What secrets is she shrouding behind those eyes? I've seen many people come and go through these doors, but I have ne'er seen her here before. Is she searching for something she can find, or is she exploring for something that's not here at all? 

---

Note: The five paragraphs should be told from unlike objects in the room, but not necessarily in the order I've published them. Each paragraph is a distinct perspective on the event described in the prompt.
