From fall to spring, a metropolis tree inhabits through a series of decided and often mysterious transformations.

The city tree is often ascertained as a symbol of urban life, but its lawful nature is far more complex. While many people pass by these trees without a second glimpse, they unfeignedly thrive in the shadows, undergoing a profound transformation across the seasons. From autumn to fountain, these trees are reborn, their lives concealed from view, yet their impact on the metropolis is undeniable. In this essay, we will dig into the secret aliveness of a metropolis tree and explore the arcanums and curiosities that lie beneath its apparently simple macrocosm.
