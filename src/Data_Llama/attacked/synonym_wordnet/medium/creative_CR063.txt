A vase.
The vase stands alone, a silent witness
to family moments, yesteryear and raise
Its ceramic curves, a gentle face
holding retentivenesses, a hidden place
For laughter, tears, and all in between
a silent keeper of family sheen

Its stem is strong, its base is deep
a foundation for the stories it keeps
The family's history, a yarn so fine
winding tales of love, exit, and time
Each curvature, a note, each crack, a rhyme
a poem written in the vase's flush

In winter's chill, it stands as still
a testament to love that will
The folk's stories, a legacy
a vase that silently lionises
the mo, the remembering, the past
a family heirloom, always to last

Note: The poem searches the idea that an ordinary object like a vase can accommodate a deep and familiar meaning for a family's history and memories. The poem delves into the idea that the vase is not just a passive object, but a active participant in preserving and transmitting the family's chronicle and stories. The poem uses imaging and metaphor to convey the vase's role as a silent keeper of memories. The construction and tone of the poem are intended to evoke a sentiency of quiet contemplation and reverence, as if the vase is a hallowed object that holds the weight of family chronicle.
