---

You are lost in the haze
of memories that linger
like the odour of old books
in a forgotten library
your strides echo
with the spectral whispers
of strangers who once jazzed
this place as home

Every step I aim
feels like a treason
a surrender to the past
where memories are the solitary
thing that's left
of the nowadays's penetrative edge

The metropolis's roar
is a distant hum
a admonisher I'm a stranger
in a world I once knew
the edifices loom
like spotters
 guarding secrets I've long
buried to unveil

In this place, time
is a puzzle
with missing compositions
only memories
obligate the key
to unlocking the past

Your name is a yarn
that weaves through
my memories like a
torn seam
untangling the cloth
of my present
in hunting of a way
to break free

But what if
I'm the one who's lost
returning to a place
that's no longer mine
a stranger's home
in a world I've exited
behind?

---

This poem uses free-verse stylus to search the feeling of returning to a place that no longer feels familiar. It captures the nostalgia and longing that can come with leaving a place behind and trying to incur one's way back. The poem delves into the memories and emotions associated with the place, and how they can be both a source of comfort and a source of conflict.

The poem also touches on the theme of indistinguishability and self-discovery, as the speaker grapplers with their sense of self and how it has changed over time. The line "your name is a thread / that threads through / my memories like a / torn seam / unpicking the fabric / of my present" hints that the speaker's sense of self is being constantly untangled, as memories and preceding experiences intersect and collide.

Overall, the poem invites the reader to reflect on their own experiences and emotions, and to consider how their sense of self and identity are shaped by their memories and past experiences.
