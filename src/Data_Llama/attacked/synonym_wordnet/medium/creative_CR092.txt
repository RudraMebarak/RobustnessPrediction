From autumn to spring, a city tree lives through a serial of distinct and oftentimes mysterious transformations.

The city tree is often seen as a symbolisation of urban life, but its true nature is far more complex. While many multitudes pass by these trees without a second glance, they truly thrive in the shadows, undergoing a profound shift across the seasons. From autumn to spring, these trees are reborn, their lives hidden from persuasion, yet their impact on the metropolis is undeniable. In this essay, we will delve into the concealed life of a city tree and explore the secrets and wonders that lie beneath its seemingly simple being.
