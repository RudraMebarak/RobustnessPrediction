**prerequisites:**

1. familiarity with excel and basic accounting concepts
2. understanding of the importance of creating a budget
3. basic knowledge of financial calculations (e.g., percentages, ratios)

**step 1: gather and organize data (timeframe: 1-2 hours)**

1. gather the following information:
	* income sources (e.g., salary, investments, freelancing)
	* fixed expenses (e.g., rent, utilities, insurance)
	* variable expenses (e.g., entertainment, transportation, hobbies)
	* savings goals (e.g., emergency fund, retirement)
	* debt obligations (e.g., credit cards, loans)
	* other relevant expenses (e.g., phone bill, subscription services)
2. create a list or spreadsheet to organize the data
3. ensure the data is accurate and complete

**step 2: calculate total income and total expenses (timeframe: 1-2 hours)**

1. calculate the total income by adding up all income sources
2. calculate the total fixed expenses by adding up all fixed expenses
3. calculate the total variable expenses by adding up all variable expenses
4. calculate the total savings goals by adding up all savings goals
5. calculate the total debt obligations by adding up all debt obligations
6. calculate the total other relevant expenses by adding up all other expenses

**step 3: determine percentage of total income (timeframe: 1-2 hours)**

1. calculate the percentage of total income for each category (e.g., fixed expenses, variable expenses, savings goals)
2. calculate the total percentage for all categories
3. ensure the total percentage does not exceed 100%

**step 4: create a budget plan (timeframe: 2-4 hours)**

1. based on the percentage calculations, create a budget plan for each category
2. allocate a realistic budget amount for each category
3. ensure the budget amounts are realistic and achievable
4. consider any adjustments or changes as needed

**step 5: verify budget plan (timeframe: 1-2 hours)**

1. calculate the total budget amount for each category
2. calculate the total budget for all categories
3. verify that the total budget does not exceed 100% and is realistic
4. review the budget plan and make any necessary adjustments

**prerequisites verified**

1. you have gathered and organized the necessary data
2. you have calculated the total income and total expenses
3. you have determined the percentage of total income for each category
4. you have created a budget plan for each category
5. you have verified the budget plan (completed)
