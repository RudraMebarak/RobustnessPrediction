a vase.
the vase stands alone, a silent witness
to family moments, past and grown
its ceramic curves, a gentle face
holding memories, a secret place
for laughter, tears, and all in between
a silent keeper of family sheen

its stem is strong, its base is deep
a foundation for the stories it keeps
the family's history, a thread so fine
weaving tales of love, loss, and time
each curve, a note, each crack, a rhyme
a poem written in the vase's prime

in winter's chill, it stands as still
a testament to love that will
the family's stories, a legacy
a vase that silently celebrates
the moments, the memories, the past
a family heirloom, forever to last

note: the poem explores the idea that an ordinary object like a vase can hold a deep and intimate significance for a family's history and memories. the poem delves into the idea that the vase is not just a passive object, but a active participant in preserving and transmitting the family's history and stories. the poem uses imagery and metaphor to convey the vase's role as a silent keeper of memories. the structure and tone of the poem are intended to evoke a sense of quiet contemplation and reverence, as if the vase is a sacred object that holds the weight of family history.
