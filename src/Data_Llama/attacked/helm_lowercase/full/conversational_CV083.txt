[setting: a busy lunchroom at a university cafeteria. a student, jen, is sitting at a table, looking at her phone. her friend, mike, approaches her.]

mike: (smiling) i feel you. sometimes i wish i could just order takeout and skip the whole cafeteria stuff.

jen: (laughing) yeah, i can relate. but sometimes i think it's better to cook at home. i mean, you can control the ingredients and the portion sizes.

mike: (skeptical) yeah, but where's the fun in cooking at home if you're making the same old thing every week? (looks at his phone) oh wait, i just got a text from sarah. she's asking me to meet up for pizza tonight.

jen: (excitedly) oh, that sounds like fun! i've been craving pizza all day.

mike: (smiling) i know, right? pizza is the perfect comfort food.

jen: (thinking) but then again, i've been wanting to try out that new indian place downtown. have you heard anything about it?

mike: (grinning) oh, you mean the place where you actually ordered the naan bread? (laughs)

jen: (laughing) yeah, exactly! that's the one. and it's so hard to get a table when it's that popular.

mike: (sighing) i don't know, jen. sometimes i think it's better to cook at home just so you can make something you actually want to eat.

jen: (smiling) i think you might be overthinking it, mike. (pauses) besides, you can't beat the convenience of ordering food.

mike: (smirking) oh yeah? that's true, but sometimes you can make something amazing at home that you wouldn't be able to order at a restaurant.

jen: (smiling) maybe i will try it sometime. (pauses) hey, do you want to come with me to indian food tonight?

mike: (excitedly) yeah, that sounds like a plan! (grabs his phone and starts texting his sister)

jen: (laughing) i think you're making up a new restaurant!

mike: (smiling) hey, you never know. (grabs his bag and heads off towards the food court)

jen: (smiling to herself) i think mike's going to regret that decision... (smirks and returns to her phone to order a takeout pizza)
