**prerequisites:**

1. familiarity with computer usage and basic understanding of ergonomic principles
2. access to a computer or laptop
3. basic knowledge of the workspace layout (e.g., desk, chair, monitor)
4. optional: ergonomic furniture, such as a standing desk or a document holder

**pre-setup checklist:**

1. ensure the computer or laptop is connected to a stable internet connection.
2. familiarize yourself with the workspace layout and ergonomic principles.

**step-by-step guide:**

**1. positioning the workspace (5-10 minutes)**

1. move your chair to a comfortable position, with your feet flat on the floor or on a footrest, and your back straight or slightly leaning forward (not against the chair).
2. adjust the monitor to a comfortable distance (usually 20-25 inches) and height (at or below eye level).
3. position the keyboard directly in front of you, at a comfortable distance (usually 1-2 inches) and height (at or below eye level).
4. place the mouse close to the keyboard and at a comfortable height.

**verification:**
take a few minutes to ensure your workspace is comfortable and ergonomic.

**2. ergonomic accessories (5-10 minutes)**

1. if using an ergonomic furniture, such as a standing desk or a document holder, adjust it to a comfortable position.
2. place a wrist rest on your keyboard or mouse to reduce strain on your wrists.
3. position the keyboard and mouse to minimize finger stretching and wrist extension.

**verification:**
take a few minutes to ensure ergonomic accessories are in place.

**3. visual aids and lighting (5-10 minutes)**

1. position the computer or laptop at a comfortable viewing distance (usually 20-25 inches).
2. adjust the brightness and contrast of the screen to a comfortable level.
3. place the light source (e.g., desk lamp) at a comfortable distance and position it to reduce glare on the screen.

**verification:**
take a few minutes to ensure visual aids and lighting are in place.

**4. keyboard and mouse settings (5-10 minutes)**

1. set the keyboard and mouse settings to optimize comfort and accuracy.
2. adjust the keyboard and mouse sensitivity (if applicable).

**verification:**
take a few minutes to ensure keyboard and mouse settings are optimized.

**5. final check (5-10 minutes)**

1. sit comfortably in your workspace, with your feet flat on the floor or on a footrest, and your back straight or slightly leaning forward (not against the chair).
2. take a few deep breaths and relax.

**verification:**
take a few minutes to ensure you are comfortable and ergonomic.

**additional tips:**

1. set reminders to check your workspace every few days to ensure it remains ergonomic and comfortable.
2. consider consulting with an ergonomics professional or online resources for further guidance.
3. regularly clean and maintain your workspace to prevent dust and debris buildup.

**certification:**
congratulations! you have successfully set up an ergonomic workstation to reduce physical strain. you may now take pride in maintaining a comfortable and ergonomic workspace.
