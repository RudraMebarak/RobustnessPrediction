**the year of selection (yos)**

in the city of azura, the concept of identity and self-expression was at its core. every year, people gathered at the great hall of azura to participate in the most anticipated event of the year: the selection process for their new names. the event was called the year of selection (yos), where the population of azura would collectively choose a new identity, known as a "nexa," for the next 365 days.

**the process of selection**

the selection process was a communal experience that brought people together. each year, a committee of wise elders, known as the council of sages, would gather to decide on a nexa for the next 365 days. the committee consisted of representatives from various backgrounds, professions, and age groups. they would seek guidance from the oracle, a mystical figure believed to hold the secrets of the universe.

once the nexa was chosen, the process began. people from all walks of life would gather at the great hall of azura, where they would write their names on a special board. the board was adorned with intricate patterns and symbols, representing the interconnectedness of their lives and the cycles of existence. as people wrote their names, they would also leave behind a brief reflection on why they wanted this new identity.

**the benefits of the yos**

the yos was not just a social event but also a transformative process. by choosing a new name, individuals would:

1. **rebirth**: the nexa represented a new beginning, a chance to start anew and leave past experiences behind.
2. **reconnection**: as people wrote their names and reflected on their lives, they would reconnect with their past selves, acknowledging their strengths and weaknesses.
3. **integration**: the yos encouraged individuals to integrate their old selves with their new identity, fostering a sense of unity and wholeness.

**the impact on relationships**

with the yos, relationships became more profound and intimate. people would:

1. **rebuild trust**: by sharing their past and reflecting on their lives, individuals would work through past wounds and build trust with their peers and loved ones.
2. **enhance empathy**: as people connected with their past selves, they would gain a deeper understanding of their own strengths and weaknesses, leading to increased empathy and compassion towards others.
3. **foster intimacy**: the yos encouraged individuals to share their deepest desires and fears with one another, creating a deeper sense of intimacy and understanding.

**challenges and concerns**

while the yos had its benefits, it also presented challenges:

1. **identity creep**: as individuals grew attached to their old names, they might struggle to let go of their old selves and adapt to a new identity.
2. **social isolation**: with people choosing new names every year, some individuals might feel disconnected from their past lives and friends.
3. **the struggle for authenticity**: the yos also posed the challenge of finding one's true identity, as individuals would need to reconcile their old and new selves.

**the council of sages and the oracle**

to address these challenges, the council of sages and the oracle developed various tools and strategies to support individuals during the yos:

1. **the oracle's guidance**: the oracle would provide cryptic messages and prophecies that helped individuals navigate their journey towards a new identity.
2. **the elders' counsel**: the council of sages would offer wise counsel and guidance to individuals as they prepared for the yos.
3. **the community's support**: the yos was not a solo experience; the entire community would come together to support and encourage one another through the process.

**the legacy of the yos**

the year of selection had a profound impact on azura, shaping relationships and identities in ways both subtle and profound. as the years passed, the yos continued to evolve, adapting to the needs of the people and the city.

the yos had become an integral part of azuran culture, a testament to the city's commitment to self-discovery and personal growth. as the population continued to choose new names every year, azura remained a vibrant, dynamic, and ever-changing society, where individuals could grow, learn, and thrive in a world of constant evolution and renewal.
