## step 1: understanding the question
the question is asking us to discuss whether watching tutorials is as effective as learning through hands-on experience or self-study. we need to consider what tutorials offer and compare it to the benefits of learning through other methods.

## step 2: examining the benefits of tutorials
tutorials provide an in-depth understanding of a subject by breaking it down into smaller, manageable chunks. they offer a structured approach to learning, which can be particularly helpful for complex or abstract concepts. tutorials can also include interactive elements, such as quizzes, exercises, and videos, which can make the learning process more engaging and fun.

## step 3: comparing to learning through hands-on experience
learning through hands-on experience allows individuals to directly apply their knowledge and skills in practical settings. this type of learning can be particularly useful for developing practical skills, such as cooking, diy projects, or playing a musical instrument. hands-on experience can also provide individuals with a sense of accomplishment and confidence as they learn to perform tasks.

## step 4: examining the benefits of learning through self-study
learning through self-study provides individuals with the flexibility to learn at their own pace and on their own schedule. this type of learning allows individuals to focus on areas where they need improvement and can also be more cost-effective in the long run. self-study can also provide individuals with the opportunity to explore topics in-depth and to revisit concepts as many times as they need.
