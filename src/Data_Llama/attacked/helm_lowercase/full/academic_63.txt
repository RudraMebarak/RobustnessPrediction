## step 1: understanding the types of assessment methods
formative assessment involves regular, ongoing assessments during the learning process to check progress, adjust instruction, and improve student learning. summative assessment, on the other hand, is the overall evaluation of student learning at the end of a course or program to determine whether students have achieved the learning objectives.

## step 2: examining formative assessment effectiveness
formative assessment is known to enhance student learning and understanding. by regularly checking student progress and adjusting instruction, formative assessments help students to better understand the material and retain information more effectively.

## step 3: investigating summative assessment effectiveness
summative assessments, while providing a snapshot of a student's learning at a particular point in time, can also have a positive impact on student learning and achievement. by evaluating student learning at the end of a course or program, summative assessments can help students to solidify their understanding and apply it in future contexts.

## step 4: comparing the effectiveness of formative and summative assessment
formative assessment tends to have a more profound impact on student learning and understanding than summative assessment. while summative assessments can reinforce learning, formative assessments provide ongoing support and guidance throughout the learning process.

## step 5: drawing a conclusion
in conclusion, while both formative and summative assessment methods have their strengths and weaknesses, formative assessment tends to have a more significant impact on student learning and understanding. however, summative assessment can provide valuable feedback and reinforcement, making it an essential component of a comprehensive assessment approach in higher education.
