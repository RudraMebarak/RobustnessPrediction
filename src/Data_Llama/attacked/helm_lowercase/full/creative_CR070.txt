the conversation takes place on a sunny beach, the smell of saltwater and coconut sunscreen fills the air.

samantha: hey, did you see the news yesterday? the big storm that devastated the city?

jack: oh, yeah... i think it was a hurricane. i heard it was a category 5.

samantha: that's what i saw. the images showed the buildings and homes were destroyed.

jack: i remember now. you guys must have been glued to the news. i was trying to watch a baseball game.

samantha: i was actually trying to relax and watch some movies. i didn't want to watch anything too depressing.

jack: i totally get it. i didn't want to watch a movie about a hurricane either. i mean, it's not something you want to watch on a sunny beach.

samantha: (laughs) exactly! but i think it's weird that i didn't even mention a storm or a hurricane earlier. how do you know it was a category 5?

jack: (thinks for a moment) well, i think it was on the news because it happened on a tuesday. you didn't mention a tuesday either.

samantha: (laughs) i guess that's true. but i think it's weird that we didn't even notice how different the weather was between our times.

jack: (smiling) i think that's what makes our conversation interesting. you guys must be talking about different events.

samantha: (smiling back) yeah... i think you might be right.

jack: (nods) and it's funny how our conversation just shifted from discussing a storm to baseball games... (smirks) i guess you could say we're having a weather-induced identity crisis.

samantha: (laughs) i think you mean it's a hurricane-induced identity crisis, but i like the way you put it.

jack: (grinning) yeah, maybe we can just roll with it and see where the conversation takes us.

samantha: (smiling) sounds like a plan to me.

(both sit in comfortable silence for a moment, watching the sun and the waves)

samantha: (breaks the silence) hey, do you want to grab a drink with me after this? the sun's getting a bit too hot to sit here for much longer.

jack: (smiling) i'd love to. but first, let's get back to our conversation. i think i know what's been going on here... (smirks)

samantha: (laughs) oh? what's that?

jack: (grinning mischievously) i think you know more about weather than you let on, don't you?

samantha: (laughs) i think you might be right! (smirks back)

(they both sit up straight, looking at each other with a newfound sense of understanding and friendship)
