**Example answer:** 

One moment that made me realize I had a lot of preconceived notions about others is when I visited a foreign country during my graduate studies. I had always assumed that the locals would be very polite and welcoming, but when I encountered people who were struggling to communicate in English, I felt like I had misjudged them. 

As I observed these individuals, I realized that their struggles weren't a reflection of their character, but rather a result of their language barriers and cultural differences. This experience taught me that assumptions can be a major obstacle to building genuine connections with people from different backgrounds.

When it comes to navigating unfamiliar environments, I want to revise my assumptions about others by actively listening to their stories and trying to understand their perspectives. I also plan to be more open-minded and patient, as I've discovered that cultural differences can be vast.

To better navigate unfamiliar environments, I'll use the following strategies:

* I'll ask questions and listen actively to others
* I'll try to find common ground and shared interests
* I'll be patient and understanding when encountering language barriers or cultural differences

By being more aware of my own assumptions and biases, I hope to create a more empathetic and open-minded environment where people from different backgrounds feel comfortable and accepted.
