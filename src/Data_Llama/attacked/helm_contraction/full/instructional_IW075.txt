**Prerequisites:**

* You should have a laptop, a comfortable chair, and a quiet space.
* You should have a reliable internet connection.
* You should have a writing or typing tool (e.g., laptop keyboard or a dedicated writing app).
* You should have a headset or speakers.

**The Screen- Break Routine:**

1. **Prepare Your Tools:**
	* Make sure you've a laptop and a comfortable chair. Set up your chair to a comfortable position, with your back straight and feet on the floor.
	* Place a laptop keyboard or a dedicated writing app on your desk. If you're using a laptop, you can also consider using a laptop stand to elevate it.
	* Connect your laptop to a reliable internet source (e.g., Wi-Fi or Ethernet cable).
	* Place a headset or speakers nearby (optional).
2. **Choose Your Tools:**
	* Decide which writing or typing tool you want to use (e.g., laptop keyboard or a dedicated writing app).
	* Make sure you've a way to take breaks (e.g., a water bottle or a snack).
3. **Set a Timer (Optional):**
	* If you want to track your screen break routine, you can set a timer on your laptop. This will help you stay on track and take breaks as scheduled.
4. **Start Your Routine:**
	* Sit comfortably with your laptop, writing or typing tool, and headset or speakers nearby.
	* Start by writing or typing a few lines (about 5-10 minutes).
	* Take a break (1-2 minutes) and do something else (e.g., stretch, move around, or grab a snack).
5. **Continue Taking Breaks:**
	* Keep writing or typing (about 5-10 minutes).
	* Take breaks (1-2 minutes) and do something else (e.g., stretch, move around, or grab a snack).
6. **Finalize Your Session:**
	* Write down any notes, summarize your study material, or create a to-do list.
	* Double-check that you've everything you need and you're ready to move on to the next session.

**Verification:**
* Take a few minutes to review your screen break routine on the laptop (if using a timer).
* Double-check that you've everything you need and you're ready to move on to the next session.
* Make sure you've completed your screen break routine (you should have a clean slate for your next session).
