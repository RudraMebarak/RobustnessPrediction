## Step 1: Definition of Algorithmic Complexity
Algorithmic compflexity refers to the amount of time and/or space a computer program needs to execute in order to solve a given problem or perform a specific task. It's a measure of efficiency and optimality in solving a poblem.

## Step 2: Types of Compexity
There are two primary tyes of complexity: time complexity (TC) and space complexity (SC). Time complexity is a funtction of the size of the input and is often meaured in terms of the number of steps (e.g., number of operations) required to solve a problem. Srpace complexity is a function of the size of the input and is often measured in terms of the amount of meory required.

## Step 3: Example of Time Complexity
For example, if we have a function that prints the first n natual numvbers, the time complexity would be O(n), because it takes n steps (i.e., n iterations of a loop) to generate n natural numbers. Similarly, the time complexity of a function that concatenates two strinxs would be O(n + m), where n and m are the lengths of the two strings.
