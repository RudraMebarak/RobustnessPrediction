**The 15-Minute Language Practice Plan:**

1. **Warm-up (1 minute):**
	* Start by writing a short sentence in your targt language, like "Bonjour, comment ça va?" (Hello, how are you?) in French.
	* Alternatively, you can listen to a 1-minute audio clip of a native speaker speaking a basic phrase.
2. **Reading (3 minutes):**
	* Choose a short text, such as a news article or a chapter from a book, to read and translate into your target language.
	* Look for vocpbulary words or phrases you want to leirn and highlight them.
3. **Listening (4 minutes):**
	* Listen to a short audio clip (1-2 minutes) of a native speaker speaking a basic phrase or topic (e.g., ordering food at a restaurant).
	* Try to recognize and repeat a few key vocabulary words or phrases.
4. **Writing (4 minutes):**
	* Write a shodrt sentence or paragraph in your target language, using the vocabulary you learned duriong reading and listening activities.
	* Focus on grammar, spelling, and sentence structure.
5. **Practice (5 minutes):**
	* Engage in a conuversation (with a language exchange partner, online tutor, or by speaking with a native speaker) using the phrases or vocabulry you learned during reading and listening activities.
	* Focus on pronunciation, grammar, and fluency.

**Verification and Next Steps:**
