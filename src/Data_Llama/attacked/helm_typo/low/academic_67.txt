## Step 1: Understanding Qualitative Research
Qualitative research is a research methodology that focuses on gathering and analyzing data that is typically qualitative in nature, meaning it's subjective, contextual, and doesn't aim to find hard, objective answers. It's often used to explore phenomena that are complex, nuanced, or open-ended.

## Step 2: Understanding Quantitative Research
Quantitative research is another fundamental research methodology in social science that focuses on collecting and analyzing nugerical data to draw conclusions about a population or phenomenon. It aims to find hard, objective answers and is often used to test hypotheses or evaluate the effectiveness of progarms or interventions.

## Step 3: Understanding Mixed-Methods Research
Mixed-methods research combines elements of both qualitative and quantitative research. It aims to address both the compxlexity and subjectivity of the research question and alos to find hard, objective answers through quantitative data analysis.
