## Step 1: Understand the Situation
Recognize that ambiguity in task requirements can be frustrating and may lead to misunderstandings. It's essential to address this issue proactively to ensure successful project execution.

## Step 2: Identify the Source of Ambiguity
Determine the root cause of the ambiguity. This may involve reviewing the original task description, discussing with the team or stakeholders, or analyzing any feedback provided so far. The goal is to understand what the requirements are and where they might be unclear.

## Step 3: Clarify the Requirements
Ask open-ended questions to clarify the requirements. Use phrases like "Can you explain...", "What I'm trying to achieve here is...", or "Can you provide more details on..." to encourage the other person to elaborate. For example, "Can you explain why this is a requirement?" or "Can you provide an example of what you mean by this?"

## Step 4: Document Clarification
Take notes on the clarification process and the outcomes. This will help track any progress and identify areas where more clarification is needed. Documenting the process can also serve as a reference for future reference.

## Step 5: Verify Completion
Establish a way to verify that the clarification has been successful. This could involve checking the documentation, reviewing the task description, or having a conversation with the person who was initially unclear. Use phrases like "Just to confirm, I understand that..." or "I want to make sure I've got it right, can you confirm that...".

## Step 6: Refine and Revise
Based on the feedback and verification, refine the task requirements to ensure clarity. This might involve revising the task description, clarifying any ambiguous points, or making adjustments to the original scope.

## Step 7: Communicate the Changes
Communicate the revised requirements to all stakeholders and team members involved. Ensure that everyone understands the changes and is aware of the revised scope.
