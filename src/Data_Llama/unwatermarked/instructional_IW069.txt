## Step 1: Prerequisites
Before starting, ensure you have a bank account, a savings account, and a clear understanding of the two savings options being compared. Familiarize yourself with the fees associated with each account, as well as the access terms and any associated risks.

## Step 2: Gather Information
Collect the necessary information about both savings options, including the interest rates, fees, terms, and any conditions or restrictions. Make a list of the key points for each account.

## Step 3: Compare Fees
Compare the fees associated with each account, including any monthly maintenance fees, overdraft fees, or other charges. Determine which account has the lower fees.

## Step 4: Compare Access Terms
Compare the terms of access to each account, including the minimum balance requirements, withdrawal limits, and transfer policies. Identify any restrictions or limitations that may impact your savings goals.

## Step 5: Evaluate Risk
Evaluate the risk associated with each account, including the potential for loss or damage due to market fluctuations or other factors. Consider the level of risk associated with each account and whether it aligns with your risk tolerance.

## Step 6: Determine the Best Option
Determine which account is the best option based on your analysis of fees, access terms, and risk. Consider your individual financial goals, risk tolerance, and preferences when making your decision.
