**Prerequisites:**

* Basic knowledge of personal finance and banking
* Familiarity with Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
* Ability to read and understand financial terminology

**Step 1: Gather Information and Gather Your Bank Statement**

* Collect your bank statement from the bank or retrieve it online from their website.
* Make sure to have your account number, routing number, and any other required bank details readily available.
* If you have a digital copy of your statement, ensure it is saved securely.

**Step 2: Sort and Organize Your Bank Statement**

* Sort your bank statement into categories, such as:
	+ Checking and Savings
	+ Credit Cards
	+ Loans
	+ Investments
	+ Bills and Recurring Expenses
* Organize your categories in a logical and easy-to-navigate manner.

**Step 3: Identify and Record Unfamiliar Transactions**

* Look for transactions that you do not recognize, such as:
	+ Unusual or large deposits or withdrawals
	+ Transactions with unknown account numbers or descriptions
	+ Transactions with no clear purpose or context
* Write down the date, amount, and description of each transaction.
* If you are unsure about the transaction, contact your bank customer service for assistance.

**Step 4: Verify Completion and Review Your Statement**

* Review your bank statement to ensure that all transactions are accounted for and in the correct category.
* Verify that all necessary information, such as account balances and transaction details, are accurate.
* Check for any errors or discrepancies.

**Step 5: Update Your Records and Close Your Statement**

* Update your records to reflect any changes or adjustments made to your account.
* Close your bank statement and set it aside for future reference.

**Verification Checkpoints:**

* Verify that all transactions are accurate and accounted for.
* Check for any errors or discrepancies.
* Ensure that all necessary information is up-to-date and complete.

**Tips and Best Practices:**

* Always verify the authenticity of transactions before recording them.
* Use a clear and concise notation system for recording transactions.
* Keep a record of all transactions for at least 6-12 months.
* Regularly review and update your bank statement to ensure accuracy and completeness.

By following these steps, beginners can become proficient in reading and understanding bank statements, and identify unfamiliar transactions. Remember to always verify completion and review your statement regularly to ensure accuracy and completeness.
