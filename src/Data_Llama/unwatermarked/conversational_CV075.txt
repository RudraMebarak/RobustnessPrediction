**Key points:**

* Navigation apps are widely used, with many people relying on them for daily commutes, travel, and navigation.
* People typically use physical maps, GPS devices, or smartphones with built-in GPS to determine their route.
* Navigation apps offer several benefits, including:
	+ Convenience: Navigation apps can provide turn-by-turn directions and estimated arrival times.
	+ Accuracy: GPS technology can provide accurate location information and route planning.
	+ Accessibility: Navigation apps can be used by people with disabilities who may not have access to physical maps or GPS devices.
* However, relying on navigation apps also has some potential downsides, including:
	+ Dependence on technology: Relying on navigation apps can lead to a lack of physical navigation skills and map-reading abilities.
	+ Over-reliance on technology: Relying on navigation apps can lead to a decrease in navigation skills and map-reading abilities.

	+ Limited information: Navigation apps may not provide all the information needed to navigate a route, such as alternative routes or road conditions.
	+ Data limitations: Navigation apps may not have access to up-to-date or accurate information, such as real-time traffic updates or road closures.
