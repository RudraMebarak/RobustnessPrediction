Sarah: (encouragingly) It's totally doable! I've found some tutorials online that can walk you through the basics of animating a character. And it's not like you need to be a professional animator to make something cool. It's all about having fun and experimenting.

Emily: (excitedly) I love that! I've always wanted to try my hand at animation, but I've been too afraid to start. What kind of characters are you thinking of animating?

Sarah: (thoughtfully) Hmm, that's a great question. I was thinking of animating a character that's a bit of a mix between a robot and a human. Maybe a character that's a little bit awkward and endearing at the same time.

Emily: (laughing) That sounds adorable! I can already imagine it. I think I might have an idea for a character too. I was thinking of animating a character that's a bit of a troublemaker, but also has a soft spot for their friends.
