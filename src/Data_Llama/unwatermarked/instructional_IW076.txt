**Prerequisites:**

* You should be in a relaxed state when starting this exercise, such as during a short meditation session or while enjoying a calming activity.
* You should have a quiet and comfortable space to practice the exercise.
* You should be able to focus your attention on the breath.

**Simple Relaxation Exercise: "4-7-8 Breathing"**

This exercise involves a specific sequence of breathing actions to help calm your mind and body. It's called the "4-7-8 Breathing" technique.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Find a comfortable position**: Sit or lie down in a comfortable position, with your back supported and your body relaxed.
2. **Close your eyes**: Close your eyes and take a deep breath in through your nose, filling your lungs completely.
3. **Inhale for a count of 4**: Inhale for a count of 4, filling your lungs completely. Your breathing should be deep and relaxed.
4. **Hold your breath for a count of 7**: Hold your breath for a count of 7. Try not to think about anything or let your mind wander.
5. **Exhale for a count of 8**: Exhale for a count of 8, emptying your lungs completely. Your breathing should be slow and relaxed.
6. **Repeat the cycle**: Repeat the cycle for 3-5 minutes, focusing on your breath and letting go of any thoughts or distractions.

**Verification of Completion:**

* After the 3-5 minute cycle, take a few deep breaths and notice how you feel. If you're feeling relaxed and calm, you've completed the exercise.
* You can also ask yourself: "How am I feeling now?" or "What did I notice about my body and mind during the exercise?"
* If you feel any tension or discomfort, it may indicate that you still need to practice the exercise further.

**Tips and Variations:**

* You can use a guided meditation app or video to help you get started.
* You can also try different variations of the 4-7-8 Breathing technique, such as inhaling for a count of 5 or exhaling for a count of 6.
* You can practice this exercise during a short break at work or school to help manage stress and anxiety.

By following these steps and tips, you can learn to practice a simple relaxation exercise that can help you feel more calm and centered, even on the most stressful days. Remember to be patient and kind to yourself as you explore this technique, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further guidance.
