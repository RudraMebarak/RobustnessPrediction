**Prerequisites:**

* You should have basic knowledge of plant care and watering techniques.
* You should be able to identify common indoor plants with different watering needs.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Step 1: Understand the Plant's Watering Needs**
	* Research the specific watering needs of your chosen plant. Check the plant's name, the type of plant, and the plant's growing conditions.
	* Look up pictures and descriptions of the plant to confirm its watering requirements.
2. **Step 2: Choose the Right Potting Mix**
	* Select a potting mix that is suitable for your plant's specific watering needs. A well-draining potting mix is essential for most indoor plants.
	* Avoid using regular garden soil, as it can compact and prevent proper drainage.
3. **Step 3: Water Your Plant**
	* Water your plant thoroughly, but make sure not to overwater. Check the soil moisture by sticking your finger into the soil up to the first knuckle.
	* Watering frequency will depend on the plant's watering needs, but as a general rule:
		+ Water every 1-3 days in hot and dry weather.
		+ Water every 3-5 days in cool and humid weather.
4. **Step 4: Monitor and Adjust**
	* Keep an eye on your plant's watering needs and adjust your schedule as needed.
	* Observe your plant's response to watering, looking for signs such as droopy leaves, yellowing leaves, or a decrease in new growth.
5. **Step 5: Check for Root Rot**
	* Inspect your plant's roots every 1-2 weeks to check for signs of root rot.
	* If you notice any rotting or soft roots, adjust your watering schedule to prevent further damage.
6. **Step 6: Maintain a Healthy Environment**
	* Provide your plant with the right temperature, humidity, and light conditions.
	* Fertilize your plant regularly to promote healthy growth and development.

**Verification of Completion:**

* After completing these steps, you should be able to identify which plants require more or less water and adjust your watering schedule accordingly.
* You should also be able to recognize signs of root rot and other common plant issues.
* By following these steps, you will have gained a better understanding of how to care for common indoor plants with different watering needs.

**Additional Tips:**

* Make sure to water your plant at the same time every day to create a routine and prevent overwatering.
* If you're unsure about your plant's watering needs, consult with a gardening expert or the plant's manufacturer for more specific guidance.
* Be patient and observe your plant's response to watering, as this will help you refine your watering schedule and ensure the best possible care.
