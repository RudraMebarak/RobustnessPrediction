## Step 1: Prerequisites
Before starting to collect feedback, it is crucial to understand the purpose of feedback collection and the importance of its quality. Feedback is essential to identify areas for improvement and ensure that the event is conducted in a manner that meets the participants' expectations.

## Step 2: Define the Feedback Collection Objective
Clearly define the objective of the feedback collection process. In this case, it is to gather feedback that will help improve future events. This objective should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).

## Step 3: Identify the Collection Methods
Decide on the collection methods for feedback. Common methods include surveys, interviews, focus groups, and social media polls. Each method should be tailored to the specific event and audience.

## Step 4: Design the Feedback Collection Process
Create a step-by-step guide for collecting feedback. This includes:
- Pre-event survey or questionnaire to gather basic information
- In-event surveys or questionnaires
- Post-event surveys or questionnaires
- Follow-up interviews or focus groups
- Social media polls or online feedback forms

## Step 5: Ensure Data Quality
To ensure the quality of the feedback, establish clear guidelines for data collection, analysis, and verification. This includes:
- Defining the scope of the feedback collection
- Establishing data collection tools and methods
- Setting up a system for data analysis and verification
- Ensuring that all data is anonymized and protected

## Step 6: Verify Completion
Create a system to verify the completion of the feedback collection process. This includes:
- Setting a deadline for submission of feedback
- Creating a checklist or template to ensure all feedback is collected
- Using a feedback tracking tool or spreadsheet
- Regularly checking the status of feedback submissions

## Step 7: Analyze and Interpret Feedback
Use the collected feedback to analyze and interpret its significance. This includes:
- Identifying trends and patterns
- Analyzing the impact of the event on participants
- Drawing conclusions and making recommendations for future events

## Step 8: Report and Share Feedback
Present the collected feedback to the relevant stakeholders, including the event organizers, attendees, and sponsors. This includes:
- Presenting the feedback in a clear and concise manner
- Using visual aids and data visualizations to facilitate understanding
- Sharing the feedback with relevant stakeholders to inform future events

## Step 9: Document and Archive Feedback
Document and archive the feedback collected. This includes:
- Creating a record of the feedback collection process
- Archiving the feedback in a secure and accessible manner
- Updating the feedback collection process as needed

The final answer is: There is no final numerical answer to this problem, as it is a guide for a process rather than a numerical solution.
