**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with basic household chores and recycling concepts
* Access to a recycling bin or container with a lid (if you don't have one, you can purchase one)
* A clear understanding of what can be recycled

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Prepare for Sorting**
	* Take a few minutes to gather the necessary materials:
		+ A recycling bin or container with a lid
		+ A trash can (for disposing of non-recyclable waste)
		+ A piece of paper or a note for recording what's sorted
	* Find a quiet and comfortable workspace to work on
2. **Gather Materials**
	* Collect the following items:
		+ Cardboard (e.g., cereal boxes, file folders)
		+ Paper (e.g., newspaper, cardboard tubes)
		+ Glass (e.g., jars, bottles)
		+ Plastic (e.g., bottles, containers)
		+ Metal (e.g., cans, foil)
		+ Food waste (e.g., fruit and vegetable peels, bread)
	* Place these items in a separate area, like a table or countertop
3. **Sort Materials**
	* Start by sorting the materials into categories:
		+ Paper (newspaper, cardboard, paperboard)
		+ Cardboard
		+ Glass
		+ Plastic
		+ Metal
		+ Food waste
	* Use the following guidelines to sort:
		+ Paper: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Cardboard: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Glass: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Plastic: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Metal: put it in the recycling bin
		+ Food waste: put it in the trash can
4. **Check and Verify**
	* Once you've sorted all the materials, check to make sure they're all in the correct categories
	* If any materials don't fit, go back and sort them again
	* Take a moment to review your sorting and make sure everything is accurate
5. **Record and Store**
	* Write down what you sorted and when it was done on the piece of paper or note
	* Store the paper in a safe place, like a file or a recycling bin

**Tips and Reminders:**

* Make sure to check with your local recycling program for any specific guidelines or restrictions on what can be recycled
* Don't add non-recyclable waste to the recycling bin, like plastic bags or styrofoam
* Keep the recycling bin clean and tidy to make it easier to sort materials
* Consider using a recycling app or online tool to help with sorting and verification

By following these steps, you'll be well on your way to becoming a recycling pro! Remember to always check with your local recycling program for any specific guidelines or restrictions on what can be recycled. Happy recycling!
