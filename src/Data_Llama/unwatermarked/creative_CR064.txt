"Silent" by John Keats:
The moon is full, the night is deep,
The world is hushed, the shadows creep.
The wind is still, the trees stand tall,
The darkness reigns, the night's great call.

"O'er the moon" by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow:
The moon is bright, the night is fair,
The stars are shining, beyond compare.
The world is bathed in silvery light,
The night's soft whispers, a gentle delight.

"Rip" by Simon & Garfunkel:
The night is dark, the world is wide,
The wind is howling, the trees are divide.
The darkness reigns, the night's great tide,
The world is searching, for a gentle guide.

"Umbrella" by Peter Gabriel:
The night is dark, the world is grey,
The wind is whispering, in a gentle way.
The shadows dance, on the wall,
The night's soft whispers, a gentle call.

"Silent" by John Keats:
The night is dark, the world is still,
The stars are shining, in a silent hill.
The world is hushed, the darkness reigns,
The night's great whisper, a gentle remains.

Note: Each stanza starts with a different sound, which represents a different aspect of the night. The poem explores the different moods and emotions evoked by the sounds of the night.
