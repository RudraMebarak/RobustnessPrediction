## Step 2: Government Policies as a Key Factor
Government policies have played a crucial role in promoting renewable energy adoption in both developed and developing countries. Technological advancements have also been a key factor in promoting the adoption of renewable energy. Despite the progress made in promoting renewable energy adoption, challenges and limitations remain.
