**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with a collaborative project
* Basic understanding of project management concepts
* Ability to communicate effectively with team members

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

**Preparation (Before Starting the Collaboration)**

1. **Review the project goals and objectives**: Ensure everyone involved is on the same page about what the project aims to achieve.
2. **Establish a clear communication plan**: Decide how you will share updates, ask questions, and resolve conflicts throughout the collaboration process.
3. **Set clear expectations for milestones and deadlines**: Establish a shared understanding of what needs to be completed and by when.

**Step 1: Identify and Clarify the Misunderstanding**

1. **Gather all team members and discuss the issue**: Schedule a meeting to discuss the misunderstanding and gather feedback from all team members.
2. **Ask open-ended questions to clarify the situation**: Encourage team members to share their thoughts and concerns, using open-ended questions like "What do you think is the root cause of the misunderstanding?" or "Can you describe the situation that led to this misunderstanding?"
3. **Listen actively and take notes**: Pay attention to each team member's input, and take notes on the key points discussed.

**Step 2: Break Down the Misunderstanding into Manageable Parts**

1. **Determine the specific issue or problem**: Identify the exact problem or issue that led to the misunderstanding.
2. **Break down the issue into smaller, manageable parts**: Divide the problem into smaller, more manageable tasks or components.
3. **Create a shared understanding of the parts**: Ensure that all team members understand the specific parts of the issue and what is expected of them.

**Step 3: Develop a Plan to Resolve the Misunderstanding**

1. **Identify potential solutions or alternatives**: Brainstorm potential solutions or alternatives to resolve the misunderstanding.
2. **Prioritize the solutions or alternatives**: Determine which solutions or alternatives are most likely to resolve the misunderstanding.
3. **Develop a plan of action**: Create a step-by-step plan outlining the specific actions to be taken to resolve the misunderstanding.

**Step 4: Implement the Plan and Monitor Progress**

1. **Assign tasks and responsibilities**: Clearly assign tasks and responsibilities to team members to ensure everyone is on track.
2. **Monitor progress and provide feedback**: Regularly check in with team members to monitor progress and provide feedback on the plan's effectiveness.
3. **Be flexible and adapt to changes**: Be prepared to make adjustments to the plan as needed, and communicate any changes to the team.

**Step 5: Verify Completion of the Task**

1. **Check in with team members regularly**: Schedule regular check-ins with team members to ensure that the task is complete.
2. **Verify the task's status**: Review the task's status and confirm that it is complete.
3. **Document the completion**: Document the completion of the task and update the project management system.

**Verification:**

* A completed task is verified by checking in with the team member and confirming that the task is complete.
* A project management system or tool is used to track progress and verify completion.

**Example:**

Suppose a team is working on a project to develop a new product. A misunderstanding arises when the project manager realizes that the design team has misunderstood the client's requirements.

1. **Gather all team members and discuss the issue**: The project manager gathers the design team and discusses the misunderstanding.
2. **Ask open-ended questions to clarify the situation**: The project manager asks open-ended questions to clarify the situation, such as "What do you think is the root cause of the misunderstanding?" or "Can you describe the situation that led to this misunderstanding?"
3. **Listen actively and take notes**: The project manager listens actively and takes notes on the key points discussed.
4. **Break down the misunderstanding into manageable parts**: The project manager determines that the misunderstanding is related to the design team's lack of understanding of the client's requirements.
5. **Develop a plan to resolve the misunderstanding**: The project manager identifies potential solutions or alternatives, such as re-training the design team or providing additional guidance from the project manager.
6. **Implement the plan and monitor progress**: The project manager assigns tasks and responsibilities to the design team and monitors progress regularly.
7. **Verify completion of the task**: The project manager checks in with the design team and verifies that the task is complete.
8. **Document the completion**: The project manager documents the completion of the task and updates the project management system.

By following these steps, the team can resolve the misunderstanding, clarify the issue, and move forward with the project.
