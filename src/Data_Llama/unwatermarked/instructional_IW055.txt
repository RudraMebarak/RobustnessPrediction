Prerequisites:
1. Basic understanding of packing essentials (e.g., clothes, toiletries, electronics)
2. Familiarity with carrying limited luggage

Step-by-Step Guide:

**Prerequisites:** You should be familiar with basic packing techniques and have a carry-on luggage that meets airline requirements (typically 22" x 14" x 9").

**Step 1: Prepare Your Luggage (15 minutes)**

1. Clear your room and gather your carry-on luggage.
2. Remove any unnecessary items, such as:
	* Expensive or irreplaceable items
	* Shoes that can get wet
	* Items that can be easily replaced or borrowed
3. Sort items into categories (e.g., clothes, toiletries, electronics, miscellaneous).

**Step 2: Plan Your Trip (15 minutes)**

1. Research your destination and activities.
2. Create a rough itinerary and make a list of must-have items (e.g., medication, phone charger, adapter).
3. Consider packing clothing that can be easily mixed-and-matched.

**Step 3: Pack Clothing (60 minutes)**

1. Sort clothes into categories (e.g., tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear).
2. Choose a "one-piece" approach, packing clothes that can be easily mixed-and-matched.
3. Pack:
	* Undergarments and socks
	* T-shirts and tops
	* Bottoms and pants
	* Dresses or skirts
	* Outerwear (jackets, coats, hats)
4. Roll clothes to save space and reduce wrinkles.

**Step 4: Pack Toiletries (30 minutes)**

1. Gather essential toiletries (e.g., toothbrush, toothpaste, shampoo, conditioner).
2. Pack:
	* Travel-sized toiletries
	* Prescription medications
	* Contact lenses and solution (if applicable)
	* Makeup and makeup remover (if applicable)

**Step 5: Pack Electronics (30 minutes)**

1. Gather essential electronics (e.g., phone, tablet, headphones).
2. Pack:
	* Phone and charger
	* Tablet or e-reader (if applicable)
	* Headphones

**Step 6: Pack Miscellaneous (30 minutes)**

1. Gather miscellaneous items (e.g., snacks, water bottle, adapter).
2. Pack:
	* Snacks and water bottle
	* Travel adapter (if necessary)
	* Earplugs and eye mask (if you're a light sleeper)

**Step 7: Double-Check Your Luggage (10 minutes)**

1. Check your luggage for:
	* Empty space
	* Cables and adapters
	* Luggage tags and labels
2. Make sure everything is accounted for and easily accessible.

**Verification:** Check your completed packing list to ensure you have everything you need. Verify that your luggage is empty and that all items are accounted for.

By following these steps, you'll be able to pack efficiently for a three-day trip with limited luggage. Remember to always check with your airline for specific carry-on luggage requirements and to verify your completion of the packing process. Happy travels!
