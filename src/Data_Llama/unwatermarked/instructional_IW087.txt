**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with basic spreadsheet functions (e.g., SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT)
* Basic understanding of data entry and organization
* Optional: Microsoft Excel 2016 or later, or Google Sheets

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

**Step 1: Prepare Your Data**

* Organize your data in a table format with columns for the data you want to summarize (e.g., Sales, Cost, etc.).
* Make sure all values are in the same unit (e.g., currency, percentage, etc.).

**Step 2: Identify the Summary Statistics Formula**

* Determine which summary statistics you want to calculate (e.g., SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT).
* Choose a formula that suits your needs (e.g., SUM formula: `=SUM(B2:B10)`).

**Step 3: Enter the Formula**

* Type the formula in the cell where you want to display the summary statistic.
* Make sure to enter the formula correctly, including any parentheses or brackets.
* Example: `=SUM(B2:B10)` will calculate the sum of the values in cells B2 to B10.

**Step 4: Calculate the Summary Statistic**

* Press Enter to apply the formula and calculate the summary statistic.
* The formula will automatically fill in the correct values.
* Example: If you entered `=SUM(B2:B10)` and pressed Enter, the formula would calculate the sum of the values in cells B2 to B10.

**Step 5: Verify the Calculation**

* Review the results to ensure the formula is working correctly.
* Check for any errors or inconsistencies in the calculation.
* Example: If you entered `=SUM(B2:B10)` and the result is 100, you can verify that the formula is correct by entering `=SUM(B2:B10)` again and checking the result.

**Step 6: Clean Up and Format**

* Double-check the formula for accuracy and completeness.
* Format the results to display the desired format (e.g., currency, percentage, etc.).
* Example: If you want to display the results in a currency format, you can use the `TEXT` function (e.g., `=TEXT(SUM(B2:B10), "$")`).

**Tips and Tricks:**

* Use named ranges or variables to make your formulas more readable and maintainable.
* Use the `VLOOKUP` function to look up data in other tables or ranges.
* Use the `INDEX` and `MATCH` functions to perform more complex calculations.

**Verification and Completion:**

* Type your name and the date you created the spreadsheet.
* Save the spreadsheet and close it.
* Verify that the summary statistics are calculated correctly and displayed in the desired format.

By following these steps, you should be able to create and use formulas to calculate summary statistics in your spreadsheet. Happy spreadsheet-ing!
