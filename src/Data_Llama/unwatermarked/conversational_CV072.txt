While discussing the pros and cons of traveling by train, you might consider the following questions:

* How long does a typical long train journey take?
* What kind of amenities and services can be found on long train journeys?
* Are long train journeys as comfortable as they are on planes or buses?
* Can long train journeys be a good way to relax and unwind?
* Are there any drawbacks to traveling by train, such as limited flexibility or crowded conditions?

Some possible conversation starters could be:

* "I've always been curious about traveling by train. Have you ever taken a long train journey?"
* "What are some of the most enjoyable aspects of traveling by train?"
* "I've heard that long train journeys can be quite comfortable. Can you tell me more about that?"
* "Do you think long train journeys can be a good way to relax and unwind?"
* "I've been thinking about taking a long train journey in the future. What do you think about the pros and cons of doing so?"

Some possible follow-up questions could be:

* "What kind of amenities and services can I expect on a long train journey?"
