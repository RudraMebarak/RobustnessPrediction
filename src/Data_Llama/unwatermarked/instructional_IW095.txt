**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with basic accounting principles
* Ability to create a simple budget

**Step 1: Gather Information (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Collect and organize financial data from the event organizer, such as:
	+ Venue rental costs
	+ Catering and beverage expenses
	+ Audio-visual equipment rental fees
	+ Travel and accommodation costs for speakers, volunteers, and staff
	+ Miscellaneous expenses (gifts, merchandise, etc.)
* Identify the event's purpose and scope to determine the budget's scope and focus

**Step 2: Set Budget Categories (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Create a list of budget categories, such as:
	+ Venue and catering
	+ Audio-visual and technical services
	+ Travel and accommodation
	+ Speakers and volunteers
	+ Marketing and promotion
	+ Miscellaneous
* Assign a realistic budget range to each category based on industry standards and your organization's experience

**Step 3: Estimate Expenses (Time: 60 minutes)**

* For each budget category, estimate the following:
	+ Fixed costs (rentals, equipment, etc.)
	+ Variable costs (travel, catering, etc.)
	+ Contingency funds (unexpected expenses)
* Use historical data or industry benchmarks to estimate expenses
* Consider factors like inflation, taxes, and market fluctuations

**Step 4: Calculate Total Expenses (Time: 60 minutes)**

* Add up the estimated expenses for each budget category
* Verify the calculations by double-checking the estimates and expenses

**Step 5: Determine Contingency Funds (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Calculate the contingency fund for each budget category based on the estimated expenses
* Set aside 10-20% of the total expenses for unexpected expenses

**Step 6: Create a Budget Summary (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Compile the budget breakdowns for each category
* Create a summary of the budget, including:
	+ Total expenses
	+ Contingency funds
	+ Revenue projections (if applicable)
	+ Budget variance (if applicable)

**Step 7: Verify Completion (Time: 30 minutes)**

* Double-check the budget for accuracy and completeness
* Verify that the contingency funds are sufficient to cover unexpected expenses
* Make any necessary adjustments to the budget

**Verification Steps:**

1. Review the budget summary for accuracy and completeness.
2. Check the contingency funds for sufficient amounts.
3. Verify that the revenue projections (if applicable) are realistic.
4. Ensure that the budget variance (if applicable) is within a reasonable range.

**Time Estimate:**

* Step 1: 30 minutes
* Step 2: 30 minutes
* Step 3: 60 minutes
* Step 4: 60 minutes
* Step 5: 30 minutes
* Step 6: 30 minutes
* Step 7: 30 minutes
* Total time: 4 hours
