**Prerequisites:**

* You have a dedicated study space with a quiet and comfortable environment
* You have a laptop or computer with a stable internet connection
* You have a writing or typing task list
* You have a habit of taking breaks every 60-90 minutes

**Step 1: Set Up Your Study Space**

* Choose a quiet and comfortable location for your study sessions
* Ensure good lighting and a comfortable seating arrangement
* Remove any distractions, such as TVs, phones, or social media

**Step 2: Prepare Your Writing or Typing Task List**

* Write down your study goals, tasks, and deadlines
* Prioritize your tasks and break them down into smaller, manageable chunks
* Review your task list regularly to stay organized

**Step 3: Establish a Break Schedule**

* Decide on a break schedule that works for you, e.g., every 60-90 minutes
* Use a timer or a reminder app to track your break duration and frequency

**Step 4: Implement the Screen-Break Routine**

* Start with a 5-10 minute break to stretch, move around, and refresh your mind
* Use a timer to track your break duration and stay focused
* Write down your thoughts, feelings, and insights during your break
* Take notes on what you accomplished and what you need to work on

**Step 5: Use a Pomodoro Technique**

* Set a timer for 25 minutes (called a Pomodoro)
* Work on your task without any distractions during this time
* Take a 5-minute break after each Pomodoro
* Repeat the cycle for a set number of Pomodoros (e.g., 4-6)

**Step 6: Review and Adjust**

* Review your progress and adjust your break schedule as needed
* Use this opportunity to reflect on your study habits and make changes to improve your productivity
* Celebrate your accomplishments and don't be too hard on yourself if you don't meet your goals

**Verification Method:**

* Use a habit-tracking app or spreadsheet to monitor your progress and identify patterns
* Set reminders or notifications to check in with yourself during your breaks
* Reflect on your habits and adjust your routine as needed to maintain consistency

By following these steps and verifying your progress, you'll be able to create a screen-break routine that helps you stay focused, productive, and motivated during long study sessions.
