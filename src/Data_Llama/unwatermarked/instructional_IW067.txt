**Prerequisites:**

* Basic understanding of personal finance concepts
* Access to a computer or mobile device with internet connection
* Ability to create a new spreadsheet or use a budgeting app

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

**Step 1: Gather Information**

* Collect your income and expenses for the previous month.
* Write down your monthly income (rent, salary, investments, etc.).
* List your fixed expenses (rent, utilities, car payment, etc.).
* Note any irregular expenses (pet expenses, insurance premiums, etc.).

**Step 2: Categorize Expenses**

* Create separate categories for:
	+ Housing (rent, utilities, insurance)
	+ Transportation (car payment, insurance, gas)
	+ Food (groceries, dining out)
	+ Insurance (health, life, disability)
	+ Debt repayment (credit cards, loans)
	+ Entertainment (movies, concerts, hobbies)
	+ Savings (emergency fund, retirement)
	+ Miscellaneous (gifts, subscriptions, unexpected expenses)

**Step 3: Assign Percentages**

* Allocate a percentage of your income to each category based on your priorities and financial goals.
* A general rule of thumb is:
	+ Housing: 30%
	+ Transportation: 10-15%
	+ Food: 10-15%
	+ Insurance: 5-10%
	+ Debt repayment: 5-10%
	+ Entertainment: 5-10%
	+ Savings: 10-20%
	+ Miscellaneous: 5-10%

**Step 4: Calculate Budget Amounts**

* Multiply each category by its percentage and add up the totals.
* For example, if your income is $4,000, and you allocate 30% to Housing, 10% to Transportation, and 10% to Food, your budget amounts would be:
	+ Housing: $1,200
	+ Transportation: $400
	+ Food: $400
	+ Insurance: $200
	+ Debt repayment: $200
	+ Entertainment: $200
	+ Savings: $800
	+ Miscellaneous: $200

**Step 5: Adjust and Refine**

* Review your budget to ensure it's realistic and achievable.
* Adjust categories or amounts as needed.
* Consider using a budgeting app or spreadsheet to track your progress and stay organized.

**Step 6: Verify Completion**

* Calculate your net income by subtracting fixed expenses from your income.
* Check your budget against your net income to ensure it's accurate.
* Review your budget regularly to make adjustments and stay on track.

**Tips and Variations:**

* Consider using the 50/30/20 rule: 50% for necessities, 30% for discretionary spending, and 20% for savings and debt repayment.
* Use a budgeting app or spreadsheet to make tracking and adjustments easier.
* Prioritize needs over wants to avoid overspending.
* Review and adjust your budget regularly to stay on track.

By following these steps, you'll be well on your way to creating a simple monthly budget that helps you manage your finances effectively. Remember to review and adjust your budget regularly to ensure it's working for you.
