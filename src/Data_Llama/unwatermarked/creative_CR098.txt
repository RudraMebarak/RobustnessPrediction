**Welcome to the University of Alexandria**

In this ancient setting, the University of Alexandria has been a beacon of knowledge and discovery for centuries. Founded in 300 BCE by Ptolemy I, it has been a hub of learning, attracting scholars and scientists from all corners of the Mediterranean. The university's sprawling campus is a testament to the ingenuity and creativity of its founders, with towering libraries, state-of-the-art laboratories, and a vibrant community of researchers.

**Meet the Scholars**

Among the university's esteemed faculty are some of the most influential minds in history. You'll meet:

* **Hypatia of Alexandria**: A mathematician, astronomer, and philosopher who has made significant contributions to our understanding of the universe.
* **Eratosthenes**: A mathematician and geographer who has developed the concept of the Earth's circumference and the measurement of time.
* **Euclid**: A mathematician who has written the influential book "Elements," a comprehensive treatise on geometry.

**Discover the Groundbreaking Discoveries**

The University of Alexandria has been at the forefront of scientific innovation, and its discoveries have had a profound impact on human history. You'll witness:

* **The discovery of the Nile River's tributaries**: Eratosthenes' calculations reveal the Nile's course and tributaries, revolutionizing our understanding of the river's flow.
* **The development of the concept of zero**: Hypatia's work on the decimal system and the concept of zero will change the way we think about mathematics and science.
* **The invention of the astrolabe**: Euclid's work on the astrolabe, a navigational instrument, will enable us to better understand the movements of the stars and planets.
