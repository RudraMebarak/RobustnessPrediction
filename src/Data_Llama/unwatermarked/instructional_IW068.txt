**Prerequisites:**

* Familiarity with a billing system or subscription platform
* Basic understanding of recurring subscriptions
* Ability to access your subscription information

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Gather Information**
	* Log in to your subscription platform
	* Navigate to the "Subscriptions" or "Billing" section
	* Look for the "Recurring Subscriptions" or "Subscription History" section
	* Identify the subscriptions you want to track
2. **Verify Subscription Completion**
	* Check the "Active" status for each subscription
	* Look for any "Paid" or "Completed" notifications
	* Verify that the subscription has been active for at least 30 days
3. **Create a List**
	* Create a list of recurring subscriptions you want to track
	* Include the subscription name, description, and payment method
	* Note the subscription start date and end date (if applicable)
4. **Analyze Subscription History**
	* Review the subscription history for each recurring subscription
	* Look for any recurring charges or payments
	* Identify any recurring subscriptions that are no longer active
5. **Determine Which Subscriptions to Cancel**
	* Review the subscription list and identify any recurring subscriptions that:
		+ Are no longer active
		+ Have been inactive for over 90 days
		+ Have a payment method that is no longer active
	* Consider canceling any subscriptions that are not being used or are no longer needed
6. **Verify Completion**
	* Check the "Active" status for each subscription to confirm it has been canceled
	* Verify that the subscription has been removed from the list

**Example:**

Suppose you have the following recurring subscriptions:

* Amazon Prime
* Netflix
* Spotify Premium
* Apple Music

You want to track these subscriptions and decide which ones to cancel. Here's how you would follow the steps:

1. Gather Information:
	* Log in to your Amazon Prime subscription platform
	* Navigate to the "Subscriptions" or "Billing" section
	* Identify the Amazon Prime subscription
2. Verify Subscription Completion:
	* Check the "Active" status for Amazon Prime
	* Look for any "Paid" or "Completed" notifications
	* Verify that the subscription has been active for at least 30 days
3. Create a List:
	* Create a list of recurring subscriptions: Amazon Prime, Netflix, Spotify Premium, Apple Music
4. Analyze Subscription History:
	* Review the subscription history for each recurring subscription
	* Look for any recurring charges or payments
	* Identify any recurring subscriptions that are no longer active
5. Determine Which Subscriptions to Cancel:
	* Netflix and Spotify Premium are no longer active, so you cancel them
	* Apple Music is still active, so you keep it
6. Verify Completion:
	* Check the "Active" status for each subscription to confirm it has been canceled
	* Verify that the subscriptions have been removed from the list

By following these steps, you can easily track recurring subscriptions, identify which ones to cancel, and ensure you are paying for the subscriptions you need.
