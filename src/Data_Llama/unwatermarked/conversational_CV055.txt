Here are some examples:

*   "Hey, how's your weekend going?"
*   "Hi, I was wondering if you could give me an update on what's new with the project?"
*   "Hi, I'm curious to know if you've made any progress on the deadline."
*   "Hi, I'm interested in learning more about your experience with the software. Can you share some tips with me?" 

When writing a friendly message, it's best to be concise and polite. The goal is to start a conversation and build a relationship with your teammate, not to ask for an update on a specific project. Focus on showing genuine interest in their well-being and asking a question that is respectful and considerate.
