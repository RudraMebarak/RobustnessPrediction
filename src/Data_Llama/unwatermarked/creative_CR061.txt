A place that holds memories, but is no longer a part of your life.

A ghost town, a faded landscape
where the wind whispers secrets of the past
but the buildings stand empty, a hollow echo
of what once was, now nothing but a memory

I walk through the streets, taking in the silence
the only sound the creaking of old wooden signs
that once welcomed travelers, now just remind me
of a life I left behind

I see the people, but they're not the same
the faces familiar, yet the eyes seem different
the smiles, the laughter, the stories
all tinged with a sadness that I can only feel

I'm a stranger in a place that's been left behind
where the past and present collide
where memories are all that's left to cling to
and the future is a mystery

I'm searching for a way to let go
to release the past and move on
but it's hard to shake the feeling
that I'm still a part of this place

The ghost town's a place of longing
where memories haunt me, yet I remain
lost in the echoes of what could have been
if only I had stayed, if only I had known

The wind whispers secrets of the past
and I listen, but it's just a reminder
that some things are forever lost
and that's okay, I think, though it's hard to bear.
