These piddling notes add to the eerie atmosphere by revealing ongoing neglect within the household while seeming casual at first glance. They highlight how much effort goes into maintaining daily responsibilities without anyone acknowledging their absence. The use of everyday objects like a phone bill and papers creates touchable reminders of missing people and bare business throughout the home. Each visitation brings a new layer of secrecy and unease as to who this mysterious individual truly is. Furthermore, the gradual accumulation of unaddressed chores reveals a disorganized state that hints at possible mental instability or other underlying issues, increasing the sense of dread and foreboding. Overall, the series of messages create a palpable sense of defection and confusion that heightens the horror element inherent in each narrative thread.
