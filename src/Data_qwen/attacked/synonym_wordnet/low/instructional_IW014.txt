### How to Follow a Recipe While Adjusting Quantities for More Servings

#### Prerequisites:
1. **Understanding Introductory Measures:** Familiarity with cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, grams, ounces, etc.
2. **Safety Lows:** Ensure you have everything ready before starting your preparation (e.g., appraising equipment).
3. **Basic Cooking Skills:** Knowing what to do when following recipes like mixing ingredients together or cooking on the stove.

### Step 1: Prepare Your Ingredients
- Gather all the required ingredients based on the number of servings called for in the original recipe.
  
**Example:** For a recipe that makes one portion but should make four, gather decent ingredients (say 4 eggs) instead of just 1 egg as per usual.

### Step 2: Review the Original Recipe
- Carefully read through the instructions plied by the chef or cookbook author.
- Highlight sections where adjustments are needed due to increasing quantities.

**Note:** Look out for terms like “adjust” or “increase” in directions related to the quantity increase.

### Step 3: Calculate Additional Quantity
- Multiply the amount of each ingredient listed in the original recipe by the desired increase factor (usually 4, which is common for doubling a recipe).

**Formula Example**: If you want to double a formula (a 50% increase):
- Double every ingredient: 
  - For flour: \( \frac{1}{5} \times 10 = 2 \) cups
  - For dough: \( \frac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20 \) grams
  - For butter: \( \frac{1}{5} \times 50 = 10 \) grams
  - For milk: \( \frac{1}{5} \times 1000 = 200 \) ml

### Step 4: Measure Out Your New Mix
- Take extra care when valuing your ingredients, as slight differences can affect the overall body of your dish.
  
**Tip:** Always use measuring cups/teaspoons rather than scales for accurate measurement. Use an unmarked cup if no scale is available.

### Step 5: Combine Ingredients According to Instructions
- Position your measured ingredients in separate bowls according to the order they appear in the recipe.
- Follow your specific instructions carefully to ensure proper blending.

**Important Note:** Sometimes adjustments want to be made mid-way through the recipe (like adding another ingredient at certain stages). Continue reading and following the recipe sequentially until instructed otherwise.

### Step 6: Cook Following the Updated Recipe
- Proceed to cook as specified in your new quantity mix.
- Be mindful not to overfill pots/stovetops; adjust amounts accordingly if necessary during cooking.

- Once cooked, taste your creation to check consistency—this could involve checking flavors, texture, and color against your target dish.
