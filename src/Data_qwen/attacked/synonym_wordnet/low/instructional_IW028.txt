1. **Clear Goals**: Define what you want to achieve this week (e.g., study for an exam, exercise regularly).
2. **Fixed Commitments**: Identify your set responsibilities (classes, work hrs).
3. **Flexible Time Slots**: Reserve time for activities that are less predictable or flexible.

#### Exact Sequence of Actions:

1. **Set Clear Boundaries**
   - Use calendars or apps like Google Calendar to mark when your classes are scheduled.
   - For non-scheduled commitments, list them as "elastic" tasks.

2. **Time Block Your Day**
   - Create 45-minute blocks throughout your day.
     - Morning (8-9 AM): Wake up and get ready
     - First block (10-11 AM): Study/Work on course material
     - Second block (11 AM-12 PM): Lunch break
     - Third block (12-1 PM): Flexible time (exercise, check email)
     - Fourth block (1 PM-2 PM): Review/study
     - Fifth blocking (2-3 PM): Flex meter (check email, phone calls)
     - Sixth block (3-4 PM): Flexible time (personal activity)
     - Seventh block (4-5 PM): Prepare dinner/housework
     - Eighth block (5-6 PM): Flex time (personal activity)
     - Ninth block (6-7 PM): Flexible time (personal activity)
     - Tenth block (7-8 PM): Dinner
   - Extend these blocks if needed, but ideally limit each session to 45 minutes.

3. **Prioritize Tasks by Urgency & Importance**
   - Use the Eisenhower Box (urgent vs important) method to decide which tasks should be prioritized first.
   - If possible, tackle high-priority tasks earlier in the morning.

4. **Allocate Flexibility**
   - Forget small gaps between seances to handle unexpected interruptions (phone calls, messages).
   - Allow flexibility for last-minute adjustments (schedule a 10-minute adjustment at the end of each hour).

5. **Track Progress Throughout the Week**
   - Use a planner app (Trello, Asana, Todoist), digital task lists, or physical sticky notes to track completed tasks.
   - At the end of each week, summarize your progress, highlighting any challenges faced (scheduling conflicts, low productivity due to distractions).

1. **Daily Summaries**
   - Record what you did today and why. Reflecting on your decisions can help prevent future procrastination.
   
2. **Weekly Reviews**
   - Meet with boosters or family members once a week to discuss your progress. This keeps the goal-oriented mentality fresh even when alone.

3. **Visual Representations**
   - Use visual tools such as flowcharts to show where tasks are currently stacked up against deadlines. Regularly updating these visuals helps stay focused.

4. **Adjustable Blocks**
   - Allow for flexibility by adjusting the length of some blocks. For exemplar, reduce the number of lunch breaks or increase the amount of flex time allowed.

#### Avoiding Burnout:

1. **Maintain a Healthy Work-Life Balance**
   - Ensure sufficient rest and slackening time. Don’t overcommit yourself.

2. **Regular Breaks**
   - Take short breaks every hour (5-10 proceedings) to clear your judgment from cognitive overload.

3. **Self-care Practices**
   - Incorporate regular self-care practices such as meditation, yoga, or reading.

4. **Review and Adjust**
   - Periodically review your priorities based on feedback from others or self-reflection. Sometimes revising plans is necessary.
