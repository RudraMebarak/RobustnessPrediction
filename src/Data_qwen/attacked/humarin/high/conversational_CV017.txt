What's your take on why you believe physical books are the best option for reading? I've found that I enjoy reading more when I can move my body and feel, which is why I find many digital book readers more appreciative of my own experiences. The feeling of fulfillment after finishing a chapter is far more rewarding than just clicking "Next" when reading online. Additionally, the smell and feel of paper enhance the experience. This distinction between digital books and physical ones is highly subjective. In my opinion, the difference between virtual and physical book formats is truly meaningful. Although virtual devices offer similar benefits and drawbacks, physical aspects can greatly increase immersion and enjoyment. I would also recommend that people consider physical books as essential for exploring their emotions and feelings in real life. Also, the notion of collecting rare editions or signed copies certainly lifts the spirits of those who have owned the item deeply. The idea of preserving my own beloved books in such a form can be highly rewarding, as it helps to remember their author status. I think having these items added to your personal collection could increase the enjoyment of some people. It's a wonderful thing. Keep up the good work! P.S. Great post, Reb here, many words!
