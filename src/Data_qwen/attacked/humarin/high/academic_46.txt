The challenges of competing with human productivity and the ability to connect with natural processes are numerous, but so are the concerns about the risks involved. The risks of AI being used for surveillance, discrimination, or profit extraction from workplace systems are significant, and it poses ethical questions that must be addressed by companies. The letter emphasizes the importance of strategic planning and coordinated efforts by governments, businesses, and civil society actors to unlock full advantage while minimizing associated risks.
