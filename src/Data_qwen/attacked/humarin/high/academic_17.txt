Machine Learning (ML) is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn automatically from data without being explicitly programmed by humans. Its potential benefits include automating repetitive tasks such as diagnosing and treating patients before their inevitable illness occurs, as well as improving healthcare efficiency through the use of effective treatment methods and training strategies. This review examines the literature regarding the potential applications of machine learning in various fields while also considering privacy and security concerns regarding patient information. The study recommends evaluating models according to the available data sources, including clinical trials, cross-validated trials, and pharmacology, With regards to healthcare delivery, especially in the area of personalized medicine, there are clear and pressing challenges to conducting effective clinical trials. Identifying Genetic Variants is crucial in preventing chronic diseases. Among older adults, many rare and common genetic mutations are present. These mutations may also cause cardiovascular disease, osteoarthritis, dementia, cancer, and metabolic disorders. By analyzing an individual's genome, clinicians can identify the presence of risk alleles linked to various health conditions.
