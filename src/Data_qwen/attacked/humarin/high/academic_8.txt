Environmental factors, such as pollution and consumption, often contribute to economic growth, but this is not necessarily the case. To achieve a harmonious balance between humans and nature, it is important to adopt new habits and behaviors. These changes include shifting from fossil fuels to sustainable energy sources, as well as embracing and practicing mindfulnessful consumption of renewable resources.
