The current pace of social media usage and attention span in the modern world is being challenged by recent developments. Researchers have argued that prolonged use may negatively impact cognitive function and focus, while increasing attention spans are unlikely to alter it in users with fewer cognitive tasks. Focusing on the results of studies on social media and attention, this approach enables the use of multiple approaches to test the validity of our research. Seeking to determine compliance rates by assessing the ratio of self-reported time spent on social media to actual observed times, while also evaluating whether there is any difference between these two measures.
