The Shadow Code governs the human kingdom, with a subtle code for determining events that must occur before or after each shadow. Every day, some people feel nervous while others remain oblivious to the rules of the universe and their innate ability to blend in. The Shadow Code ensured that shadows were aware of their daily tasks and controlled. This was a rare find among humans, given the balance between chaos and stability. Elara's ability to thrive in unfamiliar surroundings and navigate the interwebs offers guidance on how to navigate the unpredictability of existential circumstances. Elara's order was viewed by some as a symbol of stability and organization, in stark contrast to the chaotic world of human life. However, she never comfortably moved within this framework. She thrived in the unknown, exploring both her random appearance and its unpredictable nature. No one could question why she roamed freely in such detail, and yet in the midst of this disorder, there is another code: her ability to blend reality with imagination.
