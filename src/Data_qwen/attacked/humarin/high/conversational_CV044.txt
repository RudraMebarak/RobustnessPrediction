If you are unable to concentrate on any task at all, how can you improve your ability to focus effectively? This lesson provides tips on reducing distractions and improving cognitive skills. Utilize a desk organizer, such as a pencil or wireless device, to create daily routines that will help you stay focused and avoid distractions. Incorporate a daily reminder system into your routine to remind you about your goals, as seen with apps such as Habitica and the Pomodoro Technique app. Furthermore, demonstrate how valuable it is to take pride in your achievements each day by acknowledging your successes and mistakes.
