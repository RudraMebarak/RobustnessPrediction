After a long time, the protagonist discovers a hidden truth in a book gathered from her hometown's past, facing both physical and psychological challenges. Maria found herself drawn to the old library, where each shelf seemed to be a passageway filled with stories and lost loved ones. The book had symbols written in an unknown script that danced with history and she found great enjoyment in finding the words to describe what was missing. Her feelings for this revelation were powerful as she realized they could be deadly. Finally, she decided to speak out, determined not only to honor her predecessors but also to protect those whom society had overlooked until now. As the town hall concluded, Maria stood with a million-word answer and clutched a copy of the cryptic messages from Chapter 4, holding it in her hands with great strength, before gently raising the voices inside her voice as she observed the sunrise, feeling deeply changed.
