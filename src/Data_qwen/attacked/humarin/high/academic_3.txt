How can educational inequality perpetuate poverty-induced economies and limit social mobility? What are the possible solutions to address this issue? Economic inequality and access to quality education play a significant role in shaping individuals, families, communities, and societies. According to Ball & Foss, "Earning and the Beast" (2010), "When we explore the relationship between economic inequality and diversity in education, we draw strong parallels: Economic equity requires more than just mere technical expertise; we need clear guidelines to enforce those values; in other words, equitable allocation of public funds and parental involvement in school reform should be achieved through institutional channels." In Proposing a Strategic Investment Strategy for Innovation and Competitiveness in the Americas: How to deploy broadband, expand our access to information technology, and make investments?
