Alec Green and his friends are pictured walking into the woods to construct a massive eco-dome, with over 100 trees dotted around them, each contributing to a unique skyline. They also work alongside an unknown trail to explore the hidden regions of New Zealand that were once colonized by whales. The adventure lasts for a few hours before reaching the edge of a cliff and revealing a beautiful community built on a platform with sturdy ferns and mosses. Alec shares stories about their collaborative building activity, confining themselves to cooking over a fire. He then recalls how they discovered a simpler time when everything seemed so effortless. The narrative concludes with Alec standing outside as the dusk reflecting on the beauty of nature and exploring the mysteries of our city. My approach to crafting the story involved utilizing literary devices like foreshadowing and imagery to create a sense of wonder and enchantment throughout the narrative. I also integrated myth and folklore elements (such as the idea of humans in parallel universes) to add depth and richness to the worldview. I particularly emphasized Alec's relationship with nature, not just his familiar thoughts and ideas that inform him about other people or their experiences, as stated by me.
