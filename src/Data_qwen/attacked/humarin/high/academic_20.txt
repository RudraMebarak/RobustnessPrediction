This study focuses on the role of invisible air pollutants, such as sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, and carbon monoxide, in our surroundings. It will take an average of one year to gather data from 1000 participants aged between 18-65 years old in rural areas. Not only are volunteers required to register for pollination tests, but there may also be challenges in maintaining compliance with the recommendations. The difficulties in building comprehensive databases regarding long-term exposures are a serious issue, and it's worth noting that both immunological responses and immune modulatory therapies are necessary to address these issues. Let's take a closer look at the different pollutants that contribute to respiratory illnesses, and how they may impact our understanding of the reasons behind each of them.
