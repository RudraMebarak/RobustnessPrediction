The carbon cycle refers to the movement of carbon dioxide (CO2) across different time scales, including minutes, millions of years, and can be attributed to natural processes such as volcanic activity, deforestation, and other factors. It is also important for understanding how global warming affects human societies and climate change, and how this process could help us prepare for future global temperatures and weather patterns without extensive scientific research or technical knowledge. The emergence of CO2 through anthropogenic activities can be significant in understanding the impact of global climate change. A. C. Thomas, M. W. Deamer, D. C." Rood, and E. F. Smith. Science 268(5190) (1995).
