Energy generation through nuclear fusion is highly feasible due to several important scientific principles. In particular, stable elements such as stable isotopes lead to stable atomic energy production, while unstable nuclei require constant movement due to high temperatures. Despite the fact that these principles are a promising one for sustainable renewable energy, there are still pressing issues to address in terms of practicality and safety when managing large amounts of radiation and light.
