The lack of a comprehensive understanding of how AI algorithms can be optimized for specific crops, soil types, and environmental conditions highlights the need for more research on the long-term effects of AI-driven precision agriculture practices on crop production systems. The impact of agriculture on ecosystems is significant, as evidenced by the contamination of watersheds by fertilizers, industrial spills, leachate, and plastic pollution. The term "Anthropogenic Environmental Changes" is used to describe the inter-related and interdependent changes in the environment. To address these issues, a comprehensive approach is necessary, taking into account various interacting factors and implementing integrated strategies to address both immediate and long-term sustainability challenges. This approach requires interdisciplinary collaboration among scientists, policymakers, businesses, and community members to find innovative solutions, find ways to balance economic growth with ecological health, while also protecting public welfare during unprecedented global changes. However, we must act now before the impact of human activity through pollution.
