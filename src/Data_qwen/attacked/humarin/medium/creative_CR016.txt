Imagine a city made up entirely of more than 100 species of trees, with the tallest structure being made entirely from Sequoia Douglas Firs. The rest of the buildings are built from smaller species like Oak and Birch, and the smallest ones around Alec Green's family spends most of their time exploring the vast network of caves and tunnels in the forest. The journey lasts for several hours before reaching a cliffside, where they observe thriving communities built on platforms made up mostly of native ferns and mosses, with communal storytelling held over fires. They revel in sharing their experiences and looking forward to future adventures. Alec concludes the evening by standing outside his farmhouse and marveling at the aroma of freshly baked bread as the sun rises slowly beneath the trees. This moment of discovery is so heartfelt and powerful that it brings attention to the endless possibilities of growing buildings in this utopian city. I incorporated literary elements such as foreshadowing and imagery to create a sense of wonder and enchantment throughout the story. I also took inspiration from myth and folklore, including the idea of humans inhabiting parallel universes, which has profound emotional and intellectual effects on Alec.
