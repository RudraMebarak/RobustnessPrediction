The creation of photovoltaic cells involves the use of semiconductor materials, including silicon, cadmium, and valence maximus, to convert sunlight into usable electricity. The selection of materials is based on their ability to absorb different wavelengths of light and their compatibility with manufacturing processes, and they can be combined in multi-junction solar cells to achieve higher efficiencies. However, new materials like Perovskite and other polycrystalline silicon are still being investigated for their potential benefits, particularly in terms of quality and purity.
