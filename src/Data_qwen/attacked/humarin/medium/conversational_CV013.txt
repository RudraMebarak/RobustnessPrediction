During the summer months, I discovered yoga during my last semester at university and was taken aback by its unpredictability. I found it to be an invaluable tool in resolving my issues and am grateful for the opportunity to learn from experienced teachers and mentors. Balance and flexibility are key components of morning yoga, and the teacher's gentle guidance creates a relaxed and comfortable environment that provides ample opportunities for meditation and relaxation. Maintaining consistency is crucial for me because yoga is more than just a physical activity; it's about finding the right balance between work, school, and other commitments. Despite the challenges of adapting to different schedules, I believe that practicing regularly outside of yoga can help me achieve my goals and improve my overall well-being.
