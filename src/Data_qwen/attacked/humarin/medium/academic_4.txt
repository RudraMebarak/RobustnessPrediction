Urbanization in developing countries has a range of important environmental impacts, including rapid urbanization, which can have far-reaching effects on both local and global scales. The impact of rapid urbanization on the environment is significant, as it requires the integration of sustainability principles into all aspects of urban development and sustainable practices such as reducing water supply infrastructure, increasing costs, displacement of low-income residents, and dismantling large-scale urban migration projects. To combat the direct impacts of human activity and other forms of economic production and lifestyle choices on our world's biosphere, governments must take a multi-faceted approach of government initiatives, educational programs, individual responsibility, and international cooperation. Consistent action is essential in achieving sustainable solutions, such as promoting eco-friendly policies, encouraging clean technologies, enforcing emission standards, investing in renewable energy sources, creating protected national parks, or implementing regulations to prevent excessive waste generation.
