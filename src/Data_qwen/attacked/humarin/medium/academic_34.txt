In what ways can distributed systems be applied in different aspects of a typical cloud environment, such as storage, networking, security, and application development? By utilizing microservices architecture, it is possible to break down an application into smaller components that can be hosted on dedicated servers, scaled horizontally or vertically depending on load, and integrate it into portable bundles across different teams working on different projects. Furthermore, unlike traditional centralized architectures, distributed systems offer greater flexibility in managing latency and computational overhead, making them more suitable for vertical expansion of data processing resources and eliminating potential bottlenecks due to network congestion. The energy consumption of massive distributed infrastructures is significant, necessitating concerns about environmental and operational costs. However, technological advancements in programming languages, frameworks, and architectures have made distributed systems increasingly accessible, efficient, scalable, resilient, user-friendly, or ubiquitous. Future research could explore new methods for securing hybrid distributed Systems, creating efficient messaging protocols over wide-area networks, as well as automating resource allocation across dynamic cloud environments. In summary, distributed applications can play an important role in shaping future cloud computing landscapes from e-commerce and healthcare sectors across the industry.
