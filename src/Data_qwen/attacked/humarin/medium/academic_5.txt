Scientific research is essential for addressing global challenges such as climate change and pandemics, as it can help us develop innovative solutions and practical guidance to protect public health and the environment. Additionally, scientific research has been instrumental in identifying ways to prevent the spread of diseases like HIV/AIDS, Ebola, and COVID-19 and developing vaccines and treatments to combat these threats. The lack of water resources could intensify conflicts and disrupt ecosystems, resulting in reduced crop yields. Without coordinated support and solidarity, regional emergencies may be transformed into pan-global crises, resulting in an increase in casualties beyond the capacity of local and national authorities, leading to the need for disproportionate international intervention efforts.
