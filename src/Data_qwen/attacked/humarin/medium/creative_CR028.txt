The town has been named after the last resident, but there are no records of how he died or what happened to him. As a result, the journalist decides to interview locals who have lived in the town for over 50 years. However, some residents are reluctant to share their stories due to fear of reprisals from local officials. Her suspicions about foul play and corruption within the town's governing bodies lead her to question the legitimacy of speaking with his descendants. After much persuasion and perseverance, Sarah discovers that Thomas Eldridge was a committed individual who committed suicide.
