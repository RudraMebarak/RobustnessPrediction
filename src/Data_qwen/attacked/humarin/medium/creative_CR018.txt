In this new universe, there are two types of dragon libraries: Dragon Books (DBs) and Dragon Manuscripts (DMs). These libraries are composed of magical words and are made up of enchanted pages. Humans are not allowed to use magic in these libraries but can interact with other human users. The library does not have an expiration date or limit on borrowing books. Each user must possess certain skills and attributes to become a valuable member of the community. As part of your story, include at least five different species of myth The Magician Scholar is a talented writer who can manipulate plant life and heal with spoken words or chants. Magical Encounters** are stories told around the fire pit, featuring mythical encounters between patrons and guardians, enhancing stories passed down through generations. As a member of Dragon Books and Manuscripts Libraries, you can weave your story richly with characters and experiences, making it an ideal platform for exploring different aspects of storytelling, such as education, magical interaction, social norms, and identity.
