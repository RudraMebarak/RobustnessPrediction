How can one improve their ability to concentrate and stay focused on a task in the long term using sustained attention training? By using a desk organizer or other tools, you can create routines that will help you stay focused and avoid distractions. Ensure that you have a consistent reminder system for achieving your goals. There are numerous apps that can be used to set reminders on your phone, such as the Habitica app or the Pomodoro Technique app. Additionally, show appreciation for your accomplishments, whether significant or minor. Track your progress by recording your achievements, challenges, and mistakes regularly.
