Machine Learning (ML) is a field of artificial intelligence that can learn automatically from data without being explicitly programmed by humans. Its potential benefits include automating repetitive tasks, predicting disease outbreaks, and aiding doctors in diagnosing patients more quickly. However, privacy and security concerns surrounding the collection and storage of sensitive patient information limit the effectiveness of machine learning models. The selection of models for evaluation should be based on available data sources, such as logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, neural networks, or cross-validation techniques.
