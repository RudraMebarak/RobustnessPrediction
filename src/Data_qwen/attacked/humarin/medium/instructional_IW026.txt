For those who are new to reading, what motivational tips can be offered to help you stay motivated for the long haul? Prerequisites: Read for at least five days a week, read one new book or e-book, and gradually increase your reading time. It's important to maintain a satisfying and enjoyable reading experience so that you can fully immerse yourself in captivating stories and ideas. Regardless of the type of book you prefer to read, there are lessons to be learned from every page and word spoken. Take a journey through your books, and let your imagination run wild!
