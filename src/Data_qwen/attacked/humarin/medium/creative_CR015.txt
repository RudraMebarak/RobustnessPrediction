The Shadow Code governs the human kingdom, with a mysterious set of rules that govern each creature's daily activities. The Shadow Code governs the actions of creatures, who were aware of their readiness to participate in daily activities and held a sense of predictability and control. Some individuals accepted this system as a source of stability and order, while Elara never felt at ease within it. She thrived in the unknown, exploring places that others could not comprehend. Despite this, few people were willing to question why she moved so freely, and her secret code, which allows her to explore spaces that are too small for others to enter, blends reality and imagination.
