The human immune system plays a crucial role in identifying and destroying viruses, as well as protecting against future infections. Both non-specific immunity and adaptive immunity are crucial in defending against viral infections, with the former working directly after infection and the latter developing over time. To prevent the transmission of colds and flu, it is important to regularly wash hands, clean surfaces, and cover mouths when coughing/sneezing, as precautions can differ greatly by country/state/etc. Stay tuned for further updates on local health guidelines during the coronavirus pandemic. Enriching your body with exercise, proper nutrition, hydration, avoidance of unhealthy habits (such as excessive drinking and smoking), and regular hand washing are effective ways to strengthen your immune system and safeguard against infectious diseases.
