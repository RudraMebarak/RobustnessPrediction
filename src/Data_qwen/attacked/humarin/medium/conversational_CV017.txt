Can you share your thoughts on whether reading physical books is preferable to reading digital ones? I find myself favoring one over the other, especially since I enjoy the smell of old books and feeling ecstatic when I open them. The experience of closing a chapter and walking away is far more satisfying than simply pressing "next" in an e-book. Additionally, the scent and feel of paper make reading books more personal and meaningful, which can only be achieved by stepping outside of the book. Also, the idea of collecting rare or signed copies adds to the enigma of having a well-loved book in your possession. The thought of preserving such treasured items for future generations fills me with immense joy. There's genuinely good reason to accumulate such items, as they might serve as heirloom props. I hope this helps clarify why some individuals may choose to own physical books instead of digital ones. Good luck, dear!
