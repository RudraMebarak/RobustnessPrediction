The HCI student is expected to start with one programming language and not multiple others. To achieve proficiency, the student must complete a single language once and spend at least one month reviewing it before progressing to the next. It is advisable to begin with low-code languages such as Swift and Kotlin, as they require less syntax knowledge than high-level languages like C++ and Java, and also choose the similarity between them and follow the similarities of languages.
