Sustainable development is often associated with increased consumption and pollution, but it is not a prerequisite for economic growth. To achieve a harmonious balance between humans and nature, we must make conscious choices about our behavior and consumption. This can be achieved by investing in renewable energy sources rather than solely relying on fossil fuels.
