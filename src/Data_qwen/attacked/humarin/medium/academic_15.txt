Integrating multiple fields and perspectives can aid in the development of a broader understanding of issues, according to research. Additionally, integrating diverse perspectives into education is crucial for addressing complex contemporary challenges. This approach provides learners with comprehensive knowledge across various fields, and allows them to view problems from different perspectives. Integrated understanding fosters creative thinking and innovative solutions, while training in one discipline does not result in deep thinking or analytical skills. Increased employability is achieved through interdisciplinary education. Collaboration across multiple scholarly domains and promotes open-mindedness.
