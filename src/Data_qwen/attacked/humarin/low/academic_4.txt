Urbanization in developing countries has significant environmental consequences, including significant amounts of pollution at the local and global scales. The impact of rapid urbanization on the environment is significant, as it requires the integration of sustainability principles at all levels, including water scarcity, increased costs, and impact on industries and households, as well as the displacement of low-income residents who reside in informal settlements, leading to social unrest and migration pressures in these areas. Addressing the direct impacts of human activity and other forms of economic production and lifestyle choices on our world's biosphere through a multi-faceted approach requires government initiatives, educational initiatives, individual responsibility, and international cooperation. Consistent action is essential to achieve sustainable development and secure a brighter future for future generations. To achieve this, governments should promote eco-friendly policies, encourage the adoption of clean technologies, establish stringent emission control standards, invest in renewable energy sources, establish protected national parks, and enforce strict regulations to prevent excessive waste generation.
