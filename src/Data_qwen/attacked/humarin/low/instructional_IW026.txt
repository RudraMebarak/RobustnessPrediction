For those who are new to reading, what motivational tips can help you stay motivated for the long haul? Prerequisites: Read for at least five days a week and read at least one new book or e-book every two weeks. Keep your reading experience enjoyable and enjoyable so that you can continue reading. Happy reading! If you're more efficient, feel free to work at a slower pace. Regardless of the type of book you prefer to read, there are lessons to be learned from every page and word spoken. Take a journey through your books, and let your imagination run wild!
