In this new universe, there are two types of dragon libraries: Dragon Books (DBs) and Dragon Manuscripts (DMs). DBs consist of spells, while DMs are composed of enchanted pages. Users of these libraries are not allowed to use magic themselves, but can borrow books from the library. Once inside, the library does not display an expiration date or any restrictions on the number of books that can be borrowed. Each species of mythical creature within the DM community is expected to possess distinct characteristics and abilities. The Magician Scholar is a talented writer who can manipulate plant life and heal with spoken words or chants. Magical Encounters** are stories told around the fire pit, featuring mythical encounters between patrons and guardians, enhancing stories passed down through generations. As a member of Dragon Books and Manuscripts Libraries, you can create intricate tales and adventures throughout your fantasy world, making it possible to explore education, magical interactions, societal norms, and individual identity.
