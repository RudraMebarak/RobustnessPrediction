In what ways does sleep play a role in mental health and overall well-being, and what specific research questions should be asked? The correlation between sleep quantity and academic performance at the collegiate level can be used to enhance college curriculum design and implement effective strategies to promote sleep hygiene among university students. This knowledge could aid educators in creating environments that promote improved cognitive functioning and improved performance for students in need. Recent studies emphasize the crucial role of sleep in promoting mental health and overall well-being (Klerman et al., 1985; Lambert & Dinges, 2007). In contrast, relatively few academic papers have focused on undergraduate universities, which are the most prevalent form of tertiary education globally (World Bank, 2020).
