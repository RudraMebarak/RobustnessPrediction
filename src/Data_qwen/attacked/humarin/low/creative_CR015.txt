The Shadow Code governs the human kingdom, with a mysterious set of rules that determines the occurrence of events. Each shadow has a specific time and location, and her body wakes up before her shadow hits the ground. The Shadow Code was governed by a mysterious and secretive council, and shadows were aware of when they would be ready for the day's activities. This was a feeling of predictability and control seldom seen among human beings. Elara, on the other hand, thrived in the unknown and seamlessly blended the real and the imagined. Her irregular appearances were both a source of fear and wonder, and she now navigates a labyrinthine form of existence. Elara's order was viewed by some as a beacon of stability and order, which offered a stark contrast to the chaotic lives of humans. However, she never felt at ease within this system. She thrived in the unknown, venturing beyond the scheduled times and surprising those who asked why she moved so freely. Despite this chaos, Elara shares a secret identity that allows her to explore dimensions that others fear to enter, mixing reality and fantasy.
