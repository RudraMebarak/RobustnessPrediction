The human immune system plays a crucial role in identifying and destroying viruses, as well as protecting against future infections. There are two main types of immunity: non-specific immunity that occurs immediately after infection and adaptive immunity that develops over time following exposure to harmful pathogens. Adaptive immunity involves the release of specific antibodies and helper cells, while innate immunity does not rely on memory cells. To avoid the risk of contracting a cold or flu, it is important to wash hands frequently, clean surfaces frequently, and cover mouths when coughing/sneezing, as the appropriate precautions can differ depending on the country or state. It is crucial to exercise, eat well, hydrate, and avoid unhealthy habits like smoking and drinking to strengthen your immune system. Follow official guidelines and advice from trusted sources during the ongoing global health crisis due to COVID-19. Stay alert and stay informed.
