Depending on the industry, geography, and duration, there are various reasons why open-source or proprietary software is a better choice for software development. The benefits of open-source code are not the same as those of proprietary code, and the decision to use it depends on the specific needs of the project or product. The question of whether open-source or proprietary software development models produces more consistent software output is a persistent issue in computer science and engineering. While both approaches have their advantages and disadvantages, the benefits and drawbacks of each approach are dependent on various factors such as project type, development team size, project timeline, organizational culture, and target market.
