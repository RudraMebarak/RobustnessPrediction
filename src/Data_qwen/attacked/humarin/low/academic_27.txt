There is a lack of a comprehensive understanding of how AI algorithms can be optimized for specific crop varieties, soil types, and environmental conditions. Additionally, there is an urgent need for further research on the long-term effects of AI-based precision agriculture practices on crop production systems, as well as small-scale farmers who lack access to advanced technology and financial resources to develop effective AI solutions. The impact of agriculture on ecosystems is significant, as well as the pollution caused by fertilizer, pesticides, and industrial waste discharges. The term "Anthropogenic Environmental Changes" is used to describe the inter-related and interdependent changes in the environment. To address these issues, a comprehensive approach is necessary, taking into account various interacting factors and implementing integrated strategies to address both immediate and long-term sustainability challenges. This approach requires interdisciplinary collaboration among scientists, policymakers, businesses, and community members to find innovative solutions, find ways to balance economic growth with ecological health, and protect public welfare during unprecedented global changes. However, we must act now before the impact of human activity through pollution.
