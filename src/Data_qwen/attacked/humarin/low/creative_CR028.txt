The town has been named after the last resident, but there are no records of how he died or what happened to him. As a result, the journalist decides to interview locals who have lived in the town for over 50 years. However, some residents are reluctant to share their stories due to fear of reprisals from local officials. The story explores topics such as secrecy, censorship, and the potential for hidden truths to emerge. Sarah Thompson suspects foul play and corruption within the town's governing bodies, prompting her to speak directly to the descendants. After much persuasion and perseverance, she uncovers a disturbing chapter in town history by asking them to share their story.
