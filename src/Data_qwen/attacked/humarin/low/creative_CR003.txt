The main character, Maria, is a librarian who discovers an ancient mystery in her town's history and struggles to reconcile it with her social history. The old library felt like a doorway to a past era of love and untold stories, with a tattered cover and symbols written in an unknown script that danced with history. Maria was overcome with fear and anxiety as she deciphered the mysterious words and symbols. Ultimately, she determined to honor not only her predecessors' struggles but also those who society had neglected until now. She stood before the town hall and contemplated the future with shivering fingers, clutching the old book that contained cryptic messages and holding it close to her heart. “Let us never forget our heroes!” she whispered, followed by a longing.
