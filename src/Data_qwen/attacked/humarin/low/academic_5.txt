Scientific research is essential for addressing global challenges such as climate change and pandemics, as it provides practical solutions and guidance for implementing effective strategies to mitigate adverse effects. Additionally, it has been instrumental in identifying ways to prevent the spread of diseases like HIV/AIDS, Ebola, and COVID-19, and providing guidance for adapting to environmental changes. The lack of water resources could intensify conflicts and disrupt ecosystems, resulting in reduced crop yields. Without coordinated support and solidarity, regional emergencies could escalate into pan-global crises, resulting in an increase in casualties beyond the capacity of local and national authorities, leading to the need for disproportionate international intervention efforts.
