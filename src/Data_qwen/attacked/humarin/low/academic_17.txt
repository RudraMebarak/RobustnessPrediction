Machine Learning (ML) is a field of artificial intelligence that can learn automatically from data without being explicitly programmed by humans. Its potential benefits include automating repetitive tasks, predicting disease outbreaks, and assisting doctors in diagnosing patients more quickly. However, privacy and security concerns surrounding the collection and storage of sensitive patient information limit the effectiveness of machine learning models. The selection of models for evaluation is based on available data sources, such as logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, neural networks, and cross-validation techniques. Real-time monitoring and analytics enable patient management, optimize hospital operations, and facilitate telemedicine. ML models can also help with pharmaceutical R&D, but they face several challenges in terms of data quality, interpretability, bias, ethicalness, and privacy when used with AI-assisted decision-making systems. The Role of Personalized Medicine in Preventing Chronic Diseases: 1. Identifying Genetic Variants: Many rare and shared genetic mutations are found in older people, which can contribute to cardiovascular diseases, osteoarthritis, dementia, cancer and metabolic disorders. By analyzing an individual's genome, clinicians can identify the presence of risk alleles associated with various health conditions. Genome Wide Association Studies (GWAS) have shown how certain single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) increase the risk of heart failure, Alzheimer's disease, and cancers.
