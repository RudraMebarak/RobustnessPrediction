Imagine a city made up entirely of more than 100 species of trees, with the tallest structure being made entirely from the Sequoia Douglas Firs. The rest of the buildings are built from smaller species like Oak and Birch, and the smallest ones surround a vast network of caves and tunnels carved into the ground. Alec Green, who has spent most of his life navigating these forests without ever having seen the outside world, visits his home after work and reads from a book called "The Last Stand" during the evening. The journey endures for several hours before reaching a cliffside, where they witness a vibrant community built on a platform. They witness buildings made mostly of native ferns and mosses, supported by sturdy lichen scaffolding. They revel in communal storytelling sessions held over a fire, while Alec shares his thoughts on future projects and reminisces about simpler times when everything could be done by slumbering alone. I incorporated literary elements such as foreshadowing and imagery to create a sense of wonder and enchantment throughout the story. I also incorporated myth and folklore to enhance the fictional world and highlight Alec's connection to nature.
