After a two-hour wait in line at the airport for my flight home from Europe, I decided to take a taxi and return home. Although I had been informed by the pilot that there might be any problems with my visa, I was unable to make a decision immediately. The day after my flight home from Europe, I found out that my passport had an error and the process was delayed due to an in-flight printer error. This incident highlighted the importance of verifying details before leaving the country. The airline representative called me to report some minor discrepancies in my passport information, just as I was about to board our most important flight. I rubbed my temples and cursed deeply.
