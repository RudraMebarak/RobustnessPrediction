Online Shopping Behavior (OSB) is based on the following theory: 1) Perceived Value Theory: Consumers evaluate the value of goods and services on the basis of quality, price, brand reputation, ease of use, and ease of purchase (e.g., Amazon.com, Amazon Web Services, Google Analytics, etc.) 2) The Online Trust Model: The consumer is more likely to trust e-commerce platforms by observing the reviews, security protocols, transaction history, and delivery addresses, which assumes higher levels of trust in order. The occurrence of this phenomenon is due to positive brand associations and future benefits from staying with a specific company or product line. The theoretical framework highlights several factors that affect people's choices when shopping online, including which products to choose and which ones do not. Furthermore, these frameworks do not take into account ongoing changes in the industry, such as technological advancements, evolving regulatory standards, and consumer preferences.
