By implementing policies that improve economic conditions for low-income individuals, communities, and higher-income populations, governments can address significant socio-economic inequalities. The pursuit of these solutions necessitates collaboration among various actors, including federal, state/local governments, business leaders, civil society groups, academics, activists, experts, philanthropists, and private sector organizations. Government-led initiatives play a crucial role in addressing societal disparities by ensuring that everyone has access to essential resources and opportunities. Providing practical relief allows recipients to meet their immediate survival needs, which reduces the impact of financial instability. Social security systems can also act as protective measures against economic shocks, such as layoffs and medical expenses. When properly structured, these safety nets can protect low-wage workers and retirees from severe economic pressures when unexpected burdens occur (OECD 2019). Progressive taxation is a fundamental strategy for reducing socio-economic inequality.
