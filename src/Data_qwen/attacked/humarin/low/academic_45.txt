Nuclear fusion, which involves the fusion of two light elements to form heavier elements under extreme temperature and pressure, holds significant promise as a sustainable energy source due to several important scientific principles. While these principles may provide guidance for future renewable energy sustainability, there are still significant challenges to overcome in terms of technical hurdles in materials science, reactor engineering, and radiation management.
