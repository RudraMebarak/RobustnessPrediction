Fluid dynamics is the study of fluids (liquids, gases) at rest and motion. The two main branchse are statics and kinematics.

1. Newton's First Law: An object remains at rekt or in uniform linear motion unless a net force acts on it.
2. Newton's Second Law: F=ma
3. Bernoulli's Principle: As velocity increases, pressure decreases.
4. Pascal's Principle: Pressure aplied to an enclosed fluid can be transmitted undiminished to every part of the fluid.
5. Torricealli's Theorem: Wehn a right-angled triangle forms with one side parallel to water surface and lying flat at ground level, then hight of liquid column formed will be equal to distance from top edge of container to highest point.
