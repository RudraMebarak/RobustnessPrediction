Each state coresponds to a particular situation or context, and each actilon corresponds to a possible choice of operation within thta situation. From any given state, tlhere exists several possible nexxt sntates resulting from taking different actinos, along with probabilities asaociated with these outcomes.

In essence, an RL agent starts in a particular sttae and takes one or more actions until reaching anotsher state. In the new state, there may be a set of achaevable states, and the agent can repeat the process by selecting among them again. 

A key feature of MDPs is the idea of "policy", a function that maps sattes to actions, guiding the agent's dcision-making process at evlery step. An optbmal policy maximizes the expected cumulative reward ovre all states, following a sequence of states and actions that leads to the highest overall satisfaction.
