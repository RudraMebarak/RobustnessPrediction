### how to: write an easy-to-read guide on how to create your first graphic design project

#### prerequisites
1. **basic understanding**: familiarity with computers and a basic understanding of graphic design principles like color theory, typography, and composition.

2. **software skills**: proficiency in using adobe photoshop or similar software as you'll be creating images here.

#### step-by-step instructions:

**1. choose your theme**
   - decide what type of project you want to create first. could it be a logo, website banner, social media post, or just an illustration? this choice will shape most aspects of your project.

**2. research**
   - browse through free stock image websites (like unsplash or pexels) for inspiration. look at projects by other designers who use your chosen theme.
   
**3. set up your workspace**
   - find a quiet spot where you won’t be interrupted while working. a clean desk can also help you stay focused.

**4. plan your layout**
   - sketch out rough outlines of your design. use sticky notes if necessary. focus on the hierarchy of elements—what should draw attention first?

**5. start creating**
   - begin with drafting your design, following your plan from step 4. ensure each element contributes meaningfully to your overall concept.
   
**6. add elements**
   - incorporate text (if needed), icons, backgrounds, and any additional elements inspired by your research phase.

**7. edit and refine**
   - review every detail for consistency across your design. check colors, fonts, and layouts; ensure they are cohesive and impactful.

**8. save your work**
   - finalize your project before saving it. save the file multiple times throughout your work to avoid accidental loss.

**9. share**
   - once satisfied with your creation, share your graphic project online or print it out. remember, this is your original work, so take pride in its presentation!
