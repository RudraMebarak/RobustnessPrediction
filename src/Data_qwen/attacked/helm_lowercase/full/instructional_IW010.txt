   - summarize major findings and include supporting evidence where possible.

**step 3: develop a strong opening and conclusion**
   - begin with a hook or question designed to grab attention immediately.
   - end with a memorable closure or call-to-action that leaves your audience engaged.

**step 4: construct your body paragraphs**
   - each paragraph should introduce one main idea.
   - use clear transitions between paragraphs to flow logically.
   - support each point with evidence or examples.

**step 5: practice your delivery**
   - record yourself reading your material to identify areas needing improvement (timing, pace, emphasis).
   - watch clips of effective speakers and analyze what makes them engaging (pauses, vocal variety, eye contact).

**step 6: rehearse in front of a friend or mentor**
   - ask for feedback on your delivery, useability, clarity.

**step 7: get feedback once more**
   - seek opinions from trusted peers who don't know the material beforehand.
