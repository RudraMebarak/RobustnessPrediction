yes, i definitely do modify poses depending on my current fitness level or any health conditions i may be dealing with. for instance:

- when my knee pain becomes particularly sharp, we usually skip standing poses requiring significant weight-bearing on one leg.
- if i’m dealing with low back discomfort, we might focus less on stretching certain areas and include more balancing sequences that keep pressure off those muscles.
- on days i’m not feeling great overall, we alternate between gentle seated poses and calming breathwork exercises.
  
adjustments can vary greatly based on immediate needs and progress. my goal remains consistency with respect to structure and principles taught by my teacher, but modifications ensure i’m doing the work safely and effectively.
