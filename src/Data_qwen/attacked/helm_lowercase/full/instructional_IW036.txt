when should i use commas?

here are some situations where commas are necessary:
 
1. to separate items in a list (a, b, c).
  2. in appositives (john smith, who was a famous politician). 
  3. after introductory elements (however, he did not believe it). 
  4. before coordinating conjunctions (and she likes jazz music and country music). 
  5. when referring to years (1990’s), dates (october 18th), addresses (1600 pennsylvania ave.), percentages (85%), statistics (15% decrease in sales)).
  6. between independent clauses joined by certain coordinating conjunctions (although i hate dogs, nevertheless i love cats.).
