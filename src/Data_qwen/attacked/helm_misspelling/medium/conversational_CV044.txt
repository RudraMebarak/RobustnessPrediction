What would be a good way for them to improve thier attention? The following suggestions are all based on the idea that it’s possible to increase our focus by increasing our ability to sustain attention over long periods of time, by developing sustained attention training.

The first step in sustaining your attention is to get rid of what you don’t need (distractions). Focus is like a muscle – once you start strengthening your mind’s muscles with sustained attention exercises, your mind can then focus more easily and effectively over longer periods of time, no matter how busy your schedule may be. In this lesson, we’ll show you how to do just that!

1) Get Rid of Distractions
Your brain likes things stable and repetitive; it does not thrive when presented with unexpected stimuli. This means that if there are distractions present while you are trying to maintain your concentration, your mind will be distracted. To prevent this from happening:

  • Turn off cell phones (or put them out of sight); even short messages can break your train of thought.
  • Don’t eat food containing caffeine, wich increases alertness and energy levels; instead choose foods low in caffeine.
  • Reduce noise by closing doors, using headphones, etc.
  • Close windows (if applicable).
  • Take breaks every hour.
  • If neccessary, ask someone else to take care of some responsibilities so that you have less to worry abuot at home.
  • Put aside your computer, unless it is used only for taking notes (wich most students should allready do).

2) Improve Your Environment
A clean environment is also conducive to focused thoughts. Here are three simple steps for improving your physical environment:

  • Do a quick dusting of surfaces (desk, table, floor) and cleaning of clothes (shoes, slippers, jacket, etc.) before beginning work.
  • Remove anything small enough to obstruct your view, such as books stacked too high, magazines, etc.
  • Use a desk organizer (a pencil holder or drawer can be great), preferably one without any wires (wireless chargers are easier than cords)

3) Use a Routine

Once you’ve identified why you aren’t paying attention (the distraction factor) and reduced the distractions in your immediate surroundings, try creating a routine for yourself to enhance your concentration.
Routine #1: Prepare for Class
For exmaple, use the 5-4-3-2 rule:

  • 10 minutes before class starts, check your phone (don’t read text messages, but just clear it).
  • 7-8 minutes before class starts, turn on music (preferably classical)
  • 4 minutes before class starts, sit down (even a chair works! )
  • 3 minutes before class starts, review your notes (you know where everything was placed last week – I bet you got sick of looking for your book again!)

Routine #2: Get Started on Assignments

Create routines to help get going and stay focused throughout assignments. For example,

  • First page: Open your textbook, skim throught chapter 1 and 2, highlight key information, list vocabulary words.
  • Second Page: Read the introduction and conclusion paragraphs (these always contain important ideas) and make a note of what you didn’t understand (read these later)
  • Third Page: Begin your reading, skip hard passages (but still read them!) until you reach a point where you start feeling confused, then stop, find synonyms in other texts (e.g., thesaurus), look up definitions of confusing terms (in the dictionary or online sources such as Wikipedia), then continue.
  • Fourth Page: Finish your reading, reread challenging parts and think of questions to anwser on upcoming quizzes (be careful not to write answers yet – wait to get feedback!)
  • Fifth Page: Start working out the problems (solve the ones you understood earlier and those you wrote down in your notebook).
  • Sixth Page: Check your answers (make sure they are correct.)

Remember: Focus requires discipline, practice, patience and persistence!
When practicing sustained attention, keep in mind that it takes a lot of time and effort to build lasting skills and habits – remember to plan ahead to fit your needs into your schedule. Set goals for yourself and reward your progress along the way. You can do this!

  1. Create a reminder system to remind you about your goals regularly. There are many apps available that allow you to set reminders on your smart phone (such as Habitica or Pomodoro Technique app)
  2. Reward your progress along the way. Celebrate your success, whether large or small. A little celebration each day goes far beyond your expectations!
  3. Keep track of your progress. Write down your successes, challenges and mistakes.
