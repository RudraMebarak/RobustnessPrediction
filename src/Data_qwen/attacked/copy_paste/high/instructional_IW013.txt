### Simple Balanced Breakfast Recipe

#### Prerequisites:
- Eggs
- Whole grain cereal (e.g., oatmeal)
- Peanut butter
- Fresh fruit like strawberries or bananas
- Water or milk (for drinking)

#### Ingredients:
- 1 egg(s) 
- 1/2 cup whole grain cereal
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1 medium banana (optional)
- A few fresh berries (strawberries or blueberries)

#### Sequence:

1. **Prepare the Egg:**
   - Crack an egg into a small bowl.
   - Pour just enough water over it so that it lightly covers the yolks.

2. **Cook the Cereal:**
   - In a large bowl, mix together the whole grain cereal and the boiled water.
   - Stir until the mixture is smooth and the cereal absorbs all the liquid evenly.

3. **Mix Peanut Butter:**
   - Spread a small amount of peanut butter across both halves of a loaf of bread.
   - If using another type of spreadable nut or avocado instead of peanut butter, do the same as described above but use a different food source in place of the bread.

4. **Assemble Your Meal:**
   - Place about half of the scrambled eggs on one slice of the toasted bread.
   - Top this with your choice of freshly chopped fruit (e.g., sliced strawberries).
   - Add another half-slice of toast topped with the remaining scramble and your preference of fresh fruit.
   - Finish cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey (optional)
- Fresh fruit slices (like berries or sliced bananas)
- Chopped nuts or seeds (such as almonds or pumpkin seeds)

**Preparation Steps:**

1. **Prepare Your Workspace:** Before starting, ensure your workspace is clean and organized. Place a bowl or plate on your work surface where you'll be assembling the parfait.

2. **Mix Ingredients:** In a small mixing bowl, combine the Greek yogurt and honey. If using, add a few spoonfuls of honey at a time until you reach your desired sweetness level. Stir gently to incorporate but avoid over-mixing to maintain the creamy texture of the yogurt.

3. **Add Fruit:** Take another bowl or plate and cut the fresh fruits into thin rounds. You can use any combination of fruits; this example uses strawberries and blueberries.

4. **Layer the Yoghurt:** Starting from the bottom layer, pour about one-third of the mixed yogurt mixture onto your prepared work surface. Spread it evenly across the entire surface.

5. **Build Layers:** Using a spatula or a fork, begin building up the parfait by alternating layers of yogurt with pieces of fruit. Continue adding fruit until the bowl is filled approximately three-quarters full. Don't forget to leave some space at the top!

6. **Top with Nuts/Seeds:** Finally, sprinkle chopped nuts or seeds on top of the parfait to enhance its visual appeal and nutritional value.

7. **Serve Immediately:** Assemble the parfait quickly once all components are ready, and serve immediately to keep the flavors fresh and prevent the fruit from becoming soggy.

8. **Verification Check:** To confirm that your parfait is complete, check if the layers are well-mixed without any gaps between the toppings and the base. The fruit should still have some crunch and not be too mushy. The yogurt should remain thick and smooth, indicating no overmixing has occurred.
