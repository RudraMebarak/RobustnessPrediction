### Setting Up Your New Laptop for Secure Everyday Use

#### Prerequisites
1. **Hardware**: A basic laptop with a modern operating system like Windows 10 Pro.
2. **Software**:
   - Basic antivirus like Malwarebytes Antivirus or Microsoft Defender
   - Firewall (built-in in Windows) 
3. **Network Setup**: An internet connection (preferably Wi-Fi)
4. **Account Information**: Create user accounts if needed, but keep passwords safe.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Set Up Your Laptop

1. **Initial Setup**
    - **Start-up Security Check**
        - Turn off the "Fast Startup" feature while booting.
          - Press `Shift` + `F10` during startup.
        - Enable automatic restart after 15 minutes.
        - Restart the computer.

    - **Defragmentation & Virus Scan (Windows Only)**
        - Right-click on “This PC” -> Manage -> Disk Management
        - Find any corrupted files/disk spaces developers".
3. **Check for Updates**: Scroll down and look for “Windows Update”. Check if there are updates available. If so, install them as soon as they become available.

**Verification:**
- After updating, check the same location in the settings menu again to confirm if any updates were applied successfully.

---

#### Step 3: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
**Objective:** Add an extra layer of security.

**Action Steps:**
1. **Go to Settings**: Click on the gear icon and select "Accounts". Then, go to "Two-Factor Authentication".
2. **Enable It**: Turn on two-factor authentication. Follow the prompts to configure it, usually using a text message code sent to your phone.

**Verification:**
- Log out and back into your account. Try logging in without having the second factor enabled. You should receive a prompt asking for a code.

---

#### Step 4: Set Up Parental Controls (Optional but Recommended)
**Objective:** Restrict access to inappropriate content for children.

**Action Steps:**
1. **Access Control Panel**: Press the Windows key + R, type `control`, and press Enter.
2. **Parental Control Options**: Under "System", click "Parental controls".
3. **Add Devices**: Add the device (your laptop) you want to control.

**Verification:**
- Navigate to `Device Management` within the parental control settings. Ensure devices like phones and tablets are also restricted.

---

#### Step 5: Regularly Back Up Data
**Objective:** Protect yourself against data loss.

**Action Steps:**
1. **Download Backup App**: Download a reputable cloud backup service app (like Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive).
2. **Set Up Backup**: Sign in to your chosen service account and set up a regular backup schedule.

**Verification:**
- Check your email or log into the service to see recent backups.

---

#### Additional Tips:

- Always keep your antivirus software updated.
- Disable unnecessary services running in the background.
- Be cautious about sharing personal information online.
- Regularly update your operating system and applications.

By following these steps, you will ensure that your new laptop operates securely and efficiently, providing a better virus scan using reputable antivirus software.

### Additional Security Measures
1. **Use Strong Passphrases:**
   - Do not reuse passwords across multiple sites.
   
2. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):**
   - Enable this for your most important accounts (e.g., Google, Email Services).
   - For added protection, enable 2FA for sensitive apps and services.

3. **Keep Software Updated Regularly (at least monthly):**
   - Important for preventing vulnerabilities exploited by hackers.

4. **Regular Backups (at least once weekly):**
   - Use cloud storage solutions for backups; periodically sync local backup devices too.
   
5. **Physical Disposal Plan:**
   - Know how you’ll properly dispose of old hardware once its end-of-life has arrived to protect your privacy.
