### Research Proposal: Investigating the Use of Blockchain Technology in Supply Chain Management

#### Introduction
Blockchain technology has emerged as a disruptive force across various industries, revolutionizing processes by leveraging decentralized databases that are secure, transparent, and tamper-proof (Cascini & Storchi, 2018). In the context of supply chain management (SCM), blockchain offers opportunities to enhance transparency, traceability, efficiency, and sustainability. By providing immutable records of transactions, it enables organizations to streamline operations, reduce costs, and increase trust.

This study aims to explore how blockchain can be effectively integrated into supply chains to improve visibility, security, and collaboration among stakeholders. To achieve this goal, we will conduct comprehensive interviews with industry leaders and practitioners, analyze historical data, and evaluate case studies to identify best practices and challenges.

#### Literature Review
The literature reviews highlight several key areas where blockchain is currently being utilized in SCM:

1. **Increased Transparency**: Enhancing traceability through real-time tracking of goods from source to consumer (Peters et al., 2019).
   
2. **Reduced Fraudulence**: Mitigating counterfeit products and financial fraud using smart contracts (Katz et al., 2018).

3. **Improved Efficiency**: Automating document processing and reducing paperwork associated with trade compliance (Lipson et al., 2017).

4. **Enhanced Security**: Securing sensitive information with cryptographic techniques (Taher等人, 2015).

5. **Optimized Logistics**: Optimizing transportation routes and improving inventory management (Rogers & Fung, 2016).

However, there are limitations such as scalability issues, regulatory hurdles, high initial costs, and the need for skilled workforce (Bhargava et al., 2018; Cascini & Storchi, 2018).
