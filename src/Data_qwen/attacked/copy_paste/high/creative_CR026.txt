The once-grand facade now bore witness to a dark history - one where secrets lay hidden within its walls. The owner had moved away years ago without leaving any trace, but the echoes of their presence remained palpable. Now, a group of seasoned detectives, led by Detective Maria Rodriguez, had been called to investigate.

Their first stop was the old-fashioned front door, standing ominously tall against the weather-beaten brickwork. The hinges creaked softly as they pushed open slowly. Inside, the air was thick with the musty scent of decay and dust. 

The rooms beyond revealed nothing new at first glance: a cluttered hallway leading to several interconnected chambers filled with remnants from different time periods – discarded clothes, broken furniture, and stacks of old papers. But amidst this chaos, something caught their attention. In one corner of Room 418, the investigators stumbled upon a small box, barely noticeable under piles of books and junk.

Maria took it gingerly and lifted it off the floor with her boot. Inside was a single letter addressed to "Lucas," penned in a neat script. As she unfolded it, her heart sank. The message was clear:

"A man will die soon. Whoever finds this letter before midnight tonight shall be warned."

With no other clues, the team decided to move on, determined to solve Lucas' fate and find out more about his past. They split into pairs, each searching for information while listening for sounds or movement outside the building.
