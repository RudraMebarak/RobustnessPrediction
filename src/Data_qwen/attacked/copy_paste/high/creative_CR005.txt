One night though, something strange happened. As Lila was closing up the diner, she noticed that there was no sign of any customers coming through the door. But then, out of nowhere, a man appeared and started ordering food without saying anything.

Lila was puzzled by this new development. She had never seen anyone come into the diner like that before. And yet, he kept coming back every single night. No matter what time it was or how many people were already inside, he always showed up.

At first, Lila thought he was just some shady character trying to rob the place. But after talking to him for a few minutes, she realized that he wasn't lying. He claimed to be homeless and needed a place to stay. Lila was touched by his honesty and decided to help him find somewhere to sleep.
