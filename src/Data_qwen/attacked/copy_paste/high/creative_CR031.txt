Title: The Haunting of Serene Lane

One evening during a storm, 25-year-old Emily arrives at the end of the road to visit her grandmother, who lives on one of the houses. A cold wind howls outside as Emily makes her way down the cobbled path leading up the lane. The rain falls thickly from darkening clouds above, making everything seem surreal.
