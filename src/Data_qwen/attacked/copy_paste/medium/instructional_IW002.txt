Backing Up Your Files To An External Drive

1. **Choose The Right Time:** Choose a time when your computer won't be too busy or running low on power. Also, ensure there are no people nearby as you'll need direct access for backup.

2. **Purchase Or Create Your External Drive:** Decide if you want to use a hard disk (HDD) or Solid State Drive (SSD). If unsure, SSD is recommended because it's faster but also more expensive and less reliable than HDD drives. Purchase one that can store at least 50GB worth of data.

3. **Connect Your External Drive:** Plug in the external storage device to your computer using a USB cable.

4. **Launch Your File Management Software**: Select your preferred software (like EaseUS Todo Backup, Carbon Copy Cloner, or Disk Utility depending on your operating system).

5. **Locate And Prepare Your Computer’s Essential Data:** Identify folders containing the most crucial documents, pictures, videos, music, etc. These should include personal notes, project files, family photos, financial records, passwords, etc.

6. **Start The Backup Process:** Open your chosen backup software. Click "Add" to add your folders or select them directly from the available space on your computer. Then choose "Create Backup."

7. **Wait For The Process To Complete:** This could take some time depending on the size and quantity of files you are backing up. A progress bar will show you where you are at all times.

8. **Verify Completion On The External Drive:** After completing your backup, plug in your new backup location. Most programs would display a message informing you the backup is complete. 

9. **Check Integrity Of The Backup:** Run a scan with your backup software. It should show any issues. Check the integrity of copied content by viewing individual file details.

10. **Secure The Drive:** Always keep this backup on a secure, offline location such as a fireproof box or encrypted cloud service. Do not share this backup unless you've explicitly authorized users.

By following these steps, you can easily safeguard your valuable information, ensuring peace of mind and protection for future use. Always make sure to regularly test backups to verify their functionality and schedule periodic updates for optimal protection. Keep in mind, Make sure your computer has USB ports available.

### Step-by-Step Backup Process

#### 1. Prepare Your Computer
- Connect your external hard drive to your computer via USB port.

![Connect External Drive](https://example.com/connect_drive.png)

#### 2. Open File Explorer
- Press `Win + E` to open File Explorer.
- Navigate to the folder containing your important files.

![File Explorer](https://example.com/file_explorer.png)

#### 3. Create a Backup Folder
- Right-click on any empty space in File Explorer.
- Select "New" > "Folder".
- Name it something like "Backup_Files".

![Create Backup Folder](https://example.com/create_backup_folder.png)

#### 4. Move Important Files
- Drag-and-drop each file/folder you want to backup into the new "Backup_Files" folder.

![Move Files](https://example.com/move_files.png)

#### 5. Copy to External Drive
- Right-click on the "Backup_Files" folder.
- Select "Properties".
- Go to the "Customize..." tab under the "General" section.
- Click "Browse...".

![Browse for Storage](https://example.com/browse_storage.png)

- Locate your external hard drive and select its location.
- Checkmark "Read-only" and click "OK".

![Select External Drive](https://example.com/select_drive.png)

- Back on the Properties window, go to the "Format" tab.
- Under "Options", check "Quick format".
- Set the time limit (e.g., 1 minute) and click "Start Quick Format".

![Quick Format Options](https://example.com/quick_format_options.png)
