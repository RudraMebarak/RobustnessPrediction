Title: The Silent Customer

At 10 PM sharp, Sarah had just checked out of the building and was heading towards her car when she noticed something odd at the front door. A man appeared behind the curtain, holding a large bag and walking slowly. He walked around the corner and disappeared into the darkness. 

"What the hell is going on here?" she said to herself as she parked her car outside the diner.

"Okay, maybe someone's leaving," another worker said from the diner. "Let’s check it out."

As they approached the diner, they could see that there were several customers standing around. They took turns looking inside the diner. No cameras or video surveillance devices anywhere. The only lights came from the diner itself.

They decided to split up. One went upstairs while the other stayed downstairs checking the restrooms and bathrooms for hidden cameras.

After a few minutes, the first worker came down the stairs. She told them, "No luck so far! They’re definitely not using any kind of camcorder."

The second worker then turned to the first saying, "Look, I saw something suspicious tonight. When the last guy left, he was carrying something really big." 

"Well what did you say it was?" asked the first worker.

"Heavy stuff!" the second worker exclaimed.

"I think he’s got some serious money coming through tonight!"

Both workers became very agitated over this development and began questioning why anyone would have such secret plans to leave without paying and how they knew exactly how much money was needed.

Hours later, they heard screams coming out of the diner. They quickly made their way there and discovered that the owner had been attacked by the unknown customer earlier that day. All the workers became very scared and ran away.

Before long, an emergency call was received for help. The police arrived soon after, along with doctors and medical staff who treated the attack victim. The owner was eventually identified by security footage taken earlier that evening. He admitted guilt and handed himself over to authorities.

All the workers felt relieved once they heard that everyone was safe and everything was back to normal. The owners and managers promised changes and improvements to protect the diner. They also shared information with other businesses in town and urged people to report anything unusual before it happens again. As for the silent customer, no one knows where he is now. But the mystery remains unsolved until today. 

In the end, only the diner owner and his family are left alive; everyone else has either gone missing or fled town. Only time will tell if those involved can ever be brought to justice for their actions or whether fate will take its own course.
