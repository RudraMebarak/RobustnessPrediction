### Disease Control
1. **Lack of Vaccines and Treatments**: Without ongoing research and development, there would be a significant delay in creating effective vaccines against emerging diseases.
   
   - **Impact**: Epidemics may outpace discovery and deployment of medical countermeasures, causing widespread illness and death among populations around the globe.
   
   - **Consequence**: A ripple effect across healthcare systems worldwide, affecting individuals regardless of geographical location.

2. **Misguided Public Health Strategies**: Inadequate scientific input into public health initiatives could result in ineffective response protocols or dangerous misinterpretations of data.
   
   - **Impact**: Widespread compliance lapses during outbreaks could undermine containment efforts and slow recovery times.
   
   - **Consequence**: Increased morbidity and mortality linked to untreated infections, prolonged suffering, and potential societal collapse due to uncontrolled outbreaks.

3. **Falsified Data and Misleading Claims**: Biased or erroneous findings could contribute to policy errors and misinformation, hampering progress towards curbing infectious diseases.
   
   - **Impact**: Confusion in decision-making processes regarding prevention and control efforts could hinder overall effectiveness of interventions.
   
   - **Consequence**: Underestimating the severity of future crises and lack of urgency in implementing necessary preventive measures leads to irreversible setbacks and broader social unrests.

4. **Unfulfilled Expectations of Global Collaboration**: Inadequate collaboration between nations and international bodies (such as WHO) would severely undermine joint responses to global health threats.
   
   - **Impact**: Regional emergencies could spiral into pan-global crises without coordinated support and solidarity.
   
   - **Consequence**: An escalation in casualties beyond the capacity of local and national authorities to manage alone, necessitating disproportionate global intervention forces.

5. **Delayed Innovation in Technology**: Lack of scientific innovation would retard advancements in renewable energy sources, efficient technologies, personal protection equipment (PPE), and diagnostic tools.
   
   - **Impact**: Reduced access to sustainable living options and improved healthcare infrastructure, contributing significantly to economic downturns and humanitarian crises.
