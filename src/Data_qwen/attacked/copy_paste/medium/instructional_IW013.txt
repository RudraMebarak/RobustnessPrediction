### Simple Balanced Breakfast Recipe

#### Prerequisites:
- Eggs
- Whole grain cereal (e.g., oatmeal)
- Peanut butter
- Fresh fruit like strawberries or bananas
- Water or milk (for drinking)

#### Ingredients:
- 1 egg(s) 
- 1/2 cup whole grain cereal
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1 medium banana (optional)
- A few fresh berries (strawberries or blueberries)

#### Sequence:

1. **Prepare the Egg:**
   - Crack an egg into a small bowl.
   - Pour just enough water over it so that it lightly covers the yolks.

2. **Cook the Cereal:**
   - In a large bowl, mix together the whole grain cereal and the boiled water.
   - Stir until the mixture is smooth and the cereal absorbs all the liquid evenly.

3. **Mix Peanut Butter:**
   - Spread a small amount of peanut butter across both halves of a loaf of bread.
   - If using another type of spreadable nut or avocado instead of peanut butter, do the same as described above but use a different food source in place of the bread.

4. **Assemble Your Meal:**
   - Place about half of the scrambled eggs on one slice of the toasted bread.
   - Top this with your choice of freshly chopped fruit (e.g., sliced strawberries).
   - Add another half-slice of toast topped with the remaining scramble and your preference of fresh fruit.
   - Finish by placing the remaining slices of toast and a handful of your favorite berries on top.

5. **Final Touches:**
   - Garnish with a sprinkle of sea salt and a dash of ground cinnamon if desired.

6. **Serve:**
   - Slice or divide your meal into appropriate portions before eating.

7. **Verification:**
   - Confirm you have all the required items (whole grain cereal, peanuts), make sure everything is prepared as instructed, confirm no allergens are missing, and ensure your preparation method checks out without any food safety issues.

### Modifications for Dietary Preferences or Intolerances:

1. **Gluten-Free Alternative:** Replace whole grain cereal with gluten-free oats or choose gluten-free cereals. For the bread spread, opt for almond butter or a low-fat dairy product such as Greek yogurt spreaded with granola and fruit.

2. **Dairy-Free Option:** Swap milk from the recipe with lactose-free milk. Use coconut cream if adding a creamy texture.

3. **Plant-Based Proteins:** Instead of scrambled eggs, use a plant-based protein alternative like tofu or tempeh mixed with nutritional yeast for a cheesy flavor.

4. **Nut-Free Version:** Reduce or replace the peanut butter with sunflower seed butter or cashew butter. 

This basic recipe can be adapted to suit various dietary needs while maintaining balance—considering vitamins, minerals, fiber content, and allergen avoidance.
