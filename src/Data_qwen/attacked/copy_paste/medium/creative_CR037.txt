In honor of Human Rights Day, let's take a moment to acknowledge the importance of human rights advocacy and the ongoing fight against discrimination and inequality around the globe. This year marks 75 years since the Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted by the United Nations General Assembly, setting a foundation for global respect and protection of basic freedoms.

### **Understanding Human Rights**

Human rights are fundamental entitlements that belong to every person irrespective of age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, or any other characteristic. These rights include freedom from slavery, torture, and cruel treatment; access to justice; and equality before the law. They encompass the right to education, healthcare, work, property ownership, family life, and participation in civil society.

### **Promoting Inclusive Societies**

To celebrate the spirit of inclusivity, we must actively promote policies and practices that foster equal opportunities and eliminate barriers to human rights. This includes addressing issues related to poverty, discrimination, violence, and lack of representation. By working towards greater social cohesion and reducing prejudice, we can create societies where all individuals thrive without fear of harm or oppression.
