To stay safe while traveling, I believe it's crucial to plan ahead:

1. Research the area: Learn about local customs, transportation options, weather conditions, and potential hazards before leaving.
2. Pack appropriately: Prepare for weather changes by bringing layers suitable for different temperatures.
3. Use reliable sources: Always verify information obtained via reputable websites or apps over personal stories or advice from strangers.
4. Stay connected: Bring along chargers, batteries, phone chargers, etc., so you're not left behind or disconnected if something happens.
5. Inform others: Let someone close to you know your itinerary and arrival/departure times, including your planned routes and estimated return dates.
6. Dress warmly: Wear clothes appropriate for the climate and conditions; don’t forget sunscreen!
7. Be mindful of crowds: Don’t venture into crowded areas unsupervised due to low lighting, lack of awareness or aggressive behavior.
8. Respect rules and regulations: Obey traffic laws and local rules at tourist destinations and national parks.
9. Keep track of valuables: Store electronics, cash, passports, IDs, jewelry, expensive items, cameras, etc., separately from your main bags in lockable containers.
10. Stay alert: Watch your step, keep your belongings within reach and be cautious near busy intersections, rivers, lakes, pools and swimming areas.
