### Onboarding Guide for a New Team Member in a Small Project

#### Prerequisites:
1. **Basic Understanding**: The team member should have some basic understanding of your project's goals, deliverables, and workflow.
2. **Access**: They need access to all project tools and resources including project management software (e.g., Trello, Asana), communication platforms (Slack or Microsoft Teams), and any specific applications needed (like a code editor like Visual Studio Code).
3. **Communication Channels**: Familiarity with email and other forms of communication within your organization is recommended but not mandatory.

### Step-by-Step Onboarding:

**1. Welcome Meeting**
   - **Objective**: Introduce the team member to their role, responsibilities, expectations, and key contacts.
   - **Action Steps:**
     1. **Introduction**: Host an informal meet-and-greet at your workspace to welcome them.
     2. **Roles and Responsibilities**: Explain what they're expected to do on a daily basis and outline their main tasks and projects.
     3. **Expectations and Support**: Discuss what’s important and what might be challenging during the first few weeks, along with ways you can help them if needed (e.g., available mentors, virtual meetings).
     4. **Key Contacts**: List out who to reach out to directly in case of urgent questions.
   - **Verification**: Review notes from this meeting together to ensure everything was covered.

**2. Training Sessions**
   - **Objective**: Equip them with the knowledge and skills required to complete their tasks effectively.
   - **Action Steps:**
     1. **Workshop or Video Tutorial**: Attend training sessions or watch short videos covering essential topics related to the project (e.g., project planning, coding basics, or use cases).
     2. **Hands-On Practice**: If possible, schedule time for practical training where they can practice using tools or following through tasks directly under supervision.
   - **Verification**: Check if they understand by asking open-ended questions or giving simple tasks for them to try independently.

**3. Setup Their Workspace**
   - **Objective**: Ensure they're comfortable and efficient in their working environment.
   - **Action Steps:**
     1. **Software Installation/Setup**: Assist with installing necessary software and configuring settings as per guidelines provided.
     2. **Configuration Files**: Help configure any initial files or templates needed for specific programming environments.
     3. **Resource Access**: Make sure they've access to all necessary resources (files, databases, etc.) via shared folders or cloud storage services.
   - **Verification**: Confirm that everything is set up correctly and check logs or outputs regularly to make adjustments as needed.

**4. Communication Setup**
   - **Objective**: Establish clear lines of communication between the team member and the rest of the team.
   - **Action Steps:**
     1. **Set Up Direct Messages or Private Chats**: Use Slack or another private messaging tool so they can communicate with others privately.
     2. **Regular Check-ins**: Arrange regular one-on-one check-in calls every week or fortnight to discuss progress and address any issues immediately.
   - **Verification**: Observe if there are no delays or misunderstandings in communications over time.

**5. Performance Metrics**
   - **Objective**: Establish methods for tracking performance and providing feedback.
   - **Action Steps:**
     1. **Weekly Reviews**: Set aside time weekly to review progress against timelines and objectives.
     2. **Feedback Loop**: Create channels or spaces where they can share concerns or successes with the rest of the team.
     3. **Goal Setting**: Encourage setting individual goals and review progress towards these monthly or quarterly.
   - **Verification**: Track improvements made by the team member over time based on feedback.

### Final Note
Regularly review progress and adaptments throughout the onboarding process. Be flexible when problems arise, ensuring everyone feels supported and engaged.
