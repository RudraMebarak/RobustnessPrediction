After several hours spent staring blankly at my surroundings while attempting to piece together where I actually was, I finally decided to call out to the people nearby just to confirm where we were. And sure enough, there they stood: a group of local residents wearing funny hats and carrying antique furniture pieces that had clearly been sitting outside for a very long time.

"What are you guys doing here?" I asked, looking around myself as if I couldn't believe I was standing among strangers for whom I knew nothing whatsoever.

"Well," said one of them, "we're helping restore your town."
