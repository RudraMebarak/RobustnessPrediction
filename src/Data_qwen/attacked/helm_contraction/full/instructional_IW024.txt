### Beginner-Friendly Guide: Managing Competing Tasks with Multiple Deadlines

#### Prerequisites:
1. **Basic knowledge of project management**: Understanding what a project is, its goals, and how tasks contribute to achieving those goals.
2. **Time tracking skills**: Ability to keep track of time spent on each task without overestimating or underestimating.

### Steps to Manage Competing Tasks:

**Step 1: Identify the Goals (Project/Task)**
   - List out the individual tasks or goals associated with your project.
   - Assign each goal a priority level based on urgency (high, medium, low).

**Step 2: Set Deadlines for Each Task**
   - For each task, set specific deadlines. Prioritize these by urgency and importance.

**Step 3: Create a Timeline (Gantt Chart)**
   - Use a tool like Trello, Asana, or Google Sheets to create a timeline that visually represents the progress of all tasks related to your project.
   - This visual aid helps in identifying dependencies between tasks.

**Step 4: Assess Current Status**
   - Review where you currently stand against your deadline schedule using the Gantt chart.
   - Note down any tasks behind or ahead of schedule.

**Step 5: Plan Action Steps**
   - For any task behind the current deadline, decide exactly how much time it will take.
   - Break this down into actionable steps. For example, "Review project plans" takes about two hours, but realistically might be done in half an hour due to distractions.

**Step 6: Execute Action Plan**
   - Begin implementing the plan immediately after assessment. Focus on completing one action at a time.

**Step 7: Check Progress Regularly**
   - Track progress regularly (daily check-in meetings, scheduled updates).
   - Compare actual progress to scheduled progress to ensure everything stays aligned.

**Step 8: Adjust as Needed**
   - If adjustments are necessary, do them right away to avoid delays.

**Step 9: Celebrate Wins**
   - Acknowledge small successes along the way. These can boost morale and show progress towards overall goals.

### Verification
- **Weekly Reviews**: Hold regular meetings to discuss progress, challenges, and upcoming tasks.
- **Performance Metrics**: Keep records of how long tasks took to complete and compare against estimated times.
- **Feedback Loop**: Solicit feedback from team members regularly; their input could highlight areas needing adjustment.

### Troubleshooting Potential Issues
1. **Overwhelmed by Too Much Work**: Divide big projects into smaller chunks. Start by handling manageable pieces first, gradually increasing complexity.
2. **Lack of Tools**: Consider upgrading to a comprehensive project management software or try simpler tools such as pen and paper initially.
3. **Feeling Stressed or Burnt Out**: Implement breaks every hour for short walks or mindfulness exercises.
4. **Miscommunication**: Ensure clear communication among team members throughout project execution.
5. **Resource Constraints**: Allocate time slots optimally and reassess resource allocation as needed.

By following these steps systematically and flexibly adjusting as required, managing multiple deadlines becomes more manageable and less stressful. Remember, the key is to remain focused on delivering quality results within given timelines while also respecting personal and professional limits. Happy planning!
