### 🧠 Tips for Joining a New Group

- **Prepare Yourself**: Reflect on what excites and bores you most about the group or activity beforehand.
- **Be Positive About Yourself**: Focus on your unique qualities and talents rather than trying to fit in with others’ expectations.
- **Communicate Gracefully**: Learn active listening and constructive feedback—both are valuable in any setting!
- **Give Feedback Constructively**: Offer suggestions and criticisms with kindness, aiming to improve rather than point fingers.

- **Escape Room Nights**: Test your puzzle-solving skills against friends.
- **Book Swap Bash**: Share your favorite reads and swap books with other enthusiasts.
- **DIY Workshop Series**: Host activities like knitting, painting, cooking from scratch—let creativity run wild together.
