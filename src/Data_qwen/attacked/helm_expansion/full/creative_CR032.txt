Title: The Apothecary's Secret

Alex had always been fascinated by the stories she heard about secret societies operating out of abandoned buildings or underground rooms. But nothing could have prepared her for what awaited her on the fourth floor of her apartment complex.

As soon as she entered the building, she noticed something strange. The elevator did not stop at every level, but only reached three others - levels two through four. The rest appeared to be inaccessible.

At first, Alex thought it was just coincidence. She assumed the missing floors were simply unoccupied apartments. However, as time went by, she began to notice odd occurrences around her. There seemed to be a group of mysterious individuals present throughout the day who wore long robes draped over their shoulders and carried dark candles in hand.

Despite trying hard to blend into the crowd, Alex knew there had to be more to these people than met the eye. When she eventually discovered them, they revealed themselves to be a secret society dedicated to preserving ancient apothecaries' recipes for potions and elixirs.

However, things quickly turned dangerous when Alex came across the secret entrance to the fourth floor. Despite the oppressive atmosphere of the place and the strict rules imposed upon members, the society welcomed her with open arms. They told her that if she wanted to stay alive, she would need to become one of them.

Initially, Alex balked at the idea of becoming a vampire. After all, she was perfectly fine as she was. But the pull of loyalty towards friends and family made her question everything she thought she knew. So she agreed to join.

Over time, Alex learned the ways of vampirism. She became acutely aware of how different life could be from a nightmarish dream to waking up among the undead. Each morning brought new challenges as she navigated her way between the living world and her newfound nocturnal existence.

Despite its sinister allure, being part of a secret society also brought unexpected perks. For instance, she became privy to some very valuable information regarding the future apocalypse which caused chaos amongst the community. 

All in all, Alex managed to survive as both a human and a vampire. Eventually, she realized that joining the secret society was a choice driven by the need to protect those whom she loved most deeply and cherish every moment of every day.
