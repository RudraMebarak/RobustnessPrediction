In this new universe, there are two types of dragon libraries: Dragon Books (DBs) and Dragon Manuscripts (DMs). DBs are made up of magical words and DMs are made up of enchanted pages. Humans are not allowed to use magic in the library but can interact with other human users.

The rules for using these libraries are as follows:
1. To enter the Dragon Books or Manuscripts Library (DB/DM), a user must have completed the required spell-casting training.
2. Once inside the library, users are unable to take any books out by themselves.
3. Users cannot borrow multiple copies of the same book at once.
4. The library does not display an expiration date or limit on how long one can stay within it.

2. **Half-dragon Knight**
   - **Appearance**: A blend of dragon and knight attire; scales covering most of his body and armor protecting his limbs.
   - **Abilities**: Can summon earth-based magical weapons (like swords or axes) when casting certain spells.

3. **Dwarf Scholar**
   - **Appearance**: Stocky build with thick horns protruding from his head.
   - **Abilities**: Can command and move massive stones without breaking them into pieces. He possesses an incredible memory for arcane knowledge.

4. **Faun Scholar**
   - **Appearance**: With sharp antlers, tufted fur, and large ears; walks on four legs similar to a deer but wears wizard robes.
   - **Abilities**: Can manipulate plant life and healing elements around him through spoken word or chants.

5. **Gnome Writer**
   - **Appearance**: Small and delicate, usually dressed in wizardly clothes often featuring pointed hats and cloaks.
   - **Abilities**: Can write and sculpt words on surfaces instantly (e.g., walls, stone tablets) whenever needed. Also skilled in crafting miniature sculptures or amulets.
