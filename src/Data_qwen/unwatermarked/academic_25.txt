Introduction:

Blockchain technology has been gaining popularity due to its unique properties such as decentralization, transparency, and immutability. It is already being used across various industries like finance, healthcare, and energy. However, there are still limited studies on how it can be utilized in supply chain management.

Objective:
The objective of this research is to investigate the potential benefits and drawbacks of using blockchain technology in supply chain management. The study will examine different aspects of supply chain operations such as inventory tracking, payment processing, and logistics. Additionally, we aim to identify challenges that organizations face while implementing blockchain solutions for supply chain management.

Methodology:

The proposed methodology involves conducting interviews with key stakeholders from companies involved in supply chain management. These stakeholders include executives from manufacturing firms, distributors, retailers, and logistics providers. We will also conduct surveys among end-users to gather feedback on their experiences with blockchain-based supply chain systems.

Data Collection:

The data collection process will involve collecting primary and secondary data through interviews, focus groups, and surveys. Primary data will be collected by conducting individual interviews with key informants who have experience working with blockchain technology in supply chain management. Secondary data will be collected from academic journals, industry reports, and expert opinions.

Analysis:

The analysis of data will follow a mixed-methods approach. Qualitative data will be analyzed through content analysis techniques such as coding, categorizing, and theme identification. Quantitative data will be analyzed using descriptive statistics and regression models to identify correlations between variables.

Expected Outcomes:

This research aims to provide insights into the effectiveness of blockchain technology in supply chain management. The findings will help organizations make informed decisions regarding the implementation of blockchain-based solutions. Furthermore, the study will contribute to the existing literature on blockchain technology and supply chain management, thereby filling an important gap in the field.

Conclusion:

In conclusion, this research proposes to explore the use of blockchain technology in supply chain management. By examining the potential benefits and drawbacks, we aim to provide valuable insights into the adoption of blockchain-based systems. This research will not only benefit companies but also society at large by improving the efficiency and security of supply chains worldwide.
