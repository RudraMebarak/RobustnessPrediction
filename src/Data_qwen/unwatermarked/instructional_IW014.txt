### How to Follow a Recipe While Adjusting Quantities for More Servings

- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and sliced

- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 2 cups baby spinach leaves
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Fresh herbs like parsley or basil (optional)
- Optional spices like paprika or cumin (to taste)

   - **For Vegetables:** Increase them proportionally based on the original quantity per person.
