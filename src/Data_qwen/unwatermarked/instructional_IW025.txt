1. Urgency-Priority Grid:
The Urgency-Priority Grid is a simple yet powerful tool that categorizes tasks based on their urgency and importance levels. It consists of two columns - Urgent & Important and Not Urgent but Important. Tasks are placed accordingly.

Urgent & Important -> These tasks require immediate attention and should be tackled right away. They often involve high-priority issues or deadlines that cannot be delayed.

Not Urgent but Important -> These tasks have important outcomes but aren't necessarily urgent. They might include tasks related to career growth, personal development, or long-term projects.

2. Eisenhower Box Method:
Eisenhower Box Method is another popular technique used to prioritize tasks. It involves dividing tasks into four categories: Immediate Important, Important but not immediate, Not important but immediate, and Neither important nor immediate. Each category gets its own box:

Immediate Important -> Handle these immediately. These are urgent and require immediate attention.

Important but not immediate -> Schedule these tasks for later. They might be important but not urgent, requiring careful consideration before scheduling.

Not important but immediate -> Avoid doing these unless absolutely necessary. They represent low-priority tasks that can wait.

Neither important nor immediate -> Let these go. Don't waste time on them; they're neither important nor urgent.
