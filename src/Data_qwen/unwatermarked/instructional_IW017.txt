**Seasonal Vegetable Soup Recipe Guide**

### Prerequisites
- A large pot (at least 6-quart capacity)
- Knife and cutting board
- Large bowl or sink for washing vegetables
- Colander
- Cutting board and knife for chopping
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Wooden spoon or spatula
- Pot holders

### Ingredients (for one serving):
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: herbs like parsley, chives, or dill for garnish

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Prepare the Vegetables
1. Wash all the vegetables under cold running water. Peel carrots if needed.
2. Slice the carrots into thin rounds.
3. Finely chop the onion and mince the garlic.

#### Step 2: Sauté the Onions and Garlic
1. In a large pot, heat about 2 tablespoons of oil over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onions and sauté until they become translucent, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant but not browned.

#### Step 3: Cook the Carrots
1. Pour in the vegetable broth and add the diced tomatoes.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low and let simmer gently for about 10 minutes.

#### Step 4: Add the Seasonings
1. Stir in the thyme, bay leaf, salt, and pepper. Simmer for an additional 5 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.

#### Step 5: Finish with Water
1. Add enough water to reach at least half-way up the sides of the pot. The soup will continue to simmer as you prepare the rest of your ingredients.

#### Step 6: Chop Your Vegetables
1. While the soup simmers, carefully slice the remaining carrots into thin strips or cubes, depending on their size.

#### Step 7: Combine Everything
1. Once the carrots are ready, transfer them to the pot along with any juices that have accumulated around them.
2. Stir everything together and bring the soup back to a gentle simmer. Adjust seasoning if necessary.

#### Step 8: Serve Warm
1. Ladle the soup into bowls and serve hot. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

### Verification:
To ensure your soup is cooked properly, check the carrots; they should be tender yet still retain some crunch. Taste it for seasoning consistency before serving.

### Variations:
- **Herbed Version**: Add fresh or dried herbs like rosemary, basil, or oregano during the last few minutes of cooking.
- **Spicy Variation**: Increase the amount of chili powder or cayenne pepper when adding the tomato paste.
- **Vegan Option**: Replace animal broth with vegetable broth and use vegan butter instead of oil.
- **Gluten-Free**: Ensure all ingredients used are gluten-free unless stated otherwise.
