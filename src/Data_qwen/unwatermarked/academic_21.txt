However, it's important to note that other factors such as age, gender, personality traits, and individual preferences also play significant roles in determining attention span (Sutton & Krawczyk, 2018).
Previous studies have supported these predictions. For example, a meta-analysis conducted by Hertzog et al. These findings suggest that while the link between social media usage and attention span has been established, further investigation is needed to understand the extent of the relationship and explore possible mechanisms behind it.
