**How to Recognize and Report Suspicious Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are one of the most common ways cybercriminals try to steal your personal information or trick you into revealing sensitive details. Here’s how to identify them and what to do if you receive one:

### Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of computers (if you're new to tech)
- A computer with internet access

### Step 1: Identify Signs of a Phishing Email

1. **Unusual Sender:** Check the sender's address carefully. Legitimate companies typically use their official domain names.
2. **Urgent Requests:** Phishers often demand quick action like "urgently" log in or click on links.
3. **Suspicious Links:** Hover over links; they might look different from usual.
4. **Unclear Language:** Avoid using jargon or overly formal language in emails.
5. **Strange Formatting:** Unusual font sizes or styles can indicate spam.

### Step 2: Verify Information Before Acting

1. **Check URL:** Use the browser’s “View Source” option to see where the link leads. It should match the actual website.
2. **Look at Header:** The header will show the sender’s name and company. If it seems fake, it is likely a scam.

### Step 3: Respond Appropriately

1. **Do Not Click:** Never open attachments or click on links provided in an unsolicited email.
2. **Delete Immediately:** If unsure about the email, delete it without opening anything inside.

### Step 4: Report Suspicious Activity

1. **Use Email Security Tools:** Many devices come with built-in security features that can flag potentially dangerous messages.
2. **Report to the Company:** Most businesses have customer service numbers for such issues.
   - Example: *If you suspect a bank email:* Go to the bank’s website, find help, then contact fraud department.
   
3. **Contact Authorities:** If you’re still unsure or if the issue isn’t resolved quickly, call local law enforcement:
   - Contact your nearest police station.
   - In some cases, there could also be dedicated IT hotlines specific to phishing scams.

### Verification of Completion

You've successfully recognized a phishing email by following these steps. Always remember to stay vigilant and keep your digital habits safe!
