The sun was setting over the small town square as Sarah stepped out of her old car onto the sidewalk. She had been running late for work all morning, but she couldn't shake the feeling that something was off. As she walked towards the bank where she worked, she noticed a man standing on the opposite side of the street.

"Hi there," he said, waving at her. "What are you doing here?"

Sarah froze, then slowly turned around to face him. It was Mark, a local artist who lived across the street from her apartment building. He always seemed so different when he wasn't drawing or painting - now his eyes were bright with excitement and he looked like he might burst into song if it weren't for his thick glasses perched precariously on his nose.

"I'm just getting my lunch," Sarah explained, trying not to let her voice tremble too much. "Are you okay? You seem... different."

Mark laughed heartily. "Oh, I'm fine! Just thinking about some new art project I've got lined up for next week." He gestured towards his notebook stuffed full of sketches. "I thought we could maybe meet up later this afternoon and talk more about it?"

As Sarah listened to Mark chat away, her mind began to wander back to earlier in the day. That morning, after dropping off her daughter at school, she had been driving home along the same route she usually took. The trees lining the road had grown taller since she last saw them, and she could almost feel her breath hitch as she passed by the house she used to live next door to. There were no signs of life inside though - only empty rooms filled with dust and shadows.

When she pulled into her parking space at the end of the driveway, she realized she'd made an error. She hadn't taken any photos of anything interesting on her trip down this familiar path, but there had definitely been a change. Something had happened between those trees, somewhere between the cars parked along the curb and the houses beyond. A moment of quiet realization washed through her, followed by a rush of fear.

"Wait!" shouted Sarah, reaching out to grab Mark's arm before he could leave. "Can you come look at this with me?"

When Sarah came home from work, she found his notebook open on her kitchen table, pages crumpled and yellowing with age.
