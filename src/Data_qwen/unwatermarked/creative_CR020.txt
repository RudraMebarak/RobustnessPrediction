In this unique world, the land is divided into vast rainforests and deserts, with no roads or cities visible from above. The mapmaker's name is Alex, and he has been working tirelessly to create a comprehensive atlas of all known countries on Earth. His latest project involves mapping out an entirely new continent called "Aquapolis," which seems to exist solely during the rainy season. As Alex delves deeper into his research, he begins to notice strange phenomena occurring within the region.

One day, while exploring the dense rainforest, Alex stumbles upon a hidden waterfall that cascades over a cliff face straight into the sky. He marvels at the sight and realizes that the water never actually reaches the ground but instead disappears into thin air. This observation prompts him to start investigating further.

As he investigates more closely, Alex finds evidence of a massive underground reservoir beneath the forest floor. When it rains heavily, the water pressure forces its way up through cracks in the rock formations, creating a temporary city-like structure that floats just above the surface. These cities are composed almost entirely of water, with occasional wooden structures protruding here and there as makeshift shelters.

Alex soon learns that these floating cities have a complex system of transportation and communication networks built into them. People can travel between different parts of Aquapolis by simply walking across bridges made of stone and wood, using boats powered by wind energy, or even flying through the air using specially designed wings attached to their bodies.

However, life in Aquapolis isn't without its challenges. There are limited resources due to the fragile nature of the floating cities themselves. Many inhabitants rely on fishing expeditions along nearby rivers and streams to survive, though they must be careful not to disturb the delicate balance of aquatic ecosystems below.

Alex also notices that each morning, a new floating city appears in the sky, while other areas remain submerged under murky waters. He hypothesizes that this phenomenon might be linked to some kind of natural cycle or pattern that governs the formation and disappearance of these floating cities.

Over time, Alex develops a keen understanding of how Aquapolis operates. He maps out detailed routes for travelers navigating the region, noting the best times of year to visit and potential hazards one might encounter. With each discovery, Alex becomes increasingly fascinated by the unique ecosystem of Aquapolis and strives to document everything meticulously.

### Human Resource Management (HRM) and Promoting Diversity and Inclusion

#### Introduction
Diversity and inclusion (D&I) in the workplace refer to practices aimed at fostering environments where individuals from diverse backgrounds feel valued, respected, and able to contribute effectively. Human Resource Management (HRM), being integral to organizational success, plays a pivotal role in implementing strategies that promote D&I. Effective HRM can significantly enhance employee engagement, productivity, innovation, and overall business performance by ensuring that everyone feels included and supported.

#### Key Functions of HRM in Promoting Diversity and Inclusion

1. **Policy Development and Compliance**
   - **Establishing Policies**: HRM ensures that the organization’s policies reflect a commitment to diversity and inclusion. This includes anti-discrimination policies, equal opportunity employment guidelines, and fair hiring practices.
   - **Compliance Monitoring**: Regular audits and reviews ensure adherence to legal requirements related to D&I, such as Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, and Equal Pay Act.

2. **Recruitment and Selection Processes**
   - **Diverse Recruitment Channels**: Utilizing diverse recruitment channels such as social media platforms, targeted advertising campaigns, partnerships with community organizations, and open forums helps attract candidates from various backgrounds.
   - **Standardized Assessment Methods**: Implementing standardized tests and assessments that do not discriminate based on race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation
