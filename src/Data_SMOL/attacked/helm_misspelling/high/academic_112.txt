1. **Objective:** 
To determine the degree to which students in rural secondary schools have access to digital resources and the factors that influence their digital access.

2. **Target Population:** 
Students enrolled in grades 9-12 in rural secondary schools.

3. **Sampling Strategy:**
a. **Sampling Frame:** 
The sampling frame includes all students in grades 9-12 in rural secondary schools within the specified geographic area.
b. **Sampling Method:** 
1. **Stratified Random Sampling:** 
Divide the rural secondary schools into strata based on factors such as school size, socioeconomic status, and distance from urban areas. 
2. **Simple Random Sampling:** 
Within each stratum, select a random sample of students to participate in the study.
c. **Sample Size:**
