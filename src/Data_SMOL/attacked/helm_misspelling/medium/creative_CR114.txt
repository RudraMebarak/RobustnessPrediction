3. **Expert Panel Review (Weeks 5-8):**
	* A specialized expert panel is convened to review the preliminary assessment report and provide expert opinions on the dream's feasibility and potential risks.

4. **Cost-Benefit Analysis (Weeks 9-10):**
	* A team of economists and financial analysts conducts a thorough cost-benefit analysis of the dream, weighing the potential benefits against the costs, risks, and societal impact.

5. **Final Approval (Weeks 11-12):**
	* Based on the public discussion, expert panel review, and cost-benefit analysis, the TTB issues a final recommendation to the governing body.
	* If the recommendation is approved, the TTB will establish protocols and regulations for the transformation process, including safety measures, licensing requirements, and oversight mechanisms.
6. **Implementation (Weeks 13+):**
	* Once the governing body approves the recommendation, the TTB will begin implementing the transformation process.
	* This involves establishing guidelines, training programs for healthcare professionals and individuals, and establishing a system for monitoring and evaluating the process.
