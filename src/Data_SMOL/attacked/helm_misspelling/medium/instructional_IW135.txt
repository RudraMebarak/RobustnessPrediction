Prerequisites: 
1. Knowledge of the label's contents and usage instructions (which can be found on the label itself).
2. Basic understanding of safe handling and storage of cleaning products.

Sequence of actions:
1. Gather necessary cleaning supplies (cleaning products, necessary tools like gloves, and cleaning materials).
2. Read and follow the label's instructions for the cleaning product you are about to use (check the concentration, mixing requirements if any, and storage information).
3. Put on gloves to protect your hands.
4. Measure the cleaning product and pour it into a clean container (if required).
5. Apply the cleaning product as instructed to the affected area (following any specific directions for application).
6. Rinse the affected area as instructed in the label (if required).
7. Disinfect and clean as instructed in the label (if required).
8. Dispose of any cleaning materials and cleaning products according to their label's instructions (proper disposal methods vary).

Verification of completion:
1. Check that the cleaning product has been applied as instructed to the affected area.
2. Verify that the cleaning product has been rinsed and cleaned as instructed in the label.
3. Ensure that any cleaning materials and cleaning products have been disposed of according to thier label's instructions.

Please be aware that this is a general guide and may vary depending on the specific cleaning product being used. Always consult the label or manufacturer's instructions for the safest and most effective way to use the product. 

**Safety Reminder:** Never mix cleaning products unless the manufacturer's instructions say you can. Some products can be mixed, but many can cause dangerous reactions. If you are unsure whether a particular product can be mixed, consult with a professional or the manufacturer.

**Example:** 
For a general-purpose cleaner with the following label: 
- Warning: Do not mix with ammonia or bleach.
- Use: Spray or wipe surfaces clean.
- Safety Data Sheet (SDS) or manufacturer's instructions: For further information.

You would follow these steps:
1. Gather the necessary supplies (cleaning products, gloves, cleaning materials).
2. Read and follow the label's instructions (you may not be able to mix with ammonia or bleach).
3. Put on gloves.
4. Measure and pour the cleaning product into a clean container (if required).
5. Spray or wipe surfaces clean as instructed in the label.
6. Rinse surfaces as instructed in the label (if required).
7. Disinfect surfaces as instructed in the label (if required).
8. Dispose of the cleaning materials and cleaning product according to their label's instructions (proper disposal methods vary).

Remember to always follow the label's instructions and handle cleaning products safely. If you are unsure about any step, consult with a professional or the manufacturer's instructions.
