the letter should contain at least three references to their childhood memories and include a nostalgic tone. format: choose one from options: handwritten, typed, or printed.

handwritten letter

october 25, 2023

dear emily,

i hope this letter finds you well. it's been a lifetime since i last saw you, and as i sit down to write, memories of our childhood flood my mind. you know, when we were kids, we would spend hours playing in the park, our laughter echoing through the trees. it's as if time stood still for us back then.

do you remember our backyard, where we built a treehouse? i used to climb it until it felt like it was calling my name. and the time you discovered the old trunk in the attic and found a treasure chest hidden inside? it felt like we were on an adventure.

i was thinking about you the other day, reminiscing about those days. i miss the way you used to play dress-up with me, pretending to be a princess or a superhero. it's incredible how much you've grown, yet the spark in your eyes, the same spark i saw as a little girl, remains.

i wish i could be there with you now, holding you tight as we did back then. life has been treating me quite differently, and i find myself reflecting on the journey we've both taken. i often wonder what you're up to, what you dream of, and if you ever think about us.

please know that i'm always here, just like i promised back then. i hope you're doing well and that your heart is as full as it once was. write back when you can, and i promise i'll do the same.

with warm memories,

oliver
