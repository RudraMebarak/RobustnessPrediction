prerequisites: 
1. ensure safety measures are in place, such as proper fire extinguisher location and training.
2. have an escape route and a way out of the kitchen identified.
3. avoid using water or any flammable materials.

steps to respond:

step 1: stay calm - remain composed and do not panic.

step 2: turn off the heat - locate and shut off the heat source immediately.

step 3: use a fire extinguisher - select a suitable fire extinguisher (b or c rating) and approach the fire at a safe distance.

step 4: aim at the base - aim the extinguisher at the base of the fire and sweep it from side to side.

step 5: avoid using water - never use water or any flammable materials to extinguish the fire.

step 6: keep a safe distance - stay a safe distance from the fire, as it could reignite.

step 7: evacuate the kitchen - if the fire persists or you cannot control it, evacuate the kitchen immediately.

step 8: call emergency services - call 911 and provide the operator with the location and a brief description of the fire.

verification:
1. ensure the fire is fully out and there is no sign of heat.
2. double-check that you have turned off the heat source.
3. verify the extinguisher was used correctly and no water or flammable materials were used.
4. check for any injuries and provide first aid if necessary.

additional tips:

- practice fire safety drills regularly.
- keep fire extinguishers easily accessible and regularly inspected.
- avoid overcrowding the kitchen and keep a clean workspace to prevent grease buildup.
- keep a fire blanket or towel nearby as an alternative to a fire extinguisher. 

remember, the key to responding to a grease fire safely is to remain calm and follow the correct procedure. taking unnecessary risks can only worsen the situation and put you and others at risk.
