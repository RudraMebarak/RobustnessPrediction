**data collection method: interviews**

we plan to conduct semi-structured interviews with 20 university students (10 males and 10 females) who have experienced barriers to accessing mental health services. the interviews will be conducted in-person or via phone/video call, depending on the student's preference.

we will use a standardized interview guide to ensure consistency in the data collection process. the interview guide will cover the following topics:

1. demographic information (e.g., age, year of study, academic major)
2. reasons for seeking mental health services (e.g., feeling overwhelmed, experiencing anxiety)
3. barriers to accessing mental health services (e.g., stigma, lack of awareness, difficulty finding services)
4. barriers related to university policies and resources (e.g., waiting lists, limited counseling services)
5. recommendations for improving mental health service access and resources on campus

we will also collect data on the students' mental health status (e.g., current diagnosis, severity of symptoms) and their perceived level of support from their university and community.

interviews will be recorded and transcribed to ensure accurate data collection. we will also obtain informed consent from participants and ensure confidentiality and data protection.

we will use the data collected from these interviews to:

* understand the barriers to mental health service access among university students
* identify potential solutions to address these barriers
* develop targeted interventions to improve mental health service access and resources on campus

**data analysis plan**

we will use thematic analysis to identify patterns and themes in the interview data. we will also use descriptive statistics to summarize the demographic and mental health data.

to ensure the validity and reliability of our findings, we will:

* pilot-test the interview guide with a small group of participants
* train interviewers to ensure consistent data collection
* use multiple data sources (e.g., interviews, administrative data) to triangulate findings

we expect to collect a total of 20 interviews, with a response rate of 100%. the data will be analyzed using a qualitative research approach, with a focus on understanding the lived experiences of university students who face barriers to mental health service access.
