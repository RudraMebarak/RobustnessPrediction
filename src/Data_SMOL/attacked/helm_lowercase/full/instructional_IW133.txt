**title: emergency contact and essentials checklist for your household**

**introduction**
disasters happen unexpectedly, leaving families vulnerable. a well-prepared household minimizes risks, ensures safety, and supports recovery. this guide will walk you through creating an emergency contact and essentials checklist.

**prerequisites**
1. gather family members and agree on responsibilities.
2. define your community's hazard risks (e.g., earthquakes, hurricanes).

**step-by-step guide**
1. **form a team:**
   - **objective:** create a list of family members and assign tasks.
   - **actions:**
     - schedule a meeting to discuss responsibilities.
     - create a list of family members and their roles (e.g., phone contact, medical info, communication).
     - agree on a primary and secondary phone number for each member.
   - **check:** ensure every family member's role is documented and known by all.

2. **create the essentials list:**
   - **objective:** gather necessary items for sheltering-in-place and evacuation.
   - **actions:**
     - gather a first-aid kit (basic supplies like bandages, pain relievers).
     - collect non-perishable food items (e.g., canned goods, dried fruits).
     - include a battery-powered radio or tv for news updates.
     - stock up on water (1 gallon per person per day).
     - gather a multi-tool or portable power source (e.g., solar charger).
   - **check:** verify all essential items are easily accessible and functional.

3. **organize the list:**
   - **objective:** make the list easily accessible.
   - **actions:**
     - create a binder or folder for the checklist.
     - label sections for phone numbers, first-aid, food, and water.
   - **check:** ensure all sections are clearly labeled and easily found.

4. **practice the plan:**
   - **objective:** familiarize the household with the plan.
   - **actions:**
     - conduct a drill to simulate evacuation or sheltering-in-place.
     - assign specific roles during the drill.
     - gather all necessary items for the drill.
   - **check:** conduct a drill at least once a year to maintain readiness.

5. **verify completion:**
   - **objective:** confirm everything is ready to go.
   - **actions:**
     - review the checklist with all family members.
     - confirm all essential items are stocked and accessible.
     - ensure all phone numbers are current and known by all.
   - **check:** complete the checklist and review it to ensure everything is ready.

**conclusion**
preparation is key in emergency situations. by following these steps, you will create a checklist that ensures your household's safety and readiness. regularly review and practice the plan to maintain preparedness and confidence.

**additional resources**
- **fema's emergency preparedness guide:** provides more detailed information on creating a household emergency plan.
- **american red cross:** offers training and resources for disaster preparedness.

by following this guide, you will be better equipped to handle emergencies and protect your family. stay informed, stay prepared, and stay safe! 

this guide should be revised as family members and community hazards change over time. regular updates will help maintain a responsive plan that meets the evolving needs of your household and community.
