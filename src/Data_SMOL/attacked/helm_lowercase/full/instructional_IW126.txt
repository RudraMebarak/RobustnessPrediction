prerequisites: 
1. knowledge of the student organization's platform (e.g., facebook, discord, or slack)
2. understanding of basic communication principles
3. familiarity with the platform's features and navigation

guide steps:
1. **understand the organization's goals and mission**
   - start by reviewing the organization's constitution, mission statement, and goals to gain a clear understanding of what you are trying to achieve.

2. **define the guide's purpose**
   - based on the organization's goals, determine the specific tasks and information you want to include in the guide.

3. **organize the content**
   - create a list of the information and tasks that need to be covered in the guide. group similar topics together to make navigation easier.

4. **create the first draft**
   - write a first draft of each section, making sure it is concise and to the point. use bullet points or numbered lists to make the information easily digestible.

5. **add visual aids**
   - incorporate relevant images, videos, or charts to enhance understanding and make the guide more engaging.

6. **test and revise**
   - share the guide with a few trusted members for feedback. make revisions as necessary to ensure the information is clear and helpful.

7. **publish the guide**
   - once satisfied with the guide, publish it in a visible and easily accessible location on the platform (e.g., facebook, discord, or slack).

8. **promote the guide**
   - share the guide with new members through various channels (e.g., emails, group chats, or announcements) to ensure maximum visibility.

verification steps:
1. **check for clarity**
   - ensure that all sections are easy to understand and free of jargon.

2. **test navigation**
   - verify that the guide's navigation is intuitive and that users can find the information they need without confusion.

3. **assess the guide's accuracy**
   - double-check all information for accuracy and completeness.

4. **get feedback**
   - collect feedback from a small group of new members to ensure the guide meets their needs and expectations.

5. **iterate**
   - based on feedback, make any necessary revisions to the guide.

6. **finalize and publish**
   - once satisfied with the guide and the feedback, finalize and publish it on the platform.

note: this guide can be adjusted based on the organization's specific needs and platform features. regularly updating the guide as new members join or as the organization evolves will be important to maintain its relevance and usefulness. 

---

**tip: keep it simple, friendly, and engaging** – new members want to know what to expect and how to contribute, not get overwhelmed with information. break down tasks into manageable steps, and use motivational language to encourage participation and success. 

---

**bonus: use templates or tools**
- many platforms have built-in tools or templates for creating guides (e.g., facebook's groups, discord's server info, or slack's workspaces).
- consider using a pre-made template to save time and ensure consistency.
- many organizations use online guide creators (e.g., canva, google forms) for a visually appealing and interactive guide.

---

**bonus: use keywords**
- when creating the guide, incorporate keywords related to the organization's goals and members' interests (e.g., "new member introduction," "get involved," "join our team").
- this will help the guide be more discoverable when new members search for information.

---

**bonus: use consistent language**
- use the same terminology and language throughout the guide to avoid confusion.
- this consistency will help new members quickly understand the organization's language and culture.

---

**bonus: use visual hierarchy**
- use headings, bullet points, and subheadings to create a visual hierarchy and make the guide easy to scan.
- this will help new members find the information they need quickly and easily.

---

**bonus: use call-to-actions**
- encourage new members to engage with the guide and participate in the organization's activities.
- use phrases like "get involved," "join our team," or "join a committee" to prompt action.

---

**bonus: use a feedback loop**
- create a system for new members to provide feedback on the guide and the organization as a whole.
- this will ensure the guide stays relevant and helpful over time.

---

**bonus: use consistent branding**
- use the organization's brand (logo, colors, fonts) consistently throughout the guide.
- this will create a cohesive visual identity and reinforce the organization's values and mission.

---

**bonus: use interactive elements**
- incorporate interactive elements like quizzes, polls, or discussion prompts to engage new members and encourage participation.
- this will make the guide more engaging and help new members feel connected to the organization.
