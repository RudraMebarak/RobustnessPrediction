During her morning nap, a small cloud named Lulu awoke. She was wrapped in her arms and legs and felt the wind blowing through her legs and into the air. She knew she had the power to become a mountain, and as she fought to hold her own, she felt like she was flying high above the clouds. With the wind in her hair and the sun rising above her face, Lulu knew that she had made a decision. She became a mountain, and the rest of the clouds couldn't take it anymore. As she stood, she realized that she was no longer just a tiny cloud, and she was the tallest thing she had ever seen. But as she looked at others, she saw that she wasn't alone. She was now ready to take on any challenges or embark on a journey that could take her beyond just her fluffy clouds.
