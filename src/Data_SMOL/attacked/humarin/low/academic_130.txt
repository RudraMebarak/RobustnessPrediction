How do low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellations differ in terms of their impact on communication services and how do they impact global coverage gaps? LEO satellites have a limited lifespan due to atmospheric drag and debris, necessitating regular maintenance and replacement to maintain service continuity. LEO satellite constellations are a source of competition in the geopolitical arena, where countries compete to control and create satellite constellation networks. Furthermore, there are regulatory hurdles, such as international cooperation, spectrum management, and cybersecurity concerns, as well as potential issues with satellite spying and hacking that can impact national security and data integrity.
