I felt a sense of homelessness as I walked through the gated area of the festival, surrounded by the vibrant colors and raucous music. The sun shone through the aisles, and I marveled at the colorful displays of spices, jewelry, and local crafts. I also found myself lost to the familiar rhythm of people dancing to each beat. I missed the comfort of my home and the sense of belonging I had built up around me. As I walked through the gates of the festival, I realized that I couldn't replicate the magic of the music, lights, and community that I had experienced so far. I felt a sense of wonder and excitement as I played with people and their music. As the sun sets over the festival grounds, you feel like you've been playing for hours, and the crowd is roaring with delight and a smile on your face. Afterward, you look around, admiring the smiling faces, and admire the Ferris wheel's seats with gleaming rays. You feel enthralled and astounded, as if you were a part of something truly magical.
