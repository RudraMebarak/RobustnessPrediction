Reporting a Public Infrastructure Issue: What Is It? Don't panic, there's more than one way to go about it. When reporting an issue with the City Public Works, it's important to identify the authority. Gather information, such as the street name, City (Springfield), State (CA), date (10:00 AM), and phone number to follow up. Review your email for confirmation and if necessary, ask for pictures. Check the "Report a Problem" or "311 Service" link on the city/county website for assistance. If you can't wait for a technician to arrive, you'll be glad that it was resolved quickly. Use official city websites with a "Report a Problem" or "311 Service" link to report any issues with your city's website. --- **Final Note:** These steps can be used to resolve any issues you may have had with public infrastructure.
