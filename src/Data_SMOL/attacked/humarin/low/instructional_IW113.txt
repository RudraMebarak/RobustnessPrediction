To ensure proper maintenance of your tools, examine them thoroughly for signs of damage, including nicks, dents, and scratches, and disassemble them thoroughly if necessary. If necessary, you may need to disassemble the tools themselves with a soft cloth or wire brush to remove any small debris. If you disassemble tools with plastic or rubber handles, clean them thoroughly and remove any debris that may get smudged against the surface, as well as keep the tools safe from rust or corrosion by storing them in a dry location. To maintain the longevity and effectiveness of your hand tools, it is important to regularly clean and store them regularly. To do so, consider investing in a tool chest or dedicated storage area to ensure your tools are well-maintained and can be easily cleaned. To clean wooden handles, use a soft cloth or sponge to clean them with a small amount of wood cleaner, taking care not to get water into them or onto the metal parts.
