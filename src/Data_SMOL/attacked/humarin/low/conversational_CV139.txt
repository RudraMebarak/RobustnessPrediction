The story of John's unsuccessful attempts at setting up a computer network for his small business is akin to the unexpected, as his teenage daughter Emily convinced him to do it. Despite the challenges of setting up a business on a computer network, Emily managed to find a tutorial that actually worked. After discovering it, she followed the steps like a pro. John was taken aback when Emily's phone line received the signal and the connections remained unaffected. As they laughed together, John realized that sometimes you need a fresh perspective. Perhaps he should listen to his children more often.
