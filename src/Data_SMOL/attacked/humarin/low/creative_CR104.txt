This prompt encourages imaginative writing and world-building. There are those who claim that on a clear night, you can hear the whispers of history, carried by the winds that howl through the ancient corridors of Mount Elyria, carry with you the memories of those who came before you, the rocks that are a reminder of the joys and sorrows of coming back to earth, and a piece of wisdom that will remain with you as you depart, a living testament to the magic of the mountain, whether in fantasy, adventure or historical fiction. The prompt can be customized to accommodate different genres.
