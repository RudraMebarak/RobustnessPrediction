RAG systems aim to enhance the efficiency and performance of natural language processing tasks by incorporating retrieval mechanisms into the training or inference process. These systems consist of a retrieval mechanism that retrieves relevant information from pre-trained knowledge sources, and a generation mechanism that generates human-like text based on user input. The training phase involves training the model by exposing it to the knowledge base, which aids in understanding the context and generating more accurate responses. MTL is an essential component of training RAG systems as it enables the retrieval head to learn how to retrieve relevant information and integrate it with the pre-trained model's output.
