Explain the construction of these codes and their role in detecting and correcting errors. Using a non-binary approach, Reed-Solomon codes can add symbols to the original data stream. These symbols are calculated using a polynomial function and are then used to correct multiple errors in various applications such as data storage, satellite communication, and error correction. The use of error-correcting codes can maintain data integrity and reliability by: detecting and correcting errors, ensuring that the received data matches the original transmitted data.
