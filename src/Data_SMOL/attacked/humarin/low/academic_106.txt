The aging population in developed countries is causing a rise in the demand for healthcare services, leading to challenges in resource allocation and equity. How can governments and healthcare organizations balance their individual needs with those of children and young adults to ensure that their care meets the needs of aging adults? The aging population is a multifaceted issue that necessitates a coordinated effort among governments, healthcare organizations, and the private sector. If we work together, we can create a sustainable healthcare system that serves the elderly population and promotes healthy aging.
