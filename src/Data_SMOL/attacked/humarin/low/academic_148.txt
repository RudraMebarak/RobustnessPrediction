The significance of internal validity lies in the fact that the results of a hypothetical study can be attributed directly to the independent variable (IV) and not to any other factors. The internal validity and external validity of a study can help researchers understand how exercise affects weight loss and whether it can be applied to different populations. To enhance the internal and external validity of empirical research, researchers can use various techniques, including: * using a random sample of patients to ensure representative representation of the general population, * controlling for other variables such as changes in diet or health, * utilizing a double-blind study design to minimize bias, * using statistical tests to determine if the findings are generalizable to the broader population, and so on.
