To ensure accuracy when measuring, it is essential to understand the workings of a kitchen scale. To make a perfect savory recipe out of your kitchen, use a kitchen scale to measure flour, sugar, and egg. Make sure to test out all the ingredients and ensure that they are mixed correctly, as the scale will not measure everything. Place your mixing bowl or measuring cup on the scale's platform and press the "tare" or "zero" button to ensure the dry ingredients are prepared.
