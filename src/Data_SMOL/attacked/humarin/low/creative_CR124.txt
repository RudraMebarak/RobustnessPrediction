Is it possible for them to keep their secrets, or will their minds be shattered? Ravenshire has a unique way of welcoming strangers, as evidenced by the scent of freshly baked goods on the pavement. As you walk through the town, you can't help but wonder what it is that draws you to the town. The city, its people, and surrounding areas all seem to be aware of your thoughts, even if you're not aware of theirs. You're left wondering whether to keep your distance from them or to uncover the truth. The boundary between reality and madness is a thin line, and you must find a way to navigate it before you lose yourself completely. Would you choose to keep the secret or to dive into the unknown?
