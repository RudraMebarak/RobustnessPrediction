Magnetic resonance imaging is a medical technique that uses magnetic fields and radio waves to create detailed images of the body's internal structures. By using techniques like signal averaging and image subtraction, the MRI machine can generate high-quality images of the body and diagnose various medical conditions. Compared to other imaging techniques like CT and X-ray, MRI has several advantages, including the lack of ionizing radiation, which is safer for patients. MRI does not require contrast agents, which reduces the risk of allergic reactions. Moreover, it can generate high-resolution images of the body. * **Multi-dimensional imaging**: MRI can generate multi-dimensional images with a strong magnetic field, which can be challenging for some patients. * *Need a specific radio frequency**: Radio waves require MRIs.
