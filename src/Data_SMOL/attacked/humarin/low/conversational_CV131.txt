For someone who is new to coding and wants to learn, what are some recommended resources such as online courses, tutorials, and YouTube channels? Those interested in machine learning would highly recommend the 'CS50's Introduction to Computer Science' on YouTube. - User L 13. 'Programming for Everybody (PFO)' by Charles Severance is a free online book and course for beginners. 'Game Maker' is another option for those interested in a more interactive approach to programming, with 'Scratch' being a great resource for learning. Users V 23 and W 24 recommend 'Cybrary' for Cybersecurity courses, 'R's Data Visualization and Programming' for data analysis, and 'Game Maker Studio' for game development.
