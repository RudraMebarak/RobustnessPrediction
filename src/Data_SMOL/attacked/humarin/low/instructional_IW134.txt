To combat a grease fire, it is important to ensure that you are prepared with proper safety measures, such as a fire extinguisher, a designated area for cleaning and disinfecting equipment, and an exit strategy for cleaning the kitchen. If you have a grease fire, please don't attempt to extinguish it yourself and call 911 immediately. If you are struggling to extinguish a grease fire or suspect a fire, please seek help from 911 as quickly as possible.
