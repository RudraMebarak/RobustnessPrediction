RAG systems aim to enhance the efficiency and performance of natural language processing tasks by integrating retrieval mechanisms into the training or inference process. These systems consist of a retrieval mechanism that extracts relevant information from pre-trained knowledge sources, while another mechanism refines and augments it with human-like text generated by the generation mechanism. This approach ensures that NLP tasks are more efficient and accurate when combined with RAG models, which finely tune themselves to include the relevant data, including relevant statements. By training the RAG system, a model can be trained to understand and produce more accurate responses. In summary, MTL is essential in training RAG systems as it enables the retrieval head to learn how to retrieve relevant information and integrate it with the output of the pre-trained model.
