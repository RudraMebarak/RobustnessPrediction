How do low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellations differ in terms of their impact on global communication, including their ability to provide consistent data rates and minimum power requirements for addressing communication gaps? The difficulty level is significant in addressing operational challenges such as the cost of launching and maintaining an LEO satellite, which often lasts only 5-7 years. Geopolitical competition and regulatory challenges are also present in LEO satellite constellations, which can result in the development of space debris and environmental concerns.
