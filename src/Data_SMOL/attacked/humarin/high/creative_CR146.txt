For me, it was a dusty, vintage music box sitting on the back porch with never-so-old songs playing in the background. It was the sound of something ancient and beautiful creeping up my memory ... And then I felt... well maybe not even close to heaven but definitely something there that somehow had meaning to this: a quiet guardian siren. As for me, maybe it’s just this one time: some kind of magic playing between us on this particular topic – the music box between them... everything about the perfect balance. I believed myself to be an adult woman amidst the complexities of an impending transitional phase. The music box had instilled in me a feeling of wonder and curiosity, which gave me hope. As I wound up my box, the music played again, and so did the familiar melody that reminded me of my grandmother's childhood memories. My last words were comfort and peace as I accepted the idea of moving forward with love. I felt like an old owl guarding me as I learned. One evening, when the sun shone and the shadows disappeared, I opened my music box and the lid cracked open with the melody "Mean tones", the sound was so haunting that it felt like the sounds of something else: nothing had ever been heard before... Close my eyes, closed my thoughts as I opened them, and in moments like this, I experienced peace over my soul as sound waves.
