Determine the required preparation by comparing the number of files saved to an external drive with their storage capacity, connection speed (such as USB or FireWire), and any additional software or backup tools. Backups are made easy to restore by testing the integrity of your files, but even if you forget about it, they may be insecure. After resuming the backup process, you discover that it's taking an unusually long time and your computer crashes due to a virus attack. As a result, you quickly shut down the laptop and restart it, then perform another scan with an antivirus program, before testing again to ensure that the files are back up without interruption. This is a crucial step in ensuring that backup data is safe.
