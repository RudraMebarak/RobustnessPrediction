After walking through an old antique store, you walk into a shop where you come across antique teapots adorned with intricate patterns and designs from the Ming Dynasty. You feel transported to another time and place as you contemplate the ancient, mystical images within the pieces. You notice the sharpness of your eye contact. "What do you want to see in these teapots?" The shop owner asks. "Just because I'm not really there for it, I just want something to help me calm down." As you turn and touch the pot, the calm becomes more intense. Then, you become astonished by his gentleness. You're aware that you've been searching for something - or someone - to help you through this difficult time. Despite this, you didn't realize until now. As you walk out the door, the shop owner smiles and addresses you: "Alright, I know that you are not alone." With a warm heart, you leave your thoughts behind and acknowledge his generosity, feeling the peace and serenity of those present in front of you.
