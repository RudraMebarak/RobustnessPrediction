Traveling through Europe on a train that stops at cities such as Frankfurt, Prague, and Budapest, you encounter various conversations between passengers. You also hear about the challenges faced by parents and children during pregnancy exams, and learn about entrepreneurial opportunities in different parts of Europe. As you sit in your seat, lost in the beauty of your life and the stories you hear, you too feel "You should be finding out what else" awaits you on this journey - where you can explore your own homeland, where you will find connections and meaning behind your discoveries, where the train leads you to a place and an area to be crossed, a train that slows down, and a new feeling about who is coming next. The feeling of anticipation and excitement fills you as you prepare to ride the next train, amidst abundant cities, beautiful countryside, and endless urban spaces. You know that you'll be immersed in more of your own culture while also exploring greater learning opportunities.
