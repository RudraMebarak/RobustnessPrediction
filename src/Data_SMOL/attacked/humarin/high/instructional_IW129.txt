To report a software bug consistently, you must have at least one basic knowledge of operating systems, knowledge of handling files, and experience with command-line interfaces. Step 4: Reporting a software bug. Locate the official website and go to "Report a Bug" or "Contact Us." Write down all necessary information, including any attachments, such as screenshots or videos, and then open the program again to replicate the bug. If you report that the bug failed after testing with another computer, double-check if there are any errors in your documentation.
