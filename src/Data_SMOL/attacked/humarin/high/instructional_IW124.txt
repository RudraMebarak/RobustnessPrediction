Steps To Help You Report a Public Infrastructure Issue: (1) What Does It Mean** and (2) How Do I Know the Authority Responsible for Resolving an Issue?** Please refer to the "Report an Error" page to obtain specific details and photos. **Notes** *Please note that the authority responsible for this issue may not be immediately accessible on this site, nor will they contact you upon completion of your report. **Tells** Can't respond on phone********? Connect with the appropriate authority (City Public Works) to find out about street specifics. Gather detailed information such as a street's name, city and county code number, a date and location for an appointment, contact the authority via phone or online form, and let them know the problem before you report it. Confirm that your request has been completed and send pictures for approval. Follow up on any questions you may have later by calling the authority, including when they arrive from an emergency services team. Remember, nobody wants to be lost in the chaos! Remember Proactive reporting and contacting the appropriate city staff are both helpful tools for fixing public infrastructure issues.
