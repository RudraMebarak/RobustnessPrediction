To ensure a successful response to a grease fire, you must take appropriate safety measures. Provide an exit plan and an escape strategy, such as identifying if water is available or not, and staying at a safe distance, such that the heat is contained, and ensure your equipment meets all other necessary safety standards. Evaluate the situation for any potential threats, including electrical shock, poor breathing, or physical harm. If you experience a grease fire, don't attempt to extinguish it yourself and call 911 immediately. If you are struggling to extinguish a grease fire or suspect it, please call 911 immediately.
