Handwritten notes remain relevant in the digital age, serving as reminders, inspiration, and way of connecting with others. However, these days, many people find themselves writing rather than using computers or smartphones due to their limited accessibility, lack of skill in creating text messages, and perceived challenges in remembering details. Despite this, many appreciate their ability to provide personal meaning through subtle gestures such as opening and closing emails, touching hands, or performing minor handwriting tasks that can be time-consuming or even unproductive.
