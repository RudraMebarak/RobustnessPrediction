Magnetic resonance imaging is a technique used to produce detailed images of the body's internal structures, using magnetic fields and radio waves. The MRI machine uses several techniques, including signal averaging and image subtraction, to generate high-quality images of the body, such as spin-lattice relaxation, spin-spin relaxation, and spatial orientation. Compared to other imaging techniques like CT and X-ray, MRI has several advantages such as: removing ionizing radiation (which is safer for patients), using contrast agents (which reduces the risk of allergic reactions), producing high-resolution images of the body, and multidimensional imaging of the internal structures (which allows for more detailed analysis of internal structures) without the need for a strong magnetic field.
