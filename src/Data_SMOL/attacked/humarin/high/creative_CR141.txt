When the morning sun began to set, a cloud-like feeling emerged from her sleep. She was interrupted by the presence of a wind and breeze that carried her in a series of circles. Lulu took to her arms to become a mountainous creature. Although she thought she might be crazy, she didn't let it stop her from falling into the sky. Despite the doubts and cries for help, she couldn't hold back her determination and began breathing as she came closer to becoming a new mother. With the wind in her hair and the sun rising over her head, Lulu realized just how impressive it was and knew exactly what she wanted to achieve.
