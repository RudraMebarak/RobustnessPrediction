The moment I stood at the edge of a cliff, I realized that I had missed some opportunities and made a regretful decision for my life. I also realized that there was a hint to be found for me, as I saw myself embracing a figure and cautioning me not to take the same path. Upon leaving, I realized the importance of allowing the hand to approach me – it meant accepting a second chance. However, as I turned around and took my turn, I discovered that it was actually a trick of the light in the shadows. I was no longer certain, but I now knew it was all worth it because of the figure's presence, and the shadow had faded away. Despite feeling trapped in time with the pain of falling on my cliff edge, I was grateful for the moment when I became aware that I was left. As I bent over, the sun set upon me and I gazed down from above, my shattered life becoming brighter than ever before. I understood that the most favorable opportunities are those that were taken away due to unexpected events, while also acknowledging the possibility of missing them by adding another twist.
