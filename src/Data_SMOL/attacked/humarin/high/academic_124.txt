Containerization technology provides several benefits over other technologies, including the ability to deploy and manage resources independently. This is critical in cloud computing because it enables consistent operation across different infrastructure environments, while also managing resource allocation and reliability. Containerization technologies offer a range of benefits, including consistency across infrastructure environments, resource management, automation and orchestration, stateless architecture, and microservices architecture. These advantages enable organizations to quickly and efficiently deploy applications and manage resources more effectively.
