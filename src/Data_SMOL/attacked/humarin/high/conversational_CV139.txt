After unsuccessfully trying to set up a new computer network for his small business, John was surprised when his teenage daughter Emily showed up and helped him get ready with the networking tool. Emily was quick to respond and convinced him that she could do it for her, as she found out. Emily's failed attempts at setting up a wireless network resulted in inconsistent connections, and she responded by asking for her father's help. She found one and followed the steps, feeling confident about her abilities. Despite initial apprehension upon discovering it, John was still amazed by the stability of Emily's phone line and the positive reception she provided. They later realized that sometimes all is fair. John believes he should seek guidance from his children instead of turning away.
