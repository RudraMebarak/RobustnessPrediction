Cleaning and maintenance of tools is crucial when working with delicate materials such as wood, steel or stainless steel. To ensure hygiene, it is necessary to follow proper precautions, including removing any loose substances from the tool until it becomes wet. For proper maintenance, it is important to regularly clean and store hands tools and to invest in a tool chest or dedicated storage area so that your hands can be easily cleaned. Additionally, hand tools used for woodworking or other DIY projects should be maintained according to manufacturer specifications, not the specific type of tool you use. To clean wooden handles, use a soft cloth or sponge and gently wipe the wood's grain to remove any excess water. For plastic or rubber handles, it is best to use softer detergent or cleaning solution instead. Wash metal handles using a cloth or towel to clean them.
