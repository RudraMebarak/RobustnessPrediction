The significance of internal validity lies in the fact that the results of a hypothetical study can be attributed directly to the independent variable (IV) and not to any other factors. The internal validity and external validity of a study can help researchers understand how exercise affects weight loss and whether it can be applied to different populations. To enhance the internal and external validity of empirical research, researchers can use various techniques, including selecting a random sample of patients, controlling for other variables such as changes in diet or health, and using statistical tests to detect significant effects on the broader population.
