Examine the performance metrics and limitations of graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and evaluate their potential benefits and uses in the field of materials science. Specifically, GNNS is known for its ability to model and analyze complex relationships between nodes and edges, making it a valuable alternative to conventional ML methods for molecular property prediction. This review will examine the potential advantages and drawbacks of using GNNs in this area, as well as their computational limitations. Graph-based random forests and GSVMs have been shown to be more effective than traditional ML methods in predicting molecular properties. However, their performance metrics differ significantly from these, with one of the main drawbacks being the lack of interpretability. Furthermore, the choice of graph structure and convolutional filters for this type of model makes it difficult to understand the results. Despite these limitations, graph neural networks are not without their own benefits and should be refined in future research. Indreşti, L., et al. (2018).
