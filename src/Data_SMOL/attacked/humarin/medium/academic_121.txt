Explain the construction of these codes and their role in detecting and correcting errors. Using a non-binary approach, Reed-Solomon codes can add symbols to the original data stream. These symbols are calculated based on the polynomial function and decoded into codewords. This process is commonly used in applications such as CD and DVD data storage, satellite communication, and error correction. The use of error-correcting codes can maintain data integrity and reliability by: detecting and correcting errors, ensuring that the received data matches the original transmitted data.
