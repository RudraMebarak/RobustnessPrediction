Prepare for disaster by creating an emergency contact and essentials checklist. Prepare your household for emergencies by executing a drill that simulates evacuation or sheltering-in-place, assigning specific roles, gathering supplies, and reviewing the checklist every year. Checklist:** Review and revise the guide regularly as family members and community hazards change; explore additional resources for emergency preparedness and training opportunities; engage with your community for shared resources, training, and support; and prepare for a safer future for yourself and your loved ones by staying informed and staying connected!
