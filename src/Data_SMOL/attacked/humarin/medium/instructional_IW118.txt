Prerequisites: Know-how of email structure (title, subject, signature), importance of a professional body language, and understanding of the interview topic and expected outcomes (such as salary, company, etc.). How do you go about crafting your follow-up email after an interview? Make sure to include your contact information (phone, email, or LinkedIn) and your interest in the position. Follow these steps to ensure a successful and professional follow-up email. I had a great conversation with the team at [Company] during the interview and I'm really interested in the [Specific Aspect] of the Job or Company.
