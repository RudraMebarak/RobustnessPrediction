Public libraries have historically been essential components of educational and cultural institutions, serving as sources of education for communities of all backgrounds. However, public libraries can also play a pivotal role in promoting literacy, social mobility, and improving the quality of life for those underserved. They can offer valuable opportunities for individuals to develop literacy skills, explore new topics, participate in professional and educational programs, engage with others in communities where they are free or low-cost, provide access to books and other resources (such as summer reading challenges, literacy workshops, etc. Public libraries can play a crucial role in enhancing literacy rates and social mobility by providing access to education and resources. The subject matter centers on the role of public libraries in enhancing literacy rates among marginalized populations. To address these issues, research inquiries should be directed towards: 1. What are the benefits of using public library facilities and services to enhance literacy levels in underserved areas? 2. How do public librarians impact the socioeconomic status of underprivileged individuals? 4. How does education technology contribute to literacy growth?
