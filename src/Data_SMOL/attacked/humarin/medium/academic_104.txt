How can communities balance the need to preserve their cultural identity and the challenges posed by global changes and technological advancements while also considering the importance of cultural heritage preservation? Cultural heritage preservation can only be achieved through the support of governments and policymakers, who can also use technology to create digital archives, online repositories, and cultural exchange programs.
