I felt a sense of homelessness as I walked through the gated area of the festival, where the colors, music, and laughter filled me with. The sun shone brightly and I marveled at the colorful displays of spices, jewelry, crafts, food and fun. I also found myself lost in the familiar rhythm of people dancing around me, missing out on the company of friends and family. As I walked through the gates of the festival, I felt a sense of sadness and wonder. I didn't expect me to be as present as I was at home, but I had the experience of being part of something bigger and better. As the sun sets over the festival grounds, you feel like you've been playing for hours, and the crowds are filled with happy faces and gleeful expressions. You take a seat on the Ferris wheel and marvel at the smiling faces, knowing that there is something very special waiting for you.
