To prepare for an evacuation, one must have knowledge of the evacuation area's layout, including what exits and exit routes are available. It is important to have a well-thought-out evacuation plan in order to remain relevant and effective during crisis situations. This guide does not provide specific recommendations or guidance, and should be tailored to the individual circumstances of the group. This guide is intended to provide general information and may not be accurate or up-to-date for all situations. Always seek professional or expert guidance when dealing with a crisis.
