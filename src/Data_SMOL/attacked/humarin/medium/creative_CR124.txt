Is it possible for them to keep their secrets, or will their minds be shattered? Ravenshire has a unique way of welcoming strangers, as evidenced by the scent of freshly baked goods on the pavement. As you walk through the town, you can't help but wonder what it is that draws you to this charming town. The city, its people, and surrounding areas all seem to have knowledge of your thoughts, even if you don't. Consequently, you're left to decide whether to keep them hidden or to uncover the truth. The boundary between reality and madness is blurred, and you must find a way to navigate it before you lose yourself completely. Would you choose to keep the secret or to dive into the unknown?
