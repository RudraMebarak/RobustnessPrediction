Our project highlights the importance of using affordable sensors to monitor water quality and improve the lives of individuals and communities across the globe. The key takeaways from this brief presentation are: (1) Environmental impact; (2) budgetary constraints on sensor implementation; and (2) opportunities for reducing water pollution through cost-effective methods. Our research will examine the potential of low-cost sensors in addressing water quality challenges, their cost effectiveness, and integration into existing monitoring systems. Furthermore, we will assess their feasibility in diverse environmental settings, with a focus on regions with limited resources and infrastructure. Our objective is to provide valuable insights that can help us develop sustainable water and land management strategies.
