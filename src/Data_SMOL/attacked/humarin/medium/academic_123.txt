Magnetic resonance imaging is a medical technique that uses magnetic fields and radio waves to create detailed images of the body's internal structures. By using techniques like signal averaging and image subtraction, the MRI machine can generate high-quality images of the body and diagnose various medical conditions. Compared to other imaging techniques like CT and X-ray, MRI has several advantages, including the lack of ionizing radiation, which is safer for patients. Moreover, it does not require contrast agents, leading to a decrease in patient risk of allergic reactions. It also offers high-resolution images, multi-dimensional imaging, and radio frequency response, among other things.
