Train journeys across the countryside from Germany to Italy, including stops in Frankfurt, Prague, Vienna, and Budapest, can be challenging due to the presence of many unfamiliar passengers. As you sit in your seat, the beauty of the journey and the stories you've heard remind you that you too are on a journey of self-discovery and discovering meaning in the twists and turns of life, and wonder where you will be next, even as you wait for the train to come back, slowly but surely, with an overwhelming sense of familiarity. As you get ready to board the next train, you experience a sense of anticipation and excitement. You know that you'll be witnessing more Europe, more cities, and more landscapes, but also expect to learn and grow in your own skin as you ride this train.
