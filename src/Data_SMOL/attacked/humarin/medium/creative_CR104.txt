This prompt encourages imaginative writing and world-building. There are those who claim that on a clear night, they can hear the whispers of history and the winds that howl through the mountain's ancient corridors. As they travel to Mount Elyria, one brings with them memories of the people who came before them. The rocks at this site symbolize their triumphs and sorrows, and as they leave, he takes with him an essential piece of wisdom of memories that shape us and our relationship with the land. This prompt can be customized to fit any genre of fiction, including fantasy, or historical fiction.
