During her morning nap, a small cloud named Lulu appeared in the sky and felt the breeze blowing through her arms and legs. She knew she had the power to make her own way around the wind and storm clouds, but was overcome with determination. With the wind in her hair and the sun shining over her head, Lulu knew that she had made herself a mountain, and other clouds couldn't wait for her turn to leave. She stood tall and proud, even as she looked down at everyone around her, wondering if she was ready to explore the vast world beyond her fluffy cloud. I'm left wondering what kind of adventure awaits me in this story - whether it's about meeting new creatures or exploring the depths of the planet below.
