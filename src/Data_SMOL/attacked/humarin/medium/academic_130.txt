How do low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellations improve communication services and address coverage gaps across the globe? LEO satellites have a limited lifespan due to atmospheric drag and debris, necessitating regular maintenance and replacement to maintain service continuity. LEO satellite constellations are a source of competition in the geopolitical arena, where countries compete to control and establish satellite networks. There are several regulatory issues, such as international cooperation, spectrum management, and cybersecurity concerns. Furthermore, there are numerous threats to national security and data integrity from satellite spying and hacking.
