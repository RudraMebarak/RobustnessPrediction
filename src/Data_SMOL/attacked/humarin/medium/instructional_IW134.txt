To respond to a grease fire, it is important to ensure that you are prepared with proper safety measures, such as turning off the heat and selecting an appropriate fire extinguisher, keeping your head down and avoiding touching anything that could potentially injure you or others. This guide is not intended to be exhaustive and may contain potential dangers. If you have a grease fire, please don't attempt to extinguish it yourself and call 911 immediately. If you are struggling to extinguish a grease fire or suspect it, please call 911 immediately.
