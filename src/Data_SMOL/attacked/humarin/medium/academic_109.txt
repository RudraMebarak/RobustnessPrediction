Access to clean water is not only a necessity for human survival, but it also has significant implications for economic participation and educational attainment. By investing in clean water infrastructure and supporting rural communities, we can improve health outcomes, education, and economic productivity, which can lead to improved academic performance and higher scores. According to a study conducted by the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), students who had clean water had higher academic success in reading and math tests than those who did not have access to it. Additionally, Clean Water promotes economic activity by reducing the financial burden of water-related expenses, which can be challenging for families living in rural areas. When families are able to save money on water resources, they can focus on other essential needs such as education and healthcare, according to researchers.
