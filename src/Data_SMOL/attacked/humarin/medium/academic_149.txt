When designing and deploying AI systems, it is crucial to ensure that they do not perpetuate or exacerbate existing biases in the data used to train them. Various approaches have been suggested to address algorithmic fairness, but these tend to be conflicting. For instance, demographic parity would require that a system make employment decisions that are unbiased towards different demographic groups, just like the department store. On the other hand, a system with equal odds may not be fair to individuals from underrepresented demographic groups, and this can result in unfairness. In practical terms, the choice of fairness criterion is often dependent on several factors, such as the application of the technology and the societal values that are being emphasized.
