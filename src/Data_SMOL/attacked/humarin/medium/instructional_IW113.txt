To ensure proper maintenance of your tools, examine them thoroughly for visible damage such as nicks, dents, and scratches. Additionally, it is recommended to disassemble the tools themselves if they are damaged. If there are any small parts that need to be removed, use a soft cloth or wire brush to clean the handles. Repeat the same process with different components until the final assembly is complete. It is also important to avoid storing tools in an airtight container, which can cause accidents. To ensure long-lasting and dependable use of your tools, it is recommended to regularly clean and store them, which can help prevent damage and prolong their lifespan. It is important to use a tool chest or dedicated storage area when working in harsh conditions, as well as to follow the manufacturer's recommended cleaning and storage guidelines.
