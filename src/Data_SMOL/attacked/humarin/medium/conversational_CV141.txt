I remember making a mistake in my early 20s when I was working as an account manager at the tech company. My job required me to create engaging content for their social media platforms, which involved sharing updates, behind-the-scenes footage, and responses to popular posts. Despite receiving positive feedback, I struggled to connect with my audience, leaving me feeling let down and uncertain about my approach. I learned through this mistake that there are times when we don't trust our instincts and need to take action to create an engaging online presence. This mistake led me to prioritize taking breaks from work and reevaluating my options, which has helped me become more successful in my role as a marketing coordinator. Trusting your instincts is essential, but it's not a one-size-fits-all solution. It requires balance, self-awareness, and the ability to listen to your audience. The mistake I made led me to learn that trusting yourself, pivoting, listening to those around you, taking time to think and re-think is crucial. However, if I had to pick one lesson from the past, I would choose to focus solely on **true ourselves.** Truly speaking.
