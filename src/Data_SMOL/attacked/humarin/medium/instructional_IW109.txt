To avoid potential harm, it is advisable to switch off the power supply of your battery to prevent electrical shock. Additionally, you should have access to a multimeter that can test for battery modes and identify any abnormalities. Always follow the instructions and precautions outlined in the multimeter's instruction manual. Refrain from attempting to recharge dead or damaged batteries, as this may necessitate a dedicated battery charger or expert assistance. --- **Battery Safety and Maintenance Notes** - Take care to maintain the cleanliness of battery terminals and ensure close contact with each other.
