What is the process for reporting a public infrastructure issue? When reporting an issue with the City Public Works, it's important to identify the authority. Gather details such as the street name, City (Springfield), State (CA), date (10:00 AM), and phone number to follow up. Review your email for confirmation and if necessary, ask for pictures. Check the "Report a Problem" or "311 Service" link on the city/county website for assistance. If you can't be in the building today, please be patient and let us know when the problem is resolved quickly. Let's work together to make our cities safer and more usable for all of us.
