Handwritten notes continue to play a significant role in modern technology, serving as reminders of personal ideas and connecting with others. However, they may not be as efficient as digital tools due to their time-consuming nature, which can lead to emotional distress. Handwritten notes are a great way to stay organized and on-task. They also remind me of important dates and deadlines, which I find particularly sentimental when compared to digital tools. The effectiveness of handwritten notes is influenced by the task at hand, with digital tools being more suitable for complex tasks like brainstorming and creativity.
