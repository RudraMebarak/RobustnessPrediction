Prerequisites:
1. Basic understanding of file sharing platforms (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
2. Familiarity with creating and naming files
3. Basic knowledge of email

1. Open your file sharing platform account (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
2. Create or upload the file you want to share
   - **Google Drive:** Click "+ New" and select "File upload."
   - **Dropbox:** Click "+ New" and select "Upload Files."
   - **OneDrive:** Click "+ New" and select "Files."
3. Navigate to the created or uploaded file
4. Click or tap on the "Share" or "Access" button
   - **Google Drive:** You will find it under the file menu or on the right side of the file title
   - **Dropbox:** It's under the file menu or on the right side
   - **OneDrive:** It's under the file menu or on the right side
5. Enter the email address or username of the collaborator
   - **Note:** Ensure you have their correct email address or username
6. Specify the permission level (e.g., view only, edit)
   - **Google Drive:** You will see options like "Anyone can view," "Only specified people can view," etc.
   - **Dropbox:** It shows you the type of access each collaborator will have
   - **OneDrive:** It shows you the type of access each collaborator will have
7. Click or tap on "Send," 3: Share Files with Collaborators**

1. Navigate to the files you want to share.
2. Click on the "Share" button or similar.
3. Choose the collaborators you want to share the files with (e.g., by email address, username, or social media handle).
4. Customize the sharing permissions, such as allowing read-only access, read/write access, or full control.
5. Click "Send" or "Share" to distribute the files to the selected collaborators.

**Step 4: Manage Access Permissions**

1. Log in to your file-sharing platform account.
2. Navigate to the shared folder you created.
3. Click on the "Settings" or "Permissions" button or similar.
4. Review and adjust the access permissions for each collaborator.
5. Save your changes.

**Step 5: Verify Completion**

1. Check that all collaborators have received the shared files.
2. Verify that access permissions are correctly set for each collaborator.
3. Ensure that you can access and modify the shared files as intended.

**Additional Tips:**

* Always use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication for your file-sharing platform account.
* Regularly review and update access permissions to ensure they remain appropriate.
* Consider using the platform's built-in tools for tracking changes and revisions.
* Keep your file-sharing platform account and computer up to date with the latest security patches.
