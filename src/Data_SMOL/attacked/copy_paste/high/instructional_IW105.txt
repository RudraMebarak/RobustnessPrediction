Prerequisites: 
1. Knowledge of the basics of sustainable living and zero-waste practices.
2. Access to online grocery stores or a nearby farmer's market.

Step 1: Set Shopping Goals
   - Start by planning what you need for a week or two. Write down everything you will need to buy, making sure to think about reusable containers, packaging-free items, and whole foods.
   
Step 2: Plan Your Shopping List
   - Based on your shopping goals, create a list of the items you will need to buy. Include the specific quantities you will need based on your household size and eating habits.
   
Step 3: Research Your Options
   - Look for local or online grocery stores that offer a wide range of sustainable and zero-waste products. Research their inventory and pricing to find the best deals.
   
Step 4: Create a Shopping Plan
   - Plan out how you will shop, including which stores or markets you will visit, and what you will buy at each location. 
   
Step 5: Make a Budget
   - Create a budget for your trip, taking into account the costs of sustainable and zero-waste products.
   
Step 6: Pack Your Reusable Bags
   - Gather all your reusable bags, containers, and other tools you will need to shop sustainably. Make sure everything is clean and ready to go.
   
Step 7: Prepare to Shop
   - Double-check your shopping list, budget, and shopping plan before you head out.
   
Step 8: Shop Sustainably
   - When shopping, choose products with minimal packaging, and opt for reusable containers or bags whenever possible.
   
Step 9: Verify Your Shopping
   - Once you have finished shopping, verify that you have everything you need, and that you have purchased the right quantities.
   
Step 10: Store Your Goods
   - Once you have verified your shopping, store your goods properly, making sure to keep them fresh and organized.

**Step 2: Choose Reusable Containers and Bags**

1. Gather your reusable containers (glass jars, plastic containers, etc.) and bags (canvas, cloth, or sturdy paper).
2. Ensure they are clean and free of food particles to prevent contamination.

**Step 3: Visit Local Grocery Stores**

1. Visit your local grocery store or farmer's market to shop for fresh produce and non-perishable items.
2. Focus on buying whole foods, such as fruits, vegetables, grains, and legumes, which have minimal packaging.
3. Select non-perishable items in bulk, using your reusable containers to minimize waste.

**Step 4: Shop for Non-Perishable Items**

1. Select non-perishable items, such as canned goods, nuts, and seeds.
2. Choose items with minimal or no packaging, or opt for bulk bins.
3. Use your reusable containers to store items like grains, nuts, and dried fruits.

**Step 5: Select Fresh Produce**

1. Choose fresh produce with minimal packaging, such as loose leaf lettuce, carrots, and potatoes.
2. Avoid items with excessive packaging or those that come in individually wrapped portions.
3. Select fruits and vegetables that are in season to reduce carbon footprint.

**Step 6: Shop for Dairy and Meat Alternatives**

1. Opt for dairy alternatives like almond milk, soy yogurt, or coconut cream.
2. Choose meat alternatives like tofu, tempeh, or lentils.
3. Select items with minimal packaging or in bulk.

**Step 7: Checkout and Checkout Process**

1. Proceed to checkout, placing your items in a reusable shopping bag.
2. Use a reusable bag to carry your purchases home.
3. Verify that your items are within your budget and that you have everything you need.

**Step 8: Verify Completion**

1. Double-check your grocery list to ensure you have everything you need.
2. Verify that you have used reusable containers and bags throughout the shopping trip.
3. Check your reusable containers and bags for any contamination or damage.
