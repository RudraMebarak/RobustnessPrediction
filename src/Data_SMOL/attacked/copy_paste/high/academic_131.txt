Transparency is a crucial aspect of ensuring fairness in predictive policing. When the decision-making processes of these algorithms are not transparent, it is challenging to identify and address biases. Transparency can be achieved through various means, such as providing detailed documentation of the algorithm's development, testing, and deployment processes. Additionally, incorporating explainable AI techniques can help to provide insights into the factors driving the algorithm's predictions. By enhancing transparency, law enforcement agencies can increase accountability and ensure that the algorithms are fair and unbiased.

To ensure due process, it is essential to provide individuals with the opportunity to contest the predictions and to have their cases reviewed by independent authorities.
