For some tools, such as screwdrivers, pliers, or hammers, you may need to disassemble them to clean them thoroughly. Remove all components, paying close attention to the removal of any small parts.

Step 3: Clean Tools

2. For wooden handles: Apply a small amount of wood cleaner to a soft cloth or sponge and gently wipe down the handle, paying special attention to the wood's grain. Avoid getting water into the handle or on the metal parts.
3. For plastic or rubber handles: Use a mild detergent or cleaning solution in a soft cloth or sponge to clean the handle.

**Prerequisites:**

1.  Familiarity with basic hand tools (e.g., screwdrivers, pliers, hammers, etc.).
2.  Basic understanding of safety protocols (e.g., wearing gloves, goggles, etc.).
3.  Access to a clean, well-ventilated workspace.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

**1. Gather Safety Equipment:**

*   **Gloves:** Wear rubber or latex gloves to protect your hands from sharp edges, splinters, or chemicals.
*   **Goggles:** Put on safety goggles to shield your eyes from flying debris.
*   **Face Mask:** Consider wearing a face mask to protect against dust and debris.
*   **Work Gloves:** Wear heavy-duty work gloves for added protection.

**2. Prepare Workspace:**

*   Clear a workspace with a smooth surface, away from flammable materials.
*   Ensure proper lighting and ventilation.
*   Cover nearby surfaces with drop cloths or newspaper to catch debris.

**3. Clean Hand Tools:**

*   **Remove Tools:** Take each tool and remove any debris or dirt from your hands and work area.
*   **Dampen a Cloth:** Use a clean, damp cloth to wipe down the tool's exterior.
*   **Apply Cleaning Solution:** Apply a small amount of tool cleaner or mild soap to a cloth or sponge.
*   **Rub the Tool:** Rub the tool in a circular motion, paying attention to all surfaces, including the handle.
*   **Rinse:** Rinse the tool under warm water, ensuring all debris and soap are removed.
*   **Dry:** Wipe the tool dry with a clean cloth.

**4. Sharpen and Adjust Tools (if necessary):**

*   **Check the Blade:** Inspect the blade or cutting edge for nicks, burrs, or damage.
*   **Sharpen:** Use a sharpening stone or a sharpening tool to hone the blade.
*   **Adjust:** Adjust the tool's handle or jaw if necessary.

**5. Inspect for Damage:**

*   **Check the Handle:** Examine the handle for cracks, splits, or wear.
*   **Inspect the Head:** Check the tool's head for damage or wear.

**6. Store Hand Tools:**

*   **Choose a Storage Location:** Store tools in a dry, cool place, away from moisture and chemicals.
*   **Use a Tool Holder:** Use a tool holder or rack to keep tools organized and accessible.
*   **Store in a Box or Case:** Store smaller tools in a box or case to prevent damage.

**7. Verify Completion:**

*   **Inspect Tools:** Check each tool to ensure it is clean, dry, and properly stored.
*   **Verify Safety Protocols:** Ensure all safety equipment is still in place and working properly.
