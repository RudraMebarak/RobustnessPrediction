**Title: Emergency Contact and Essentials Checklist for Your Household**

**Introduction**
Disasters happen unexpectedly, leaving families vulnerable. A well-prepared household minimizes risks, ensures safety, and supports recovery. This guide will walk you through creating an emergency contact and essentials checklist.

**Prerequisites**
1. Gather family members and agree on responsibilities.
2. Define your community's hazard risks (e.g., earthquakes, hurricanes).

**Step-by-Step Guide**
1. **Form a Team:**
   - **Objective:** Create a list of family members and assign tasks.
   - **Actions:**
     - Schedule a meeting to discuss responsibilities.
     - Create a list of family members and their roles (e.g., phone contact, medical info, communication).
     - Agree on a primary and secondary phone number for each member.
   - **Check:** Ensure every family member's role is documented and known by all.

2. **Create the Essentials List:**
   - **Objective:** Gather necessary items for sheltering-in-place and evacuation.
   - **Actions:**
     - Gather a first-aid kit (basic supplies like bandages, pain relievers).
     - Collect non-perishable food items (e.g., canned goods, dried fruits).
     - Include a battery-powered radio or TV for news updates.
     - Stock up on water (1 gallon per person per day).
     - Gather a multi-tool or portable power source (e.g., solar charger).
   - **Check:** Verify all essential items are easily accessible and functional.

3. **Organize the List:**
   - **Objective:** Make the list easily accessible.
   - **Actions:**
     - Create a binder or folder for the checklist.
     - Label sections for phone numbers, first-aid, food, and water.
   - **Check:** Ensure all sections are clearly labeled and easily found.

4. **Practice the Plan:**
   - **Objective:** Familiarize the household with the plan.
   - **Actions:**
     - Conduct a drill to simulate evacuation or sheltering-in-place.
     - Assign specific roles during the drill.
     - Gather all necessary items for the drill.
   - **Check:** Conduct a drill contact and essentials list, gather the following:

1.  **List of household members**: Write down the names, ages, and contact information for each person in your household.
2.  **Emergency phone numbers**: Collect essential phone numbers, such as your local police department, fire department, and poison control center.
3.  **Medical information**: Gather the names, contact numbers, and medical information for each household member.
4.  **Important documents**: Keep copies of important documents, such as identification cards, health insurance cards, and property deeds.

**Step-by-Step Guide**

Here's a step-by-step guide to help you create your emergency contact and essentials list:

1.  **Gather Your Materials**

    *   Gather all the materials you've collected, including the list of household members, emergency phone numbers, medical information, and important documents.

2.  **Organize Your Information**

    *   Organize your information into categories, such as:
        *   **Emergency Contacts**: List your local emergency services, such as the police, fire department, and poison control center.
        *   **Medical Information**: List the names, contact numbers, and medical information for each household member.
        *   **Important Documents**: Keep copies of important documents, such as identification cards, health insurance cards, and property deeds.
        *   **Household Members**: List the names, ages, and contact information for each person in your household.

3.  **Create a Checklist**

    *   Create a checklist with the following categories:
        *   **Emergency Contacts**
        *   **Medical Information**
        *   **Important Documents**
        *   **Household Members**

4.  **Fill in the Checkpoints**

    *   Fill in each checkpoint with the relevant information for each category.
    *   Make sure to include:
        *   **Names and Contact Numbers**: For each emergency contact and household member.
        *   **Medical Information**: For each household member.
        *   **Important Documents**: For each important document.

5.  **Review and Update Your List**

    *   Review your list to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
    *   Update your list regularly to reflect any changes, such as new household members or changes in medical information.
