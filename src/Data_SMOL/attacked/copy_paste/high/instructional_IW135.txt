Prerequisites: 
1. Knowledge of the label's contents and usage instructions (which can be found on the label itself).
2. Basic understanding of safe handling and storage of cleaning products.

Sequence of actions:
1. Gather necessary cleaning supplies (cleaning products, necessary tools like gloves, and cleaning materials).
2. Read and follow the label's instructions for the cleaning product you are about to use (check the concentration, mixing requirements if any, and storage information).
3. Put on gloves to protect your hands.
4. Measure the cleaning product and pour it into a clean container (if required).
5. Apply the cleaning product as instructed to the affected area (following any specific directions for application).
6. Rinse the affected area as instructed in the label (if required).
7. Disinfect and clean as instructed in the label (if required).
8. Dispose of any cleaning materials and cleaning products according to their label's instructions (proper disposal methods vary).

Verification of completion:
1. Check that the cleaning product has been applied as instructed to the affected area.
2. Verify that the cleaning product has been rinsed and cleaned as instructed in the label.
3. Ensure that any cleaning materials and cleaning products have been disposed of according to their label's instructions.

Please be aware that this is a general guide and may vary depending on the specific cleaning product being used. Always consult the label or manufacturer's instructions for the safest and most effective way to use the product.

Step 5: Apply the Cleaning Solution
Apply the cleaning solution to the surface you want to clean, following the manufacturer's instructions. Ensure you cover all areas thoroughly.

Step 6: Scrub and Rinse
Scrub the surface with a clean rag or sponge, following the manufacturer's instructions. Rinse the surface with clean water, then wipe it dry with a microfiber cloth.

Step 7: Dispose of Used Materials
Dispose of the used cleaning rags, sponges, and other materials according to the label's instructions.

Step 8: Clean Up
Clean up any spills or drips immediately, using a clean cloth and water. Avoid using hot water on certain surfaces, as it may damage them.

Step 9: Wash Your Hands
After completing the cleaning task, wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water.

Step 10: Store Unused Products Safely
Store any unused cleaning products in a well-ventilated area, away from children and pets. Follow the label's instructions for storage and disposal.

Verification:
To verify that you've completed the cleaning task safely and effectively, perform a final inspection of the surface. Ensure it's clean, dry, and free from any visible residue or damage. Double-check the label to confirm that you followed the manufacturer's instructions.

Conclusion:
By following these steps, you can use cleaning products safely and effectively, maintaining a hygienic living environment without compromising your health or the environment. Remember to always read labels, follow instructions, and prioritize safety at every step. Happy cleaning!
