**Title: Emergency Contact and Essentials Checklist for Your Household**

**Introduction**
Disasters happen unexpectedly, leaving families vulnerable. A well-prepared household minimizes risks, ensures safety, and supports recovery. This guide will walk you through creating an emergency contact and essentials checklist.

**Prerequisites**
1. Gather family members and agree on responsibilities.
2. Define your community's hazard risks (e.g., earthquakes, hurricanes).

**Step-by-Step Guide**
1. **Form a Team:**
   - **Objective:** Create a list of family members and assign tasks.
   - a comprehensive list tailored to your household's needs. By following these instructions, you'll be better equipped to handle unexpected situations and ensure the safety of your family.

**Prerequisites**

Before you start creating your emergency contact and essentials list, gather the following:

1.  **List of household members**: Write down the names, ages, and contact information for each person in your household.
2.  **Emergency phone numbers**: Collect essential phone numbers, such as your local police department, fire department, and poison control center.
3.  **Medical information**: Gather the names, contact numbers, and medical information for each household member.
4.  **Important documents**: Keep copies of important documents, such as identification cards, health insurance cards, and property deeds.

**Step-by-Step Guide**
