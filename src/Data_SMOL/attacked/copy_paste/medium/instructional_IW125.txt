**Accessible Activity Planning Guide**

**Prerequisites:**

1.  Knowledge of the basics of accessibility and inclusive design
2.  Understanding of assistive technologies (AT)
3.  Familiarity with disability awareness and cultural competency
4.  Basic understanding of group dynamics and communication
5.  Experience with planning group activities
6.  Access to a diverse group of people (participants)
7.  Time and budget for preparation

**Step 1: Define the Activity**

1.  Determine the desired activity (e.g., sports, art, music, or a mix of activities)
2.  Identify the skills and abilities needed for the activity
3.  Research the accessibility needs for the activity (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, sensory-friendly spaces)
4.  Consult with participants to understand their needs and preferences
5.  Create a list of potential modifications and accommodations
6.  Write a clear and concise description of the activity and its accessibility features

**Step 2: Plan for Accessibility**

1.  Ensure the venue or location is accessible (e.g., wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms)
2.  Select activities that are safe, fun, and inclusive for all participants
3.  Provide a diverse range of options to cater to different abilities and interests

**Step 6: Verify Completion**

1.  Gather feedback from participants and facilitators
2.  Conduct a debriefing session to discuss what worked and what didn't
3.  Revise the plan as needed for future activities
4.  Document the activity (e.g., photos, videos, written testimonials)
5.  Share the activity with others (e.g., through social media, newsletters)

**Example: Accessible Scavenger Hunt**

1.  Define the activity: A scavenger hunt with a focus on physical activity and teamwork
2.  Plan for accessibility: Ensure the venue is accessible, select activities with moderate difficulty, and provide accommodations for participants with mobility or sensory impairments
3.  Create a detailed plan:
	* Start time: 9:00 am
	* Duration: 1.5 hours
	* Activities:
		+ Team-building challenges
		+ Physical activities (e.g., yoga, dance)
		+ Sensory-friendly spaces (e.g., indoor playgrounds, quiet rooms)
	* Accommodations: Wheelchair ramps, Braille materials, visual aids
4.  Prepare for participation:
	* Train facilitators and coordinators on accessibility and participant needs
	* Conduct a rehearsal to ensure smooth participation
	* Distribute materials and information
	* Plan for participant feedback and concerns
5.  Execute the activity:
	* Begin the scavenger hunt as planned, with accommodations and modifications as needed
	* Monitor participant engagement and adjust as necessary
	* Celebrate participation and success
6.  Verify completion:
	* Gather feedback from participants and facilitators
	* Conduct a debriefing session to discuss what worked and what didn't
	* Revise the plan as needed for future activities
