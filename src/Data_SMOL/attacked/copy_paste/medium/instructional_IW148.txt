To organize a messy digital folder system, you will need a structured approach that covers preparation, execution, and final review. Here's a checklist to get you started:

**Preparation**

1. **Set clear goals**: Determine what you want to achieve with your new folder system, such as improved organization, easier file retrieval, or better collaboration.
2. **Identify the scope**: Determine which files or folders you want to organize (e.g., work-related, personal, or both).
3. **Gather resources**: Gather the necessary tools, such as a laptop, computer, or tablet, as well as any external storage devices or software (e.g., file management apps).
4. **Create a schedule**: Plan the timeline for organizing your folders, including dedicated time slots and deadlines.

**Execution**

1. **Backup important files**: Create backups of important files to prevent data loss during the organization process.
2. **Create new folders and subfolders**: Organize your files into logical categories and subfolders (e.g., "Work" -> "Projects" -> "Sales").
3. **Move files into folders**: Move files into their corresponding folders and subfolders, making sure to categorize them properly.
4. **Label and rename files**: Label and rename files consistently, using descriptive titles and keywords.
5. **Delete unnecessary files**: Remove files that are no longer needed or duplicates.
6. **Organize file metadata**: Update file metadata (e.g., file names, descriptions, and keywords) to improve searchability and retrieval.
7. **Create shortcuts or links**: Create shortcuts or links to frequently accessed files on your desktop or another convenient location.
8. **Test the system**: Test your new folder system to ensure it's working correctly and you can easily find the files you need.
