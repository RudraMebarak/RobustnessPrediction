Prerequisites: 
1. Knowledge of the layout of the campus and locations of key features like the library, student center, and dining halls.
2. Basic communication skills to provide clear instructions.
3. Ability to use a map or a navigation app.

Guide:

Step 1: Approach the Visitor 
Greet the visitor warmly and ask if they need help. Offer assistance in finding their destination or just general navigation.

Step 2: Obtain their Destination 
Ask the visitor where they want to go. Write down their destination and any specific building numbers or names.

Step 3: Locate their Destination 
Using the map or navigation app, find the visitor's destination and plot a route. 

Step 4: Provide Clear Instructions 
Once you have the route, provide the visitor with clear, concise directions. 

Step 5: Offer Additional Help 
If the visitor is lost or needs further assistance, ask if they want you to show them the way or if they need a map or a navigation app.

Step 6: Verify Completion 
Ask the visitor if they have found their destination and if you can assist them further.

This process can be repeated as needed to ensure the visitor finds their way around the campus. It's important to maintain a friendly and helpful demeanor throughout the process.
