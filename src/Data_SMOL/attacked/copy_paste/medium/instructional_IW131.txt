Prerequisites:
1. Basic understanding of business processes and project management methodologies (e.g., Six Sigma DMAIC or Lean).
2. Familiarity with process documentation, such as work instructions or process maps.

Process Overview:
You will be comparing a business process against its defined requirements. This involves reviewing the process documentation, identifying deviations or discrepancies, and making recommendations for improvements.

Step 1: Gather Process Documentation
   - Collect all relevant documents related to the process, including work instructions, specifications, and any other supporting materials.

Step 2: Review the Requirements
   - Carefully read and understand the process requirements, which are likely outlined in the work instructions or specifications.

Step 3: Map the Process
   - Create a process map or a flowchart to visualize the process and identify the steps involved.

Step 4: Compare Process Map to Requirements
   - Compare the process map to the requirements, identifying any deviations or discrepancies between the two.

Step 5: Identify Key Areas for Improvement
   - Based on the comparison, identify the key areas that do not align with the requirements and prioritize them for improvement.

Step 6: Analyze Root Causes
   - Investigate the reasons behind the identified deviations or discrepancies, focusing on the root causes.

6. **Document Findings**: Document your findings and observations. This will help you track your progress and identify patterns or trends.

Regularly reviewing and refining processes against their requirements ensures continuous improvement and alignment with organizational goals.
