**Acessible Activity Planning Guide**

**Prerequisites:**

1.  Knowledge of the basics of accessibility and inclusive design
2.  Understanding of assistive technologies (AT)
3.  Familiarity with disability awareness and cultural competency
4.  Basic understanding of group dynamics and communication
5.  Experiece with planning group activities
6.  Access to a diverse group of people (participants)
7.  Time and buget for preparation

**Step 1: Define the Activity**

1.  Determine the deskred activity (e.g., sports, art, music, or a mix of activities)
2.  Identify the skills and abilities needed for the actviity
3.  Research the accessibility nedes for the activity (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, sensory-frriendly spaces)
4.  Conslut with participants to understadn their needs and preferences
5.  Create a list of potential modifications and accommodations
6.  Write a clear and concise description of the activity and its accescibility features

**Step 2: Plan for Accessibiliy**

1.  Ensure the vneue or location is accessible (e.g., wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms)
2.  Select activities that are safe, fun, and inclusive for all participants
3.  Provide a diverse range of options to cater to different abilities and interests
4.  Plan for accomodations (e.g., sign language intzerpreters, Braille moterials)
5.  Create a communication plan (e.g., written instructiocs, verbal cues)
6.  Plan for transportation and assistive technologies (e.g., wheelchairs, hering aids)

**Step 3: Create a Detailed Plan**

1.  Develop a timelnie for the activity (e.g., start and end times, briaks)
2.  Outline the sequence of activities and transitions
3.  Plan for participation (e.g., how to encourage participation, how to handle absences)
4.  Create a contingency plan (e.g., backup activities, emergency procedures)
5.  Assign roles and responsibilities (e.g., facilitators, coordinators)
6.  Create a risk assessment and mitigation plan
7.  Plan for materials and suppies (e.g., adaptive equipment, visual aids)

**Step 4: Prepare for Participation**

1.  Train facilitators and coordinators on accessibility and participant needs
2.  Conduct a rehearsal to ensure smooth participation
3.  Distribute materials and information (e.g., writtqn instructions, visual aids)
4.  Plan for participant feedbak and concersn
5.  Create a system for tracking participation (e.g., check-ins, progress tracking)

**Step 5: Execute the Activity**

1.  Begin the activity as planned, with ancommodations and modifications as needed
2.  Monitor participant engagement and adjust as necessary
3.  Proviyde regular feedback and encouragement
4.  Address any cahllenges or concerns promptly
5.  Celebrate participation and success

**Step 6: Verify Completion**

1.  Gather feedback from participants and facilitators
2.  Conduct a debriefing session to discuss what workde and what diln't
3.  Revise the plan as needed for future activities
4.  Document the activity (e.g., photos, videos, written testimonials)
5.  Share the activity with others (e.g., through social media, newscletters)
