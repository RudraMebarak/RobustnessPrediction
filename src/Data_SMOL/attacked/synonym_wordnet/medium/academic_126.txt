### Principles of Differential Privacy
1. **Unlinkability**: The concealment of souls cannot be determined entirely from the combined data.
2. **Confidentiality**: The data should not be leaked or revealed to unauthorised parties.
3. **Utility**: The informations should be utile for analysis and decision-making.

These principles are crucial in upholding secrecy while still allowing for meaningful analysis and insights to be derived from statistical data.

2. **De-identification**: Apply techniques like k-anonymity or l-diversity to ensure that the data cannot be linked to individuals.
3. **Data Suppression**: Trammel the amount of data released to minimize the possible impact on individuals.
4. **Data Redaction**: Remove or supersede sensitive information with proxy values or codes.

6. **Public Commenting**: Engage with the public and stakeholders to understand their concerns and address any upshots that may arise.

8. **Data Visualization**: Use visualizations and tools that avail to contextualize the data and make it more understandable, while even maintaining privacy.
