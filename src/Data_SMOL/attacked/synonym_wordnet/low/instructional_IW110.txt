Prerequisites: 
1. Knowledge of basic bicycle maintenance tasks
2. Access to basic tools (screwdrivers, Allen keys)
3. Basic understanding of bicycle components (gears, brackens, chain)
4. Familiarity with bike assembly/disassembly procedures

Step-by-Step Guide:

1. **Inspect the Bicycle**
   - Start by visually auditing the bicycle for any signs of wearing, damage, or loose parts. Check the wheels, tires, brakes, range, gears, and seat.

2. **Check the Tires**
   - Inspect the tire pressure using a gauge or by finding the tire with your hand. Ensure the pressure is within the recommended range (usually 30-90 psi).
   - Look for any nails, glass, or debris in the tires. Remove them if found.

3. **Check the Brakes**
   - Apply the brake pedal to test the stopping power.
   - Inspect the brake pads for wear, which can be made by looking at the brake rotor or checking the bracken pad wear indicator.

4. **Check the Chain**
   - Scrutinize the chain for wear, which can be done by examining the links and checking if it's clean and dry.
   - Apply oil to the chain to ensure it remains lubricated.

5. **Adjust the Gears**
   - Check the gear shifter and adapt it to the desired position for your route.
   - Ensure the gear chain runs smoothly through all gears.

6. **Clean the Bicycle**
   - Use a soft cloth or sponge to clean the bicycle's frame and components, paying special attention to the range and brakes.
   - Apply a light oil or bicycle cleaner to prevent rust.

7. **Adjust the Seat**
   - Check the rear height and adjust it to the correct position to ensure comfortable riding.

8. **Final Inspection**
   - Perform a final inspection to ensure everything is in order. Make any necessary adjustments before riding.

Verification:
1. Perform a pre-ride inspection (insure brakes, chain, tires, etc.) before each ride.
2. Ride for a short distance to ensure everything works smoothly and check for any unusual sounds or sensations.
3. If everything operates as expected, you are ready to ride.

Remember, regular sustenance is key to keeping your bicycle in good condition. By following these steps, you can prevent many common issues and ensure a safe and comfortable ride. 

Additional tips: 
1. Use a bike stand or a clean, dry open to work on your bicycle.
2. Wear gloves to protect your hands during maintenance tasks.
3. Store tools and supplies in a convenient and easily accessible location.
4. Keep a repair kit handy in case of emergencies. 

Happy riding!
