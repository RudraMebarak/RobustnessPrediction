**Accessible Activity Planning Guide**

**Prerequisites:**

1.  Knowledge of the basics of accessibility and inclusive design
2.  Understanding of assistive technologies (AT)
3.  Familiarity with disability awareness and ethnic competency
4.  Basic understanding of group dynamics and communicating
5.  Experience with planning group activities
6.  Access to a diverse group of people (players)
7.  Time and budget for preparation

**Step 1: Define the Activity**

1.  Determine the coveted activity (e.g., sportswomen, art, music, or a mix of activities)
2.  Identify the skills and abilities needed for the activity
3.  Research the accessibility needs for the activity (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, sensory-friendly spaces)
4.  Consult with participants to understand their needs and preferences
5.  Create a list of potential modifications and accommodations
6.  Write a clear and concise description of the activity and its accessibility features

**Step 2: Plan for Accessibility**

1.  Ensure the venue or positioning is accessible (e.g., wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms)
2.  Select activities that are secure, fun, and inclusive for all participants
3.  Provide a diverse range of options to cater to unlike abilities and interests
4.  Plan for accommodations (e.g., sign speech interpreters, Braille materials)
5.  Create a communication plan (e.g., written instructions, verbal cues)
6.  Plan for transportation and assistive technologies (e.g., wheelchairs, hearing aids)

**Step 3: Make a Detailed Plan**

1.  Develop a timeline for the activity (e.g., start and end times, breaks)
2.  Outline the sequence of activities and passages
3.  Plan for participation (e.g., how to encourage participation, how to handle absences)
4.  Create a contingency plan (e.g., backup activities, emergency procedures)
5.  Assign roles and responsiblenesses (e.g., facilitators, coordinators)
6.  Create a risk assessment and mitigation plan
7.  Plan for materials and supplies (e.g., adaptative equipment, visual aids)

**Step 4: Prepare for Participation**

1.  Train facilitators and coordinators on accessibility and participant needs
2.  Conduct a rehearsal to ensure smooth participation
3.  Distribute materials and information (e.g., written instructions, visual aids)
4.  Plan for participant feedback and concerns
5.  Create a system for tracking participation (e.g., check-ins, progress tracking)

**Step 5: Fulfill the Activity**

1.  Begin the activity as projected, with accommodations and modifications as needed
2.  Monitor participant engagement and adjust as necessary
3.  Provide regular feedback and encouragement
4.  Address any challenges or concerns promptly
5.  Celebrate involution and succeeder

**Step 6: Verify Completion**

1.  Gather feedback from participants and facilitators
2.  Conduct a debriefing session to discuss what worked and what didn't
3.  Revise the plan as needed for next actions
4.  Document the activity (e.g., photos, videos, written testimonials)
5.  Share the action with others (e.g., through social media, newsletters)
