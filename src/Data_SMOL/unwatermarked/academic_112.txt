**Sampling Strategy for a Multi-Site Study of Digital Access Among Secondary-School Students in Rural Districts**

**Study Overview:**

This study aims to assess the digital access and literacy among secondary-school students in rural districts. The research will involve collecting data from multiple sites to understand the factors influencing digital access and literacy in these communities.

**Sampling Strategy:**

To ensure the representativeness and generalizability of the findings, the following sampling strategy will be employed:

1. **Site Selection:**
	* Identify rural districts with diverse socio-economic profiles, cultural backgrounds, and geographic locations.
	* Select districts with varying levels of access to digital resources, including internet connectivity, devices, and digital literacy programs.
	* Ensure a mix of urban and peri-urban areas within each district to capture regional variations.
2. **Sampling Frame:**
	* Secondary schools in the selected districts will be the primary sampling frame.
	* Include a range of school types, such as public, private, and charter schools.
	* Include schools with different student demographics, such as male and female students, students from diverse racial and ethnic backgrounds, and students with disabilities.
3. **Sampling Method:**
	* **Stratified Random Sampling:** Schools will be sampled using stratified random sampling to ensure representation of diverse school types, student demographics, and geographic locations.
	* **Cluster Sampling:** Within each selected school, a cluster of students (e.g., 10-20 students) will be sampled using cluster sampling to ensure representativeness of the student population.
4. **Sampling Size:**
	* Aim for a total of 50 schools across the 10 districts.
	* Within each school, sample 10-20 students using cluster sampling.
	* Total sample size: 500-1000 students across all sites.
5. **Sampling Procedure:**
	* Schools will be selected using a random number generator or a sampling software.
	* Within each selected school, students will be sampled using a random number generator or a sampling software.
	* Ensure that the sampling procedure is consistent across all sites to maintain data quality and comparability.
6. **Sampling Frame Validation:**
	* Validate the sampling frame by comparing the sampled schools and students with the national or state education data.
	* Ensure that the sampled schools and students are representative of the broader population.
7. **Sampling Error and Bias:**
	* Estimate the sampling error using statistical techniques, such as the Jackknife method or the Taylor Series method.
	* Identify potential biases and take steps to mitigate them, such as oversampling or undersampling certain groups.

**Data Collection:**

The data collection process will involve administering surveys, interviews, and focus groups to assess digital access and literacy among the sampled students.

**Data Analysis:**

The data will be analyzed using statistical techniques, such as descriptive statistics, regression analysis, and machine learning algorithms, to identify patterns and trends in digital access and literacy among the sampled students.

**Ethical Considerations:**

The study will comply with ethical standards, such as informed consent, confidentiality, and data protection, to ensure the well-being and rights of the participants.

**Timeline:**

The study will be conducted over a period of 12 months, with data collection and analysis occurring over 6 months.
