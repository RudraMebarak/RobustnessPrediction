Here's a story:

Once upon a time in a cozy little forest, there lived a tiny rabbit named Fluffy. Fluffy was the smallest rabbit in the forest, and people often made fun of her because of her small size. She felt sad and lonely, thinking that she couldn't do anything without help.

One day, while exploring the forest, Fluffy came across a deep hole filled with water. She wanted to cross the hole to reach her favorite food, juicy berries. But she was afraid of falling into the water and couldn't swim. She felt hopeless and gave up, thinking that she couldn't do it alone.

Just then, a wise old owl landed on a nearby branch and asked, "What's wrong, little rabbit?"

Fluffy hesitantly replied, "I can't cross the hole, I'm too small and scared."

The owl nodded sympathetically and said, "You don't have to do it alone, little rabbit. You can ask for help. It's okay to not be able to do everything by yourself."

Fluffy looked at the owl and felt a spark of hope. She realized that asking for help wasn't a weakness, but a strength. The owl helped her build a small bridge using twigs and leaves, and Fluffy crossed the hole safely.

From that day on, Fluffy learned to ask for help when she needed it, and she made many new friends in the forest. She realized that asking for help was not a sign of weakness, but a sign of strength and courage.

As Fluffy went to bed that night, she felt happy and content. She knew that she could ask for help whenever she needed it, and she was no longer lonely or afraid.

The end.
