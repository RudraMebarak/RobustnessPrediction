* Ear protection

**Multimeter Safety Precautions**  
*Never connect the multimeter's red lead to a battery terminal while it is still in a device.*  
*Always connect the multimeter to the battery terminals directly, outside the device.*  
*Avoid touching the battery terminals while the multimeter is connected.*  
*Turn off the device if it is still powered on when checking the battery.*

**Step-by-Step Guide**  
1. **Prepare the Multimeter**  
   *Turn the multimeter on.*  
   *Select the DC voltage setting.*  
   *Set the multimeter to the appropriate voltage range (e.g., 2V for a 9V battery).*  

2. **Check Battery Type**  
   *Identify the battery type (9V, AA, AAA, etc.).*  
   *Consult the multimeter's voltage range chart to select the correct setting.*  

3. **Safety Precautions**  
   *Put on safety glasses.*  
   *Put on gloves.*  
   *Put on insulated work gloves.*  
   *Put on ear protection.*  

4. **Prepare the Battery**  
   *Remove the battery from the device if necessary.*  
   *Identify the positive (+) and negative (-) terminals.*  

5. **Connect the Multimeter**  
   *Connect the red multimeter lead to the positive terminal.*  
   *Connect the black multimeter lead to the negative terminal.*  

6. **Read the Voltage**  
   *Check the multimeter's display for the voltage reading.*  
   *Compare the reading to the expected voltage for the battery type.*  

7. **Check for Short Circuits**  
   *If the reading is significantly different from the expected voltage, the battery may be shorted.*  
   *In this case, consider replacing the battery.*  

8. **Verify Completion**  
   *Turn off the multimeter.*  
   *Remove the multimeter leads from the battery terminals.*  
   *Put off the safety gear.*
