Here's a possible intro:

**Title: The Art of Surviving the Email-Meeting Hybrid**

---

**Step 1: Prepare for the Worst**

* Understand that the meeting is probably just an email in disguise.
* Wear comfortable shoes, because you'll be sitting for a long time.
* Bring a snack, because meetings are known for their endless coffee breaks.

**Step 2: Arrive Early (or Late)**

* Arrive early to show up and disappear quickly.
* Arrive late to show up and disappear quickly, but with a fake apology.

**Step 3: Set the Tone**

* Bring a funny meme to share.
* Bring a serious document to read aloud.

**Step 4: Listen Closely**

* Take notes, but don't worry too much about what's being said.
* Ask questions to stall and buy time.

**Step 5: Be the Hero**

* Volunteer to take on a task that you're not responsible for.
* Volunteer to take on a task that you are responsible for, but make it sound like a sacrifice.

**Step 6: End the Meeting**

* Propose an action item that you'll do, but don't actually do it.
* Propose an action item that you'll do, but make it sound like a big deal.

**Step 7: Leave a Lasting Impression**

* Send a follow-up email with a funny subject line.
* Send a follow-up email with a serious subject line.

---

Remember, the key to surviving a meeting that could have been an email is to be prepared, be flexible, and always have a backup plan.
