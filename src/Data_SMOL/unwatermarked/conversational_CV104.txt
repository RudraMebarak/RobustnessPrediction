Make sure to include the word "appetizer" at least twice and end with the host saying, "Let's enjoy the rest of the evening!" Use no comma.

Example:

Guest: Hi, how are you today?

Host: I'm great, thanks for coming. Would you like an appetizer?

Guest: Sure, I'm really hungry. What do you have?

Host: We have a variety of appetizers, including a spinach and cheese quiche and some marinated olives.

Guest: That sounds delicious! Can I try a bit of both?

Host: Of course, I'll bring you a plate. Let's enjoy the rest of the evening!
