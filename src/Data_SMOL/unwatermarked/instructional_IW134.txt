Title: A Beginner's Guide to Handling a Small Kitchen Grease Fire Safely

## Prerequisites
- Basic knowledge of fire safety
- Access to a fire extinguisher
- Proper ventilation in the kitchen
- Understanding of "small" fire size (no more than 2-3 inches in diameter)

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Assess the Situation
- **Check for safety first**: Ensure no one is in the immediate area.
- **Check the fire size**: Confirm it is a small fire (no more than 2-3 inches in diameter).

### Step 2: Extinguish the Fire
- **Use the right tool**: If you have a fire extinguisher, use it. Hold the extinguisher upright and aim the nozzle at the base of the fire.
- **If no extinguisher is available**: Use a pot lid or a cookie sheet to smother the flames. Slide the lid or sheet over the fire without touching the hot pot.
- **Do not use water**: Water can make a grease fire larger.

### Step 3: Evacuate and Secure
- **Evacuate the area**: Leave the kitchen and close the door to prevent the fire from spreading.
- **Call for help**: From a safe location, call emergency services or your local fire department.

### Step 4: Verify Completion
- **Check for embers**: After the fire is out, carefully check the area for any remaining hot embers.
- **Assess damage**: Look for any signs of damage to the pot or surrounding area.

### Additional Tips
- **Prevention is key**: Keep flammable materials away from the stove.
- **Regularly clean your stove**: This can help prevent grease fires.
- **Practice fire safety**: Familiarize yourself with your local fire department's emergency number and fire safety procedures.

By following these steps, you can safely handle a small kitchen grease fire. Remember, safety should always be your top priority. If you're unsure about any step, it's best to seek professional help. Stay safe in the kitchen!
